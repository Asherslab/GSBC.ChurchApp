# Calendar

Detail behind [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md) for the week, the month, an event's page and editing it, on the phone and in the admin panel. Read DESIGN.md first; its rules win. The week band and month layout are in its *Layout*, the date block's shape in *Shapes*.

## Calendar (signature)
One running diary from today: the ordinary stays quiet and only what's different speaks.
- **Week:** the week band, your calendars' chips, then a section per day ("Today", "Wednesday"). It ends on a quiet "Carry on past …" button or, at the end of what the phone holds, a hint saying that is everything.
- **Month grid:** day buttons at least 46px with up to three marks: pale for a regular event, accent for a special, marker for a change, an open ring for a date that isn't on. Today is filled primary; the chosen day takes an inset primary ring. A caption key lists the four marks.
- **Event head:** a navy band holding the date block beside the label line, the title and the when line ("8:30–11:30am · every second Saturday"). The label line is the event's own label or its calendar's name, never words the page invents. A date of a regular event ends its when line with "· this date only"; a regular event's own head has no date block and its when line is the pattern after the repeat icon ("Every Sunday · 10:30am–12pm"). Compact in admin panes.
- **Date block:** flat, spoken as the full date.
- **Event body:** an optional 16:9 picture edge to edge; Add to calendar (primary), Register when there is a link, Share (quiet); a change note on marker with "Changed" in orange ink; a date not on as a sand note; the facts (where opens Maps; contacts call or message), About, Other dates.
- **Edit where you read it:** Edit turns a one-off event's page into its editor in place; on a date of a regular event it opens that date. The top bar reads Cancel and "Editing"; the head stays as a live preview and nothing in it can be tapped (owner, 2026-11-08). Below: Title and Label, a one-off's Date and Until and times, the other fields, add-rows ("+ Add a contact"), and contacts as rows that open into a sand box of fields; then the foot bar.
- **Foot bar:** sticky, docked on the tab bar: Save (primary) beside Not on this date ("On again" once it's off), a small quiet Ask to publish when the editor must ask, and a small danger text link to delete. Never a choice between this date and every date (owner, 2026-11-08). In the admin panel, the same bar sticky to the panel's bottom.
- **Widening sentence:** when showing an event on another calendar would reach more people than its own does, an orange-ink caption with an icon says so under the field; when it needs an answer it becomes a marker box with its choices.
- **Regular event page:** a regular event is edited on its own page, never through a date. Its navy head (calendar name, title preview, pattern line) carries the date strip and its key. Under it *Every date* (Title and Label first, then the rest of an event's fields) and *How it runs*, side by side from 760px wide. How it runs is chevron rows with their values (Repeats, Breaks, Each date needs, Also shown on, Shown in the app), each opening a sheet with only that part. Delete the regular event is last, a small danger text link. Admin: Save every date and Ask to publish at the head's top right, after a caption saying whether it's shown; phone: the foot bar.
- **Date strip (signature):** the dates from today to a year ahead as one sideways-snapping row of date tiles on the navy head. A changed date is tinted marker with a dot; a date not on (a break's too) is an open tile; today is underlined in marker; the chosen date is ringed in marker. The key under it names the first changed date and what it is, the first that isn't on and why, and says "Choose a date to change only that date". Choosing a tile opens that date alone.
- **A date of a regular event:** the event head with its date block, then a sand band: "One of the Morning service, every Sunday." and "Edit the regular event ›". Its fields say they are for this date ("Title on this date", "Time on this date", "About this date"); a field that differs says "Usually 10:30am–12pm." with "Put it back". A date in a break says so in the not-on note, and that breaks are changed on the regular event. Foot bar: Save this date and Not on this date. Admin: it opens in the right pane.
- **A note on a date:** a feature can hang a link to one of its posts under a date's row (`DaySection` `NoteOf`), as a Life Group does in *Coming up*: a 44px link lined up with the title, the megaphone, the post's title and a chevron in accent, spoken as "…, a post in News". A post saying there's no class also makes that date not on, so the row and its note agree.
- **Kept by another feature:** a regular event another feature keeps in step (Life Groups' Sundays) shows no edit fields: a sand line led by a lock, "Life Groups keeps this in step with its own dates and times, so it's changed there, not here.", then "Change it in Life Groups ›".
- **Once or regularly:** New event first asks Once or Regularly, two choice rows each saying what it covers. A page on the phone; a menu under New event in the admin panel.
- **Regular events list:** rows with the repeat icon, the title, and how it repeats, its time and next date ("On today", "No more dates"), with a pill when needed: "1 date changed" in marker, quiet "Asked to publish" or "Not shown yet". An ended one is ink-3. Empty: an empty state with the repeat icon. Phone: a section of the calendar's page, for its editors only. Admin: the Regular events tab; a regular event opens across the middle and right panes under "‹ Regular events".
- **Calendar workbench (admin):** the sand column has All your calendars, Needs details and Asked to publish, each with an orange count only above zero; then the calendars in the church's order as a `ReorderList Quiet` that Calendar Managers reorder, the order phones show (owner, 2026-11-12). While All your calendars shows, each row has a switch to show or hide it there. A calendar someone can't change is plain text. "+ New calendar" opens a New calendar page in the middle with the same settings, Cancel and Add the calendar, and Breaks in a foot group.
- **A calendar's page (admin):** a calendar opens as a page in the middle, as a ministry's does (owner's pick, 2026-11-12): a `PaneHead` with a lead ("For everyone · Needs a publisher · Shown on new phones"; "Copied from its iCal address" or "Added from Browse calendars") and, for Calendar Managers, a ⋯ with Remove this calendar (asking once, saying what goes with it), then tabs **Events · Regular events · People · Settings**. Events: Agenda or Month, From another calendar, New event. People: *People on it* (`PeopleSheet Inline`, its lead saying what Editors and Publishers do) with Add someone. Settings: *About this calendar* (name, description, who it's for, shown on new phones, needs a publisher, copy from an iCal address) with its save bar, then *Calendar apps* with the subscribe link. A calendar copied from iCal has no Regular events tab.

**The Quiet Regular Rule.** A regular event is ink-2 at 400 with its time in ink-3 and no label; a special or one-off carries its label in accent and its time in primary; a change is marked in orange until opened; a date that isn't on keeps its place in ink-3, struck through, with the circle-off line under its title, never a suffix and never dropped.

**The One Date Rule.** A date of a regular event is only that date; the regular event is changed only on its own page. Never put a "this date" or "every date" switch in an editor; link the two with words and let a date say how it differs, with Put it back.
