# Feedback

Detail behind [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md) for Send feedback on the phone and in the admin panel, and the Feedback board. Read DESIGN.md first; its rules win. The `Composer`, `DropBox` and `Board` are in its *Pattern map*.

## Send feedback (phone)
Three taps on the church mark at the foot of More, within 1.2 seconds, open it (the mark's target doesn't select or flash): a pushed page under the solid top bar ("Send feedback", back to More), no masthead, written like a message (owner's pick on the decision page, 2026-11-12, `.impeccable/mocks/admin-nav-calendar/fb-composer.html`, after #55: the sheet took too much space with the keyboard up), with the pictures at the top and the text box and Send kept as they are (owner, 2026-11-12, #59).
- **Pictures, at the top:** with none chosen, one dashed tile: "Choose screenshots or videos" over "From your photo library. Up to 6." With some, the stage: the chosen one large in its own shape with a round Don't send at its top right, beside a column of thumbnails (the shown one ringed in marker) ending in a + tile, and "1 of 6 chosen" under it. This is the only way to add pictures; the composer has no paperclip.
- **Then** the lead ("Something broken, looking wrong, or an idea? Say what happened below."), then what goes with it, led by an info icon ("Sent with it: …", naming the screenshot pages when there are some). The upper part scrolls on its own and never squeezes what's in it: with the keyboard up it keeps its order, pictures then text, and scrolls under the composer (#72). The page fills the screen to the tab bar, and to the keyboard while it's up.
- **Composer:** the shared `Composer` docked at the foot: the message field, starting three lines tall and growing to about six, and a round primary Send (the `send` icon, as in Chats) that stays outlined while there's nothing to send. While the field has focus the keyboard's ^ v ✓ bar is hidden, so the composer sits just above the keys.
- **After:** sent, waiting, refused, over the day's limit and "Send it from the app" (not on a phone) are each an empty state; sent has a quiet Send another beside Done. Nothing typed or chosen is lost without asking.

## Send feedback (admin)
The sidebar foot's *Send feedback* opens a sheet over the current page, so the page it's about stays behind it. From 720px wide it sits near the top, 600px wide; narrower it is the usual bottom sheet. Title "Send feedback", lead "About the admin panel: something broken, looking wrong, or an idea?".
- **Taking pictures:** two capsules: *Capture this page* (camera icon), shown only where the browser can share a tab, and *Choose files* (paperclip). Capturing hides the sheet while the browser takes the picture; if the browser refuses or can't, a warning says so and points to pasting.
- **Drop zone:** under them, the shared `DropBox`: "Paste ⌘V or drop screenshots and videos" (the key in a key cap, "Ctrl V" on Windows, Linux and ChromeOS) over "Or click to choose. Up to 6". The whole frame is a file pick. Pasting anywhere while the sheet is open adds the files, and so does dropping anywhere on the sheet; while files are dragged over the sheet the frame turns deep aqua. It goes once six files are chosen.
- **Thumbnails** (`AttachmentThumb`, shared with the phone) wrap beside the drop zone; a video carries the play disc; a sending file shows an up arrow and its name; each has a round *Don't send*.
- **Then:** *Tell us about it* (multiline), the "Sent with it: …" line (the page, the last few opened, the browser, window size and scheme, the version and the account), and any warning. Cancel and Send in a `SaveBar`; when it can't send (files still sending, or nothing written or added) Send is disabled and the reason is the bar's own status line, above Send at the right.
- **After sending:** the sheet closes and the sidebar foot shows "Thanks, it's sent." for 5 seconds, led by a check and announced politely.

## Feedback board (admin)
`/feedback`: numbered tickets moving left to right through status columns, under the title band "Feedback" with a lead of what's waiting ("3 new · 1 in progress · 2 fixed, to check").
- **Where it came from:** above the board, filter chips "Everywhere", "The app" and "The admin panel".
- **Columns:** the shared board, `Compact` while a ticket is docked; Resolved narrower. Heads are fixed at two lines so every column's first card starts at the same height, with the count at the other end. An empty column says so ("Nothing new."); Resolved shows its latest 20, then "And N more. Find one by its number or words." A column under a drag is ringed in deep aqua.
- **Not doing:** folded by default into a strip ("Not doing · N", set vertically) that takes drops like a column; opened, its head folds it again.
- **Ticket card:** an optional cover (the first screenshot), "#12 · {name}", the title (wrapping, never cut off), then labels. A ticket from the admin panel leads with "Admin panel" in primary tint, then the browser; one from the app shows the app build and the phone's name. Build, phone and browser labels stay on one line, cut with an ellipsis (full text on hover); source and state labels wrap, since their words carry the meaning. Then the file count with a paperclip, "Pictures deleted" once they're gone, and on a closed ticket the day its files go ("Files go 18 Nov"). A Resolved or Not doing card dims its cover and its title. The open card is ringed in marker. Cards drag between columns; clicking docks the ticket, and ⌘-click or Ctrl-click opens `/feedback/{number}` in a new tab, as a hint under the board says.
- **Docked ticket:** "#12 · 2 hours ago", *Open as a page* and a close, over the title. Then the status as the stage pipeline (New, In progress, Fixed, to check, Resolved), and under it a quiet *Mark not doing* (a "Not doing" pill once it is) and a hint: open, that pictures, videos and phone data are deleted 7 days after it's Resolved or Not doing; closed, when it closed, the day its pictures and videos go, and that the ticket goes entirely 180 days after closing. Reopening a ticket keeps its files. Its pictures and videos scroll sideways, each with its kind and size under it.
- **Ticket page:** the same ticket as a page, on a title band (the title, "#12 · {name} · 2 hours ago", back to Feedback), with larger pictures. Its side, *Sent with it*, is a fact grid: From and Sent, then from the admin panel Where ("The admin panel"), Version, Browser, Window (size and scheme), Page and Before (the pages opened just before); from the app, App, Phone and Page. A ticket from the app then has *This phone* (how much it has sent, *Block this phone* as a danger text link opening its reason field) and the stored data as a small quiet download.
- **What's happened:** a timeline of steps and who; notes have their author and a delete. *Add a note* is a field over its own `SaveBar`.
- **Blocked phones:** the second tab: rows with a phone tile, the phone and how many it sent, the reason, who blocked it and when with a link to its last ticket, and a small quiet Unblock. With none, a shield empty state.
