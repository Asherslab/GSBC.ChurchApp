# Places and App styling

Detail behind [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md) for places in the app and the App styling page. Read DESIGN.md first; its rules win.

## App styling
The app as everyone sees it, in one sentence and on a phone, with drafts as the way a change travels. One layout for every state (the Still Frame Rule).
- **Title band:** "App styling" over one sentence with its facts in ink at 600 ("Everyone sees **the church's brand**, **11 tiles** on Home and **4 tabs**."), led by the event's draft when one is on for everyone. In a draft the title is the draft's name and the line becomes the save line: status, who sees it, *Back to what everyone sees*, and the undo note. Actions: Undo, Redo, Share and More (words hidden under 1480px), *Publish now* or *Ask to publish* as the one primary, then the Drafts menu, always last.
- **Drafts menu:** a quiet "Drafts" button with an orange count of open drafts, opening a `Popover`: *On for everyone, or on a date* (a pill says when), *Waiting to publish*, *Open* (a quiet *New draft*, or a caption on what starting one means), then *Published recently*, folded, an *Undone* pill on one undone.
- **Phone column:** the app's Home on the phone frame, never below half size: what everyone sees, or in a draft the Hub and Tab bar canvases. Clicking a part opens it in the panel.
- **Preview chips:** under the phone, one sentence of sand capsules: "Preview as [a visitor] in [light] mode, on an [iPhone 15 Pro] for [today]", then *Back to today* once another day is chosen. They change only the phone.
- **Panel:** outside a draft, *What everyone sees* (Colours, Home screen, Tab bar) and *Coming up*; in one, *What this draft changes* (each part with its count) or the chosen part under a back link. A group closes with a guidance caption ("Click a part of the phone, or one of these, to change it.").
- **Coming up:** the next six months' changes, a day column then the words; a click shows that day on the phone. Five show, the rest behind *Show all n*; with nothing ahead, one quiet caption.
- **Theme cards:** *The app's colours*, sand cards two to a row with swatches, name, note and state ("Everyone sees it now", or "Chosen in this draft" in orange ink). The chosen card has an inset marker ring; a theme made here carries *Change it*. *Make a theme* is an outlined card in deep aqua. A theme with dates says so under the cards, with *Change the dates*.
- **Theme swatches:** three overlapping discs (a made theme's masthead, accent and marker), one component shared with the app's Appearance page.

## Places in the app
Every place in the app, grouped by area, beside a phone showing the chosen one. Reached from the sidebar's The app area.
- **Directory:** search and filter chips (All and each area), then a group per area of hairline rows: icon on a sand tile for pages or an accent tile for things in them (events, files), name, where it is ("More → What's new") or its date, and who can open it. The chosen row takes the Tonal Selection. A group cut short ends in "Show all …" (44px target). Nothing matching is an empty state. The same directory is the place picker, in a wide sheet.
- **Phone column:** under the phone, the name, where it is and who can open it; then the place's own change action as a small primary ("Change it in a draft"), the copy button and small quiet actions ("Announce it"); then the link (tabular, breaking anywhere) and one note on what it does on a phone. A place that can't be shown is an empty state.
- **The phone:** a feature can register a live view of its place (Home draws through HomeScreen); any other place is the phone frame with the masthead carrying the place's name and where it is.
