# Ministries and Life Groups

Detail behind [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md) for a ministry's page, leading it, its roster, the Ministries desk and Life Groups. Read DESIGN.md first; its rules win. Saving, deleting, menus and the unsaved-changes guard follow its *Pattern map*; every Ministries and Life Groups form is behind the guard.

## Ministry page (bulletin boxes)
A ministry (`/ministries/{id}/{area}`) reads like the bulletin's shaded boxes: in each area, what you may see is boxed by who it's for.
- **Masthead:** name, its one line as the note, then one action: a check pill for your place ("You lead it"); else *Join*, or *Ask to join* when every open choice needs a leader's yes; *Sign in to join* when signed out; or a clock pill, "Asked · waiting for a leader". A hidden ministry (or one under a hidden one) adds an eye-off pill, "Hidden". Joining confirms with a notice.
- **Area tabs:** a sticky link-mode tab strip once there is more than one area: About, its parts (News, Calendar, Files, Roster, Chats, each only while used) in *Its tabs* order, then People (asks counted) and Settings for those allowed. Each area has its own address. The strip scrolls sideways only, no scrollbar, keeping the chosen tab in view. A Life Group's Sundays live in its Calendar tab.
- **Calendar area:** *Coming up*, the next dozen events of its calendars (and those under it, when shown) as agenda rows, each naming its ministry when several are listed; then its calendars in their boxes, and *From the ministries under it* as quiet rows.
- **Audience boxes (`MinistryBox`):** always team, families, parents, everyone, each headed with who it's for. Team: primary. Families: accent. Parents: marker (marker-ink head). Everyone: sand (ink-2 head).
- **Items in a box:** title, body (65ch, line breaks kept), and a meta line: "Important" in marker-ink, "From …" for a post from the ministry above or one under it, age ("· edited"), and in accent "In church news", "Asked for church news" or "Kept in this ministry's news". On the phone a post links to its page, clamped to three lines; in the admin panel a leader's own post opens the post sheet. Files and calendars are rows, with a pencil for those who may change them.
- **Tools:** a primary with a plus above the boxes ("New post", "Add a link", "Add a calendar") for those who may add. An empty area says what goes there.
- **About:** the text; when, where and who it's for; the leaders; *Waiting for a leader* (with *Take back*); its teams or groups; and *Your place*, rows that act at once ("Leave the team", "Stop coming along").
- **Join sheet:** "Join …", led by "Who's it for?" when there are several choices, each a radio card; a closed choice is in ink-3 with its reason. The first open one starts chosen; an ask adds an optional note for the leaders; the primary names the result.
- **Post sheet:** in the admin panel, posting, changing a post and *Tell the group about a Sunday* open a writing page, `/ministries/{id}/news/write` or `/ministries/{id}/news/{post}/change` (`PostSheet OnAPage` in `WritingDesk`; owner, 2026-11-13, #83), led "In …. A title and the post. Pictures, video, files and more are under Insert.", with *Who it's for*, *Telling people* and *Preview on a phone*. The phone keeps a sheet ("New post" or "Change the post", led "In …"): the `PostEditor` with "Who it's for and telling them" inline: *Who it's for*, a sand note saying exactly who reads it, *Important* (notifies everyone it's for, at once) and, for everyone, *Ask for church news*. The primary names the audience ("Post for parents"), or "Save" for a saved post. In a hidden ministry the everyone choice reads "Everyone who sees it" (file and calendar sheets too), the primary "Post for everyone who sees it", the note says only its own people, the leaders above it and staff see it, and *Ask for church news* isn't offered.
- **A Life Group's Sundays:** no list of their own: Coming up shows each Sunday. *Tell the group about a Sunday* opens the post sheet with *Which Sunday* and *No class this Sunday*; the post shows under that Sunday, struck through when there's no class.

## Leading a ministry
Leaders on the phone, staff in the admin panel, through the same areas.
- **People tab:** *Team | Attendees* (when it has attendees), counted. Asks first ("Asking to join (2)", "Asking to come along"), the note in quotes, *Not now* and *Yes, add them*. Then "Find someone".
- **On the phone:** rows giving role (when not plain), age and parents' first names, or "No parent linked" in marker-ink; ending in "Add to the team" or "Add an attendee". A row opens the **person sheet**: age, role and "no sign-in of their own"; parents ("Link as their parent"); changes that act at once, accent plus to give ("Make a leader", "Add to the team as well"), quiet minus to take ("Take off the team"); a hint that only the leaders and the church office see this. Someone leading from the ministry above gets a line saying where to change that.
- **In the admin panel:** the same as a data table (Name, Role, Age, Parents or a Marker "No parent linked" pill) in a split view; add is a quiet button beside the search.
- **Settings** (`Section Grouped`, same on both sides), in order. *The details* ("What people see about it, how they join, and where it shows."): Name, In a line, About, When and where it meets, Who it's for; *Joining* (`JoiningFields`): *Joining the team* (Open, Ask, Invite only) and *Coming along* (adds No attendees), each option saying what happens to the person ("People ask to join the team, and a leader says yes"); *Life Groups* (`LifeGroupsFields`): *Show in Life Groups* and, while on, *When it shows* (All year, or an unfinished set's dates); *Hidden from everyone else* (`HiddenFields`, #73, "Only its own people and staff see it"). *Save the details* ("Give it a name to save it." without one). *Its tabs*, *Chats* and *From the ministries under it* change at once, saying "These change straight away."
- **Hidden switch states:** a switch that can't change says why: under a hidden ministry, locked on ("Hidden, because Music is. Only its own people and staff see it."); a Life Group, off ("A Life Group is listed for everyone, so it can't be hidden"); while hidden, *Show in Life Groups* is off ("A hidden ministry isn't listed in Life Groups, which everyone sees"), a sand `ministry-who` note names exactly who sees it, and *Joining* hints that only people who can already see it can join or ask. The switch's placement (the details, its own group, or the ⋯ menu) awaits the owner's pick (`.impeccable/mocks/hidden-ministries/`); it sits in *The details* until then.
- **Its tabs:** a `ReorderList` with preview strip ("About is always first"), its lead naming the menu ("or use its More menu"); each tab a toggle. An off tab is hidden from everyone, leaders too, nothing deleted. **Chats:** *Who can start a chat*, Leaders or The team too. **From the ministries under it** (parts that can gather): *Show news from the ministries under it*, *Show the calendars of the ministries under it*. Parts come from `MinistryParts.All`; a new part is one entry there.
- **Where it sits:** where it is now ("Under Music. Its people, posts, files and calendars move with it."), then *Sits under* (`SitsUnderPicker`), excluding itself and what's under it. A new place shows a notice in sentences: where it goes and with how many under it, which leaders will and won't lead it, whose combined news or calendar it joins or leaves, whether it becomes hidden ("It will be hidden, as Music is: only its own people and staff will see it.") or stops ("It won't be hidden any more: everyone will see it."), and that its own people, posts, files and calendars stay; *Move …* beside *Keep it where it is*.
- **Ministries under it** (a ministry under another is a ministry, never a team): rows, then *Add a ministry*.
- **Archive and Delete:** in the ministry's ⋯ menu, never on Settings. *Archive …* ("Puts it away without deleting anything. Staff can restore it.") says what leaves every list and what is kept, then returns to the ministry above. *Delete …* ("Shows what goes with it first") lists what goes and where the ministries under it move.
- **The directory** (*All ministries*): the tree, three levels, each indented 24px (`ListRow` `Indent`). A Life Group's line starts "Life Group"; a hidden one's starts "Hidden", with no join line.
- **A new ministry** (one sheet everywhere): *Sits under* first, each option with its path ("Kids Ministries › Impact Kids"), preselecting where it was opened, offering "Nothing: it's a top-level ministry" only to those who may, and only places the person may add under; then Name, In a line, When and where it meets, Life Groups (opened from Life Groups: on, *Coming along* Open, worded "Life Group") and joining. *Add the ministry*; "Give it a name to add it." without one.

## Roster (a ministry's tab)
What a volunteer opens it for comes first (`RosterArea`, Rosters.Client, via `MinistryPartView`).
- **You're on:** a row per date you or your child are on (position, date, time; the event or "For Tom · …"), an answer pill once answered ("Can do it"), and *I can do it* (*Yes, for Tom*), *Can't do it* (optional reason inline), *Ask for a swap* or *Call off the swap*. Who answered ("Answered by Sarah, for Tom") and the swap's state are captions.
- **Can you take one of these?** and, for leaders, **Waiting for a leader**: warm rows (*Take it*, *Tom takes it*; *Not now*, *Yes*).
- **The roster:** leaders' tools (*Add a date*, *Positions*, *How it works*), then *By date | Grid*. By date: a row per date with its positions on one line ("Lights: nobody yet"), a Marker "2 gaps" pill for leaders, opening the **date sheet**: each position with "1 of 2", its people with answer and flags, taking off in each row's ⋯, and *Put someone on* saying what's open ("1 place open · someone trained for Ben"). A trainee is indented under the position: aqua with someone trained ("Training, with Jo"), warm with the flag "Training: needs someone trained on with them" when alone, their place still open. Taking the date off is in the heading's ⋯. Picking lists the team unflagged first, flags as the line.
- **Grid:** dates across (four on the phone, eight at a desk), positions down in a sticky column with what they need ("1 person · needs a blue card · trained, or training with someone who is"). Cells: aqua answered, sand waiting, primary when answers are off, danger struck through for "can't"; flags in marker-ink; a trainee with someone trained shows "Training" in aqua, after the trained person; a gap is a dashed cell with *Put on*. Scrolls sideways, no scrollbar.
- **Days away** on the phone are their own pages (#68): the Roster tab ends with *Your days away* ("Next away …" or "None coming up") and, for leaders, *The team's days away* ("2 people away in the next four weeks" or "Nobody away in the next four weeks"), opening `/account/away` (under More) or `/ministries/{id}/away`; Account's *Rosters* group has *Your days away* too. Each page is a masthead with a note on who sees them, then `DaysAway Paged`: whose, then `Month | List` (List: "No days away coming up for you.", the rules, one-off days soonest first). A non-leader on the team's page sees "Only the team's leaders see this"; before the roster syncs, "The roster isn't on your phone yet". The admin panel keeps the editor inline.
- **Days away editor** (`DaysAway`, also a person's Days away tab and the roster in the admin panel): whose ("Me | Tom" up to three, a select beyond), repeating rules, the `MonthDays` month with its key ("Shaded: away. Top half: mornings; bottom half: evenings. Struck through: not away that time."), one-off days, and *Add days away*. A day opens what's there: a one-off its editor, a free day a new one, a rule's day a sheet titled with the date ("Away, from “Sunday mornings”." or "Not away this time, from …") with *I'm not away this day* (*Alex isn't away this day*; *Away this day after all* when skipped) over *Change the rule*. Leaders see **The team's days away** for everyone on the team.
- **Days away sheet** (`AwaySheet`): "Days away", or the rule's name. Who's away (when several), *Once | Every week* (weekday chips in one row of seven, Starting, Until, "Until further notice" when empty; or First day and Last day), *All day | Mornings | Evenings | Times*, *Not away on* as chips of skipped dates (choose one to undo), a note for the leaders. Save reads *Add the days* when new.
- **How the roster works** (`RosterSettingsSheet`), in order: *Answers* (asking, the nudge, the reminder); *When someone asks the team for a swap* (`Segmented Rows`: Anyone on the team can take it, Anyone can offer and a leader says yes, Nobody takes it); *Asking one person*; a sentence following the choices ("So when Alex can't do Sun 15 Nov, they ask the team, and stay on until it's taken. Leaders hear."); *What the team sees*; *Calendars it rosters from*.
- **Flags never block:** words in marker-ink beside a name; never a disabled button.
- **Hub tile** *Your roster*: "You're on Sunday" (or "Tom is on Sunday"), the position and event, and "Say whether you can do it" while an answer waits (unread).

## Ministries desk (admin)
`/ministries`: a title band ("12 ministries · 3 asks waiting for an answer") with *New ministry*, then the tree (240 to 300px) beside the chosen ministry; under 900px they stack and the tree stops being sticky.
- **Tree:** sticky; "Find a ministry" (results flat), then a button per ministry (at least 40px), indented per level. A hidden one has the eye-off after its name, titled and spoken "Hidden from everyone else". Asks waiting show in the orange count capsule, spoken "3 asking to join". The chosen one takes Selected Fill and is the current page.
- **The ministry:** a `PaneHead`: name, where it sits ("Under Kids › Impact Kids") and its line, led by "Hidden from everyone else" when hidden; then the tab strip (not sticky). Nothing chosen: "Choose a ministry".

## Life Groups
Every ministry with *Show in Life Groups* on, wherever it sits, in dated sets or all year; staff plan sets in the admin panel. People never see the word for a set, only its dates.
- **In the directory:** a *Life Groups* section, one row "Sunday classes": "Yours: …" or "Choose a class for you or your children".
- **The page:** once the next set shows, a card ("New groups from 1 February", "6 groups on 6 Sundays, 9am to 9:45am. Choose one for you, and for each of your children.") with *Choose a group*. *Your groups* first when you or your children are in any; then *Now on* and *All year*, each group with where it meets, your place or its line, and "until …" for a dated set. Nothing on: a book empty state.
- **Choose a group:** a paged sheet, a page per group: name, where it meets, its Sundays and time, *More about it*, and *Join …* opening the join sheet (signed out, Account).
- **The desk (`/life-groups`):** title band with *Plan the next groups* (planners) and *Add a Life Group*; a row saying where Life Groups show ("Shown in Sunday services" or "Not shown in a calendar"), opening the calendar sheet for planners; an "All year" strip of the all-year groups and *Add an all-year group*. A group not on a set's Sundays turns marker and adds "not on …". *Archived groups (N)* opens the archived sheet.
- **Board by stage:** columns *Being planned*, *Shown in the app*, *Now on*, *Finished in 2026*, counted; two under 1180px, one under 640px. A set's card: its dates (for planners, opening *Sundays and time*), its facts ("6 Sundays · 9am to 9:45am · shows in the app from …", or "Sunday 3 of 6"), its groups as links ("· needs where it meets" in marker-ink without a place; an all-year group sitting out in ink-2 with a circle-off icon), what it still needs in marker-ink ("No groups yet, and people can already see these Sundays."), and one action, *Add a group*. Only the next set to fill takes the marker outline; the set now on an accent ring. An empty column says what comes there and when.
- **Finished:** a year choice once there are several; fold lines of dates and group count, the latest three then "N more". An opened set offers *Archive their groups* (or *Delete these Sundays* while empty), each asking first.
- **Archived sheet** (`ArchivedSheet`, also from *Archived* in the Ministries desk's title band): "Archived Life Groups" or "Archived ministries", its lead saying restoring brings one back as it was; each row the name, where it sat and when it was archived, with *Restore*. Empty: "Nothing archived".
- **Planning sheet** (*Plan the next groups* or *Sundays and time*): the chosen Sundays as chips that take a Sunday off, From and To with *Add the Sundays in these dates*, Starts and Ends, and *Shows in the app from*. Its actions add *An all-year group isn't on these Sundays* and, while a set has no groups and isn't on, *Delete these Sundays*.
