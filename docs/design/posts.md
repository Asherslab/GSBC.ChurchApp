# News, posts and writing

Detail behind [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md) for the news page, a post's page, writing a post, formatted text, the church news desk, the writing page, earlier announcements and the weekly digest. Read DESIGN.md first; its rules win.

## News page (phone)
`/news`, its own tab: masthead "News", note "From the church and the ministries you're in", then `NewsList` (owner, 2026-11-13; order in DESIGN.md, *Pattern map*).
- **Whose news:** a sticky link-mode tab strip, *The church* (`/news`) and *Your ministries* (`/news/yours`, every ministry you're in), shown only to someone in a ministry.
- **Urgent today:** one marker band each above everything, no hairline: a marker News tile, the title, "Today · " and the text's start in marker-ink, a marker-ink chevron.
- **The lead:** the newest other post: its first picture at 16:9 (sand-strong while loading), the title, two lines of ink-2 text, the meta line. No picture, no frame.
- **Rows:** the rest, newest first: title, two lines of text (cut at 160 characters), the meta line, the first picture as a square at the right.
- **Meta line:** "From …" in accent with the Ministries icon for a ministry's post, any state in marker-ink, how long ago ("· edited"). Every story is a link.
- **Staff tools** (*The church*, for church news writers): a block primary "New post"; while anything waits, a warm row "3 posts to look at" ("Drafts, posts waiting to be published and ministries' asks") to *To look at* (`/news/desk`), with sections *Ministries asking*, *Waiting to be published*, *Scheduled*, *Being written* (a people icon for an ask, a clock for scheduled, a pencil otherwise).
- **Empty:** "No news yet": "News for the whole church shows here when it's posted." (*The church*), "News from the ministries you're part of shows here." (*Your ministries*).

## A post's page
`PostView`: church news (`/news/{id}`), a ministry's post, both *Preview on a phone* panes and the desk's record pane. Every post is set as a bulletin item (owner, 2026-11-14, #88; `.impeccable/mocks/post-page/a-bulletin-item.html`). Phone post pages use `TopBarStyle.SolidTitleOnScroll`, so the post's title is the page heading.
- **Head:** a 3px navy (primary) rule opens the post; under it the date block, beside it the title and who.
- **Date block:** sand: the day word in accent ("Today", "Yesterday", "Thu", or the year for an earlier year), the tabular day number in ink, the month in ink-2. Screen readers hear the long date, which is never repeated under the title.
- **Who and states:** under the title, who it's from in accent with the Ministries icon (linking to that ministry's News tab), who wrote it, "Edited". Pills: a church post's state in marker ("Scheduled", "Being written"); a ministry post's *Important* in marker, its church news line in accent, its audience quiet.
- **The strip:** only when a post opens with two or more pictures or videos (YouTube links count; `PostOpening`, Platform.Contracts): `MediaStrip` across the gutters, 4:3 frames on black swiped one at a time, a "2 of 5" pill above dots (shown dot in accent). A picture opens the picture viewer. The text skips what the strip shows; a single opening item and every later picture show in place.
- **Body:** up to 65ch; the first paragraph (of the post or its first card) is the lead, at title size. In the text: pictures with ink-2 captions; galleries in two 4:3 columns (with three, the first spans at 16:9); video on black under its own picture (a YouTube or Vimeo thumbnail; an upload's first frame); audio and files on sand, a file as a 56px link row with an accent tile, name over size; code on sand in `--font-code`; tables in a hairline box, head row on sand, cells of up to 24 characters on one line and longer ones at least 12em wide, a table too wide for the phone scrolling sideways inside its box with a shadow at the edge it continues past; highlights on `--highlight`; footnotes numbered in accent and gathered at the end over a hairline.
- **Cards:** each `===` part starts under the same navy rule. Never a sand bar: sand means the post is over.
- **App cards:** `AppCardFrame` in one of three looks, stored as `look=` (owner, 2026-11-14; `.impeccable/mocks/app-embeds/`). The title links to the thing; under it a meta line in the kind's tone and icon ("Event", "Event · regularly", "Sermon · Series, part 2", "Ministry · You lead it"), then ink-2 detail lines with ink-3 icons. Never a kicker above the title.
  - *Side rule* (default, writes nothing): a tone rule at the left, a square picture or tile at the right, actions as `Link` words.
  - *Timetable line* (`look=line`): a date block, picture or tile at the left between hairlines; small buttons, the main one primary, the rest tinted.
  - *Box* (`look=box`): sand, a 16:7 cover (16:9 for a sermon, never cropped), the title at lead size, the main button widened.
  - *Tones:* events and ministries in accent on live; sermons in primary; Life Groups marker-ink on marker. No picture: a solid tile in the kind's colour with its icon (repeat icon for a regular event, the ministry's own icon), never a ministry's own colour.
  - *States:* changed, "Changed: …" in marker-ink; cancelled, title struck, "Cancelled: …" in danger, tile or date in danger, cover greyscale; over, a quiet sand line ("An event on … that's over now."); gone or not visible, nothing for readers. A sermon shows progress and time left, then "Heard"; it opens the sermon, never plays in place.
  - *Editor only:* when the post may reach more people than can see the thing, an eye-off note "Only people who can see this event will see its card." (it still saves; owner, 2026-11-14); a gone card is a sand line "This event isn't in the app any more, or you can't see it. Take it out or choose another."
- **Its link:** a block button under the text, the places icon in the app or external-link for the web, named by the post ("Open it" when unnamed).
- **The foot:** a sand foot ends every post: "Posted **today** by **Church office** for everyone." (the day as the date block says it, or "on" the long date; the writer or who it's from; the audience in lower case). Across the gutters with a hairline above in a page section; a rounded box in the record pane. With `FillsPage` (phone post pages, both previews) it fills to the screen's bottom.
- **Actions:** the top bar's ⋯, "More for this post": *Change the post*, a rule, red *Delete the post…* (confirmed with Keep it). A ministry's post shared into church news offers there only *Take it off church news…*. On a ministry's post, publishers also get *Put it in church news…* (or *Take it off…*) with the megaphone, confirmed on a sheet with *Send a notification* and Not now.
- **An ask:** publishers see "… asks for this to go in church news" under the post, *Send a notification* (on), block *Put it in church news* and quiet *Keep it in …*.
- **Gone:** "This post isn't here any more", pointing to News (or the ministry's News tab).

## Writing a post
`PostEditor` (Platform.Ui): `/news/write`, a ministry's post sheet on the phone, and the admin writing pages inside `WritingDesk` (owner, 2026-11-13, #83 to #85; 2026-11-14, #86 to #88). A title and one editor, no blocks or Add row. Files wait and upload with the save, on the phone and in the admin panel.
- **Title:** "Give it a title"; on a writing page it is the page's headline, unfilled, over a hairline.
- **The post:** one `RichTextField` with `Headings` and `RichTextMedia.Posts`, labelled "The post", placeholder "Write the post. Formatting is in the bar; pictures, video, files and more are under Insert."; `===` splits cards.
- **Settings row** (phone): one sand row "Everyone · Notification on · Now" opening a sheet with Done; inside a sheet they sit inline under "Who it's for and telling them"; on a writing page, the settings column.
- **Church news settings:** *Who it's for*, *Send a notification* with *What it says*, *Its line*, *Urgent* (once sent, a line saying when), *A link at the end (optional)* with *The link's words*, and for publishers *When* (Now or Later, *Publish it at*).
- **Saving:** `SaveBar` with Cancel at the left, its primary "Post for everyone", "Post it", "Schedule it", "Ask for it to be published" (non-publishers) or "Save" once published; quiet *Send to my phone* after Cancel on a saved draft; no title: "Give it a title to post it."

## Formatted text
`RichTextField` (Platform.Ui Content): live Markdown on CodeMirror 6, loaded only when an editor opens, so staff who don't know Markdown see formatting as they type (owner, 2026-11-13); it stores Markdown and is the one editor for formatted text. Formatting in one row of quiet icons, everything that goes *into* the text behind one navy Insert (owner, 2026-11-14, #86); never chips under the editor, block cards with handles or a bar of thirty icons. `RichTextEditor` (the Markdown box with a preview) is what older places use until they move (owner's picks on the decision page, 2026-11-13 and 2026-11-14).
- **The bar:** a sticky toolbar "Formatting", in order: with `Headings`, a *Text style* select (Text, Heading 1 to 3, the current one ticked); Bold, Italic, Strikethrough, Highlight; Bulleted list, Numbered list, Quote, Link, Code; ⋯ *More formatting* (Superscript "x²", Subscript "H₂O"); groups split by hairline dividers, and a navy primary **Insert** pushed to the right, shown only where the place allows something. Tools are quiet icon `Button`s with `Pressed`, tooltips giving shortcuts (⌘B, ⌘I, ⌘⇧X, ⌘⇧H, ⌘⇧8, ⌘⇧7, ⌘⇧9, ⌘K, ⌘E; ⌘⌥0 to 3 for styles).
- **Insert:** the shared `Menu` with group titles: *Pictures and media* (Picture "From the library or upload", Video "Uploaded", Video link "YouTube, Vimeo", File, each through Media's chooser with Upload); *Layout* (Split into cards, Line across, Table, Code block); *In the text* (Footnote, Emoji); *From the app* (Event, Sermon, Ministry, Life Group). What it offers is the place's `RichTextMedia`: `Posts` everything; `Layout` the layout and in-the-text choices only; none, formatting only and no Insert. Audio is never offered (existing audio still plays).
- **The text:** a sand field, at least 96px, deep-aqua caret and selection. Headings `#` biggest (owner: "more # makes it smaller"). Marks (`**`, `#`, `>`, `==`) show faintly only on the cursor's line; code fences fold off it. A line across is a hairline; a card split a dashed double rule round "Next card".
- **Media in place:** each picture, gallery, video, video link or file shows where it sits; under it one quiet caption line, "Add a caption", underlined in aqua while typing (Enter or Escape returns to the text), and the library path in `--font-code` (`~/Main Photos/church-ministry.jpg`); `media:<id>` never shows (owner: "handle that invisibly"). Its pill: *Drag to move it*, *Add a picture beside it* (pictures), *Replace it*, *Take it out*. Chosen, it is ringed in deep aqua.
- **Moving:** every block drags by its pill's handle, with a mouse or a finger, onto an aqua line between blocks; the block dims while it moves, Escape cancels, and Alt ↑ ↓ on the handle moves it by keyboard. Each picture in a gallery has a ⋯ at its bottom right (on hover at a desk, always on the phone; ringed in the field edge on a picture still uploading, where dark mode would hide it) with *Move earlier*, *Move later*, *Replace* and *Take this picture out*, a sheet on the phone (owner's pick on the decision page, 2026-11-14, #92, #93).
- **From the app:** each kind only where a feature registers its card and with `RichTextMedia.AppCards` (in `Posts`). `AppCardPicker`, a sheet "Put in a card for an event": "Type part of its name" over rows of only what the writer can see ("Only events you can see are listed."). A regular event asks "It's a regular event. Which should the card show?": *Just this one date*, *The next date, rolling on*, *No particular date*, with *Back to the list* and *Put it in*. On the phone the search doesn't take focus, so the keyboard stays down.
- **Cards in place:** drawn in their look, inert, the pill above at the right, never over the title: *Drag to move it*, *Change how it looks* (the three looks as `Segmented Rows`, then Done), *Replace it*, *Take it out*. A Box has a hairline ring to stand off the field.
- **Tables in place:** drawn as on the post page, pill above (drag, *Change the table*, *Take it out*). Pressing it opens a panel under the bar: a grid of cells 8px in from its scroller, × to take out each row or column (a table keeps a head and one row), *Add a row*, *Add a column*, Cancel and *Done*. Insert → Table opens it with *Put it in*.
- **A link:** a panel under the bar, *Link to* (https, an app page, mailto or tel, else a problem line) with Cancel and *Add the link*. Video link and Emoji open panels the same way.
- **Pasting:** from Word or Google Docs keeps bold, italic, lists and links, the rest plain; `- `, `# `, `> ` start a list, heading or quote.
- **Phone:** the bar is one row that slides sideways, Insert first and pinned at its left; Insert opens a sheet of tiles under its group names, the style and ⋯ open sheets of rows. While the keyboard is up the bar docks on it, full width, with 44px-wide buttons, and the page gains room so the text clears it.
- **Not moved yet:** events' and ministries' *About* and the app's formatted settings keep `RichTextEditor` until the owner decides; moving is a component swap.
- **In chat bubbles:** `RichTextScope.Chat` in `.chat-text`; in your own bubbles links, code and highlight take on-primary. The composer stays plain (*default, owner to review*: a bar didn't fit beside attach and send).

## Church news desk (admin)
`/news` under This week: `TitleBand` "Church news" with quiet *Earlier announcements* (with a clock, for those who may see them) and primary *New post*, opening `/news/write`. The desk is the list; posts are written on their own page (owner, 2026-11-13, #83).
- **Rail:** one `FilterChips Rail` group with counts: *Ministries asking*, *Being written*, *Scheduled*, *Published*, *Kept in ministries*; it opens on asks if any, then being written, then published. On a laptop it folds into *Show*.
- **List:** title over a line ("Kids church · asked by …", "By …", "Publishes tomorrow 9am", or "From …" and the date), a pill (*Asking*, *Waiting to be published*, *Urgent* in marker; *From a ministry* in accent) and a thumbnail; the chosen row is current. Empty: "Posts show here as they're written, asked for, scheduled and published."
- **Record pane:** `PaneHead`, a state line ("Published 13 November, with a notification."), ⋯: *Back to writing* (scheduled or waiting), a rule, *Delete the post…* or, for a ministry's post, *Take it off church news…*. A post you may change opens on `/news/{id}/change`; the pane shows the post's page (up to 720px) for asks, shared posts and what you can't change.
- **An ask:** a grouped section "… asks for this to go in church news" with *Send a notification*, its foot quiet *Keep it in …* at the left and *Put it in church news* at the right.
- **New post:** "New post", Back to *Church news*, no ⋯ until saved; `?opens=` starts one with that link.

## Writing on a page (admin)
`WritingDesk` (Platform.Ui): writing something long on its own page, not the record pane: `/news/write`, `/news/{id}/change`, `/ministries/{id}/news/write`, `…/{post}/change` (owner, 2026-11-13, #83: "allow collapsing settings"). Slots: the writing, `Preview`, `Settings` (`SettingsTitle`, `SettingsSummary`), `Foot`. Layout: DESIGN.md, *Pattern map*.
- **Above it:** `TitleBand` with Back to where you came from, "New post" or "Change the post", and its ⋯ once the item exists; the desk then runs edge to edge under a hairline.
- **The page:** tabs *Write* and *Preview on a phone* (owner: "tab it, like is often for markdown editors"), the preview a centred phone frame.
- **Settings column:** sticky and scrolling on its own, headed "Who it's for and telling them" with a quiet fold button (`AppIcons.HideSidePane`, "Hide these to give the writing more room"); settings in grouped `Section` cards. Folded, only `AppIcons.ShowSidePane` remains and the accent summary ("Everyone · Notification on · Now") sits at the tabs' right end; pressing it unfolds.
- **Save bar:** sticky at the window's foot over a hairline, under the settings column, or across the page when folded.
- **1228px and under:** one column: the page, the settings under it (up to 760px, always open, no fold or summary), the save bar across. At 760px and under the sides become the gutter.

## Earlier announcements (admin)
`/announcements`, read only, from the church news desk (church news replaced announcements; owner, 2026-11-13). `TitleBand` "Earlier announcements" ("What the office sent before church news …") with a *Church news* button. A table, newest first: announcement, when, phones reached and read by (right-aligned). A pane shows its title, a state line ("Sent … to about 42 phones; 30 people have read it.", recalled, or scheduled), the message up to 65ch with its line breaks, and ⋯: *Don't send it* if scheduled, then red *Recall…*, confirmed on a sheet ("It stays here as a record"). Empty: "Nothing was sent before church news."

## Weekly digest desk
`/digest` in the split view, after the approved mock. Above the list: From, How long (One week, Two weeks, Month, Custom, segmented) and, for Custom, To; the range under them in primary, tabular; a "Calendars" label over quiet toggle chips. Item rows: grip, tick, 16:9 thumbnail, body (label, title and pills, detail), the when (primary, right-aligned, time under in ink-2, every row's ending at the same x) and always-shown move arrows. At 600px and narrower the when drops under the body, time after " · ", the thumbnail smaller. Moving a row keeps focus on it and says where it went ("Moved Kids camp to 3 of 6") in a polite status line.
