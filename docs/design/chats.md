## Chats desk (admin)
`/chats`, `/chats/{id}`: your own chats, the same as the phone's, written through the same methods. Chats sits under Home in the sidebar, outside the areas, with its count of unread chats and those waiting for your yes (`INavCounts`); checking other people's chats is *Chat safety* (`/chat-safety`, Safety area, `AppIcons.ChatSafety`).
- **Panes:** full height, ruled off by hairlines; settings start open above 1360px wide. The panel arrow is labelled *Show chat settings* or *Hide chat settings*.
- **One head line:** the list's title, the chat's `PaneHead` and the settings head share one band, so the three heads line up. The chat's lead names its ministry, what kind of chat it is and how many people ("{Ministry} · Team · group chat · 3 people").
- **The list:** a sticky head of the title and search, then rows flat on the page, grouped under each ministry's name, the ministry with the newest message first (owner, 2026-11-13: "make sure it's clear which chats belong to what ministry"). Each row's second line starts with the kind of chat ("Team · group chat", "Parents · leaders post", "Asking to join") before the latest message. The time sits top right with the unread count under it in a marker capsule; a muted chat shows the bell-off icon instead. No unread dot or chevrons here, since the count says it. A hint at the foot says where chats come from and where to start one. With nothing open, the middle says "Choose a chat".
- **The conversation:** pictures can be pasted or dropped anywhere on it (`DropOn`); dragged files outline it in dashed deep aqua. A message's ⋯ stays beside its bubble on the outer side, shown on hover or focus (Enter on the focused bubble opens it too) and always on touch screens; its popover, above the message and kept to the bubble's side, holds the reaction row, a rule, then the actions, Delete last after a rule (#76).
- **The composer (both sides):** the chat puts its reply or edit line, *Writing for* chips, mentions and picked files in `Above`; Send turns to a check while editing. While its field has focus the phone hides the keyboard's ^ v ✓ bar. With `EnterSends` (the admin panel): Enter sends, Shift+Enter starts a new line, Escape stops a reply or edit, and a small hint under it says so (hidden on touch screens).
- **Messages (both sides):** the reaction picker is the same chips in a row, without counts, at the top of a message's sheet (the admin popover's reaction row). A reply's quote is a button: it reads older messages until the original is there, scrolls it to the middle and rings it in marker for a moment; if the phone doesn't have it, the quote says "It isn't on your phone, so it can't be shown." Typed Markdown in a bubble shows bold, italic, strikethrough, highlight, code, links, lists and quotes; headings, tables, splits and pictures stay as typed (owner, 2026-11-14).
- **Chat settings:** the same `ChatPeople` as the phone's sheet: *Mute this chat* as a toggle row in a settings group, *Who's in it* as people with "Leader" at the right, then the hints. For a leader the head's ⋯ holds *Delete the chat…*, red, asking in a `Sheet`.
- **At 900px and under:** below the narrow window's bar, the list, or the chat with a quiet "Chats" back button above its head, or its settings in place of the conversation; the heads drop the shared band.

## Checking a chat (admin)
`/chat-safety/check/{id}`, in *Chat safety*: one chat folded down to what you're looking for, in the shared workbench under a title band.
- **Logged note:** first in the conversation and sticky at its top, a masthead-navy box with a marker icon, saying the reading is logged and who can see the log.
- **Messages:** sand tiles: who, the time, any quiet pills and a ⋯ menu; a quote of what it answers; the text; its photos (opening in the picture viewer); its reactions as disabled chips. A match has the found words marked in marker. The message before and after a match is unfilled with a hairline ring. A reported message carries a danger ring. Days divide the conversation with a centred line.
- **Fold line (signature):** whatever doesn't match folds into one full-width Link button, "9 messages · Show", between dashed hairlines; it opens in place.
- **Reports:** in the record pane, each report a card with its title and reporter, then a field and its own save bar to mark it handled; the checks of this chat are listed under them.
- **At 1360px and under:** the rail becomes a band of three columns above the conversation (search across the top, the Who list scrolling); at 900px and under, one column.
