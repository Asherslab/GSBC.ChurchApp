App tabs: where staff choose them

Drafts → a draft → Tab bar. Staff choose up to four tabs (each a place in the app, its word and its icon, and who sees it); More is always the fifth. Three structures, dealt from the Hub canvas's world. Decided by the build (owner, 2026-11-08: "your call" on small questions); the reasons are under the cards.

Chosen
1 · THE PHONE IS THE CANVAS

Click a tab on the phone

3 · THE BAR, ENLARGED

The tab bar drawn big across the work area

Five large cells to click and drag, settings under them. Easy to aim at, but it isn't how the bar looks, so staff judge words and icons at the wrong size.

Word

Why 1: it is the Hub canvas's own world, so staff who arrange Home already know it (choose on the phone, settings beside, orange marker on the chosen thing), and the tab's word and icon are judged at the size people see them. Raised from 2: the tabs that aren't on the bar for the person shown (for another audience, dated, hidden, or past the fourth) are listed as rows beside the phone, with the reason, since the bar can't draw them; and keyboard users move a tab with arrow buttons. From 3: nothing; the tab cells on a true-size phone are 78 by 48 points, which is enough to click.
