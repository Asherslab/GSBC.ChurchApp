Roles, redone

What's there today, the roles I propose (one set per feature, with levels), how roles for one thing move onto the thing, and how today's holders carry across. The People page sketches are on the decision page; pick one there. Your answers so far: tick boxes can be ticked independently, roles are built in only, and the Roles page goes.

What's there today

  • 36 permissions, 21 built-in roles plus one made here (“Test Role”, nobody holds it). All of them sit in one list, named the way each feature happened to name them: “Announcer”, “App editor”, “Calendars manager”, “Digest maker”.
  • Six of the 21 are bookkeeping, never given by hand: Sermon follower, Series follower, Calendar added, Calendar turned off (what a phone follows or has added), App reviewer (marks Apple's demo account) and Draft reviewer (given when a draft is shared). They still show on Roles and in the Give a role picker.
  • Roles don't bring what the job needs. A Sermons editor, App editor or calendar editor can't pick or upload a picture unless someone also makes them a Media editor. Nothing on screen says so.
  • Giving a role is out of the way. People → a person → “See what they can do” → Who can do what, past a long “What can X do?” list, to a small picker at the side. People and Who can do what are two pages for the same person.
  • Roles for one thing are given far from the thing. A calendar's settings say “given in Who can do what: choose someone, then give them Calendar editor at this calendar”. An event's organisers have no screen of their own at all. Drafts are the one exception: Share already adds people on the draft itself.
  • Making roles (copy, tick abilities, check what changes) works, but nobody has used it, and it's most of what the Roles page carries.

Bookkeeping roles

Sermon follower, Series follower, Calendar added, Calendar turned off and App reviewer stay in the engine (they're how follows and the demo account work) but are marked hidden: they never show on People, in a tick list, or in “what can they do”.

Carrying today's holders across

  • Every role keeps its record and id, so each assignment, and each Home tile or announcement aimed at “people in this role”, follows it. Account manager is the one split: its holders get both People Manager and App Review Manager, and anything aimed at it is aimed at People Manager.
  • Access managers lose “make and change roles”, because nobody makes roles any more. Holders of the roles marked + Media gain seeing the library and uploading pictures, which the job already needed. Nobody else gains or loses anything. The mapping goes in access.md.
  • Roles made here (only “Test Role” in development, held by nobody) are removed. If a Home tile or announcement is aimed at one, it's listed first and nothing is removed until it's aimed elsewhere.

What goes, what stays

  • Goes: the Roles page, the role copy editor, Who can do what, and “See what they can do” on People.
  • Moves: “What can they do, and why?” becomes something you open from the person: abilities by place, each with the role that gives it.
  • Stays: the engine (permissions, scopes, the checks on every call, the rules on who may give what, the last-Owner guard) and the developer tool, for you only.
