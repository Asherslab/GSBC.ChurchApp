Arrange, then Done. The gallery's pill gains Arrange (⊞) beside drag, add and take out. Arranging lays the pictures out as equal numbered tiles in their order, each with its own handle and ×, so the order is easy to read and drag; Done (or clicking outside) puts the gallery back as it shows in the post. A gallery left with one picture becomes a single picture.
