A: A soft fade

The row fades into the page at whichever edge has more. Nothing is added; the cut-off chip or tab just softens. The cue shows only on a side with more to see: at the start, the right only; part way along, both sides; at the end, the left only. The same answer for chip rows and tabs, on the phone and in the admin panel.
