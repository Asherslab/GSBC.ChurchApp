Taking someone's leader or admin role away now leaves them on the ministry's team.

## For staff
Changed: In a ministry's People, "Stop leading, stay on the team" and "Stop being an admin, stay on the team" take away only that role; the person stays on the team. "Take off the team" is still how someone leaves.
Where: A ministry → People → choose a leader or admin.
Try: Make someone an admin of a ministry, then choose "Stop being an admin, stay on the team" and check they're still in the Team list.
