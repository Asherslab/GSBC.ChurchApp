What's next can now include church news posts alongside events; tap one to read the post.

## For staff
Changed: The weekly digest starts empty and you add what you want highlighted: From the calendar (everything in its dates, by day, with one-offs, special dates, breaks and dates needing details marked), A church news post, or Something else you write yourself. Every item can have a picture from the media library, including things you write. Choosing an item opens it in a dialog with its fields on the left and how it will look in the app and the email on the right; Cancel and Save at the foot, Remove from the digest in its ⋯. The preview beside the list is bigger, and clicking a title in the app preview opens that item. The email now sits centred on a soft background on a computer and fills the width on a phone; for each item with a picture you choose whether it sits beside the words or across the top, and you can add a photo across the top of the email. A news post shows in the app only to people who can see the post, and its email entry has a Read the post button.
Where: Admin panel → This week → Weekly digest. The phone's What's next (Home tile or More).
Try: Add two events from the calendar and a church news post, write a "Carols in the park" item with a picture, and set its picture across the top. Check the Email preview, Copy for email into Elvanto's HTML source and send a test to yourself; check it on your phone. Send to the app and open What's next.
