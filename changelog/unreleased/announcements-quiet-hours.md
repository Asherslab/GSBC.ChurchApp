Announcements tell you when phones will get one sent in quiet hours, and what Urgent does.

## For staff
Changed: Between 9pm and 7am, phones wait until 7am for an announcement unless it's urgent. Announcements now say so before you send (on the page and in the confirm sheet, which also has the Urgent switch) and afterwards, for example "Phones buzz at 7am (quiet hours). Turn on Urgent to buzz them now." Urgent says what it does: phones buzz straight away, even in quiet hours, and it breaks through Focus.
Where: The admin panel, Announcements.
Try: After 9pm, write an announcement to yourself, press Send and read the confirm sheet; then schedule one for 9:30pm and read what it says.
