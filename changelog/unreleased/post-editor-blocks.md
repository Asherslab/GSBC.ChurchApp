Dragging a picture, video, card or table by its handle now works in every browser and by touch, and one picture can be moved or taken out of a gallery.

## For staff
Changed: Drag any block in a post by its ⠿ handle (Safari, Chrome, or a finger on the phone); an aqua line shows where it lands, Escape puts it back, and Alt ↑ or Alt ↓ on the handle moves it with the keyboard. Each picture in a gallery has a ⋯ with Move earlier, Move later, Replace and Take this picture out. Captions under every block look the same, menu items no longer have a gap under them, a card's look panel spans the editor, and a post that starts with a picture keeps its bold, headings and lists in the editor.
Where: Church news → write or change a post, in the admin panel and on the phone.
Try: Make a gallery of three pictures, take the middle one out from its ⋯, then drag the gallery below a paragraph by its handle.
