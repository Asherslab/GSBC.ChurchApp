The outreach map can fill the whole window, typing on it no longer loses your place, and adding an address to skip happens in one card.

## For staff
Changed: A Full map button at the map's top right hides the admin sidebar, the Outreach heading and tabs, and the row of jobs; the job's panel floats on the map with the job named at its top (choose another job there), and you can drag it by its head to the other side or use Move the panel right in that menu. Leave full map or Esc brings everything back; the full map stays on until you close the tab, and the panel's side is remembered on that computer. Typing in any box on the Map tab (Places, a saved area's name, a skip area) keeps its place instead of losing it after every letter. A new address to skip has its own Find the address search: the nearest matches are numbered in the card and on the map, or click the letterbox on the map. Places' own search now finds places already skipped. New addresses to skip (and ones made from a walker's report) show walkers a Don't letterbox marker unless you turn it off; existing ones are unchanged. Opening a day to plan from Plan a day no longer blanks the page. The map's credits fit on one line (the ⓘ shows them all), and suburb names give way to our pins, such as Meet here.
Where: Admin panel → Outreach → Map.
Try: Open Plan a day, choose a day, click Full map, drag the panel by its head to the right and press Esc. Then open Places, click Pick a letterbox, type the start of an address and click the 1 in the list.
