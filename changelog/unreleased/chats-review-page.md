Checking a chat now opens a full page with search and filters, and its photos open bigger.

## For staff
Changed: Opening a chat on the Chats desk (after saying why) now goes to a page of its own. Search for words, or narrow by who sent it, when, photos or files, edited or deleted, or reported: everything else folds into a "9 messages · Show" line, so each match sits with the message before and after. Photos open in the picture viewer. The chat's reports sit on the right, where you can mark them handled, and an auditor can pause a sender from a message's ⋯ menu. On the desk, Delete the chat and Pause are now in the ⋯ menu.
Where: Admin panel → Chats → Chats (or Reports → Read it in the chat).
Try: Choose a chat, say why and Open the chat. Search for a word, then choose a person under Who and open a fold to read around a match. From a report, Read it in the chat and mark it handled.
