The Prayer desk's list of requests fits beside an open request, with every Sent date whole.

## For staff
Changed: With a request open, the list of requests was a little wider than its column, so the Sent dates at its right were cut off. It now fits its column at any desktop width.
Where: Admin panel → This week → Prayer.
Try: Open a request and look down the Sent column of the list beside it, with the window wide and then narrower.
