Outreach leaders can see on a map which streets each campaign has done lately, and how much outreach adds up to.

## For staff
Changed: The Outreach page in the admin panel has three new tabs. Coverage shows each campaign's streets done in its rest period on the map, darker the more recently, with No junk mail and the places outreach skips; click a street or draw round several to see how many letterboxes they hold and mark them not done (with Undo). Numbers shows letterboxes, kilometres, walks and conversations for the last 13 weeks, this year or 12 months, against the time before, each outreach day's totals and the walkers counted by name. Places lists every area and address outreach skips, No junk mail and hostile marks, to change or clear, and adds an area (drawn on the map) or an address (clicked on the map). A walker's report now shows the place on the map and its photo, and can skip an area round it. Settings says every setting's usual value, sets spare tracts, and lists the map areas with Rebuild, Rename, Delete and Add.
Where: Admin panel → Ministries & groups → Outreach → Coverage, Numbers, Places, Reports and Settings.
Try: Open Coverage, draw round a few streets, mark them not done, then Undo. Switch Numbers to This year. In Places add an area round a few houses and delete it again.
