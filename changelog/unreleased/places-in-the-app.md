Find any page or file in the app, see it on a phone, copy its link or announce it.

## For staff
Changed: The admin panel has a new page, Places in the app, listing every page and file in the app grouped by area, with a search. Choose one to see it on a phone beside the list, who can open it, and its link to copy for a bulletin, an email or a message. From there, Announce it starts an announcement that opens that place, Change it in a draft takes you to Drafts for Home, and Open in Media goes to a file. In Announcements, What it opens → A place in the app now lets you pick from the same list instead of typing an address. It only offers places everyone the announcement reaches can open.
Where: The admin panel → The app → Places in the app. Announcements → What it opens.
Try: Open Places in the app, search for a file, copy its link and open it on your phone. Choose Home and press Announce it. In a new announcement, set What it opens to A place in the app and choose What's new; send it to your phone and tap it.
