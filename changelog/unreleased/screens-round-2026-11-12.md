Tap the quote in a chat reply to jump to the message it answers. On Account, your birth date now sits with your name.

## For staff
Changed: Tapping a reply's quote scrolls up to the original and briefly rings it, loading older messages on the phone if it needs to; a deleted original says so, and one that isn't on the phone says that instead. On Account (app and admin panel), the age question and your birth date moved into the You group, and Your family is one group again, with Add a child inside it. In the admin panel, a person's birth date and age now sit under their name, beside their email, with Change or Add it opening a small sheet; the Family tab is only about their family.
Where: Any chat on the phone; Account; People → a person.
Try: Reply to an older message, then tap the quote in your reply. Open someone in People and change their birth date from under their name; check the Family and Safeguarding tabs still read it.
