A news post shows its day beside a bigger title, a larger first paragraph and a foot saying who posted it, and pictures no longer show twice.

## For staff
Changed: A post's page opens with a navy rule, the day in a block beside the title ("Today 14 Nov") and who it's from under the title. The first paragraph is set larger, so write the main point there. A split into cards (===) now starts the next card under a navy rule, like a new item. The post ends on a sand foot: "Posted today by … for everyone". The navy band that picture-less posts had is gone; every post now looks the same at the top. Pictures and videos that open a post still lead as a strip you swipe; the ones further down stay where you put them.
Where: Any church news post on the phone, a ministry's post, and Preview on a phone when you write one in the admin panel.
Try: Open a short post with no picture, then one that starts with a picture, and compare the tops. Write a post whose first paragraph says what to do, and check it in Preview on a phone.
