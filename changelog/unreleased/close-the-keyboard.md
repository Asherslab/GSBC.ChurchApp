A button on the post-writing bar puts the keyboard away, and taking a photo or video to attach no longer closes the app.

## For staff
Changed: The formatting bar that sits on the keyboard when you write a news post ends with a Hide the keyboard button, always in view even when the bar is slid sideways. In a chat and in Send feedback, tap anywhere outside the message box to lower the keyboard. The grey hint in an empty post no longer starts under the blinking cursor. The app also asks for the camera and microphone when you take a photo or video to attach; before, the app closed the moment you chose to take one.
Where: News → New post on the phone; any chat; More → Send feedback; anywhere you add a picture or video.
Try: Start a new post, tap into the text, then tap the keyboard button at the right end of the formatting bar. In Send feedback, add a picture and choose Take Photo or Video.
