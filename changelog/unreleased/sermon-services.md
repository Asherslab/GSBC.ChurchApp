Sermons name their service one way: Sunday Life Groups, Sunday Morning, Sunday Evening, Wednesday Night, or a special one like a conference.

## For staff
Changed: A sermon's service is now chosen from a list instead of typed. The four standard services come first, then any other service a sermon already uses, such as a conference session; type a new one and choose Add to create it, and it's offered next time. Services from the website are matched onto the standard ones ("Sun PM" is Sunday Evening), and existing sermons were changed to match.
Where: The admin panel, Sermons: the Service field on a sermon, and on Upload a sermon.
Try: Open a sermon and click Service to see the list. Type "Conference, Tuesday night", choose Add, and save; open another sermon and the new service is in its list.
