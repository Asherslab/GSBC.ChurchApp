A ministry can move under a different one, and its news can include the ministries under it.

## For staff
Changed: A ministry's Settings has *Where it sits*: choose another ministry it sits under and the app says what changes before anything moves (which leaders will and won't lead it, and whose combined news or calendar it joins or leaves). Its own people, posts, files and calendars move with it. You need to lead both the ministry and its new place; only staff make a ministry top-level. *What it uses* also has *Show news from the ministries under it* (off unless you turn it on): their posts show on the ministry's News, each saying which ministry it's from, and people only see the posts they'd see in that ministry. Important posts still notify only the ministry they were posted in.
Where: A ministry's Settings, in the app (More → Ministries) and in the admin panel (People → Ministries).
Try: In Choir's Settings, choose a different ministry under *Sits under*, read what changes, then choose Keep it where it is. Turn on *Show news from the ministries under it* in Kids Ministries and open its News.
