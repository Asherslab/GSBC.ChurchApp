A News tab for the whole church, with pictures and video, plus the news of every ministry you're in. It works offline too.

## For staff
Changed: Church news replaces Announcements. A notice for the church is a church news post, sent with a notification that opens it (on unless you turn it off; urgent skips quiet hours). Posts can be saved as a draft, scheduled, or sent to your own phone first; a writer without the publishing role asks for theirs to be published. A ministry's leaders can ask for one of their posts to go in church news, and church news staff put it in with one tap. A post's page reads like a bulletin item: its day beside the title, a larger first paragraph and "Posted today by … for everyone" at the foot. Archive takes a post out of everyone's News and takes back its notification; Put it back returns it as a draft. Home can show a News tile (Front page, What's new or Headlines), and an urgent post takes it over for the day. Announcements Writers and Senders are now Church news Writers and Publishers; announcements already sent are under Earlier announcements, where they can still be recalled. A new Large uploads role lets staff add video and audio up to 2 GB (everyone else 100 MB).
Where: News in the app; This week → Church news in the admin panel; the News tile in App styling → Home screen.
Try: Write a post with a picture, save it as a draft, then post it and open it from the notification. Archive it and put it back.
