A News tab for the whole church, with pictures and video, plus the news of every ministry you're in. It works offline too.

## For staff
Changed: Church news replaces Announcements. A notice for the church is now a church news post, written in blocks (text, headings, pictures and galleries with captions, video, audio, files, a YouTube or Vimeo link) and sent with a notification that opens it (on unless you turn it off; urgent skips quiet hours). Posts can be scheduled, and a writer without the publishing role asks for theirs to be published. Ministry posts are written the same way, and a ministry's leaders can ask for a post to go in church news; church news staff put it in with one tap (or keep it in the ministry), and the ministry's edits follow it there. Announcements Writers and Senders are now Church news Writers and Publishers; announcements already sent are under Earlier announcements, where they can still be recalled. A new Large uploads role lets staff add video and audio up to 2 GB (everyone else up to 100 MB). Telling people a date is off is urgent on that day.
Where: the News tab in the app (New post, and To look at for drafts and asks); Church news in the admin panel's sidebar; a ministry's News tab and a post's ⋯ menu (Put it in church news). News is a starting tab; change the tab bar under App styling.
Try: Write a post with a gallery and a video link, send it to your phone first, then post it for everyone. In a ministry, write a post for everyone with Ask for church news on, then answer the ask from Church news.
