Staff can write an announcement, try it on their own phone, and send it to the church now or later.

## For staff
Changed: A new Announcements page in the admin panel. Write a title and a message, choose who it's for, and see how it looks on a lock screen. Send to my phone sends it only to you, marked Test. Send now, or set a time to schedule it; people who can write but not send get Ask to send instead. Non-urgent announcements wait until 7am if sent overnight; urgent ones go straight away.
Where: Admin panel → Announcements (needs the Announcer role).
Try: Write an announcement, press Send to my phone, and check it on your phone. Schedule one for a few minutes ahead and watch it arrive.
