Staff can write an announcement, see it on a lock screen as they type, and send it to the church now or later.

## For staff
Changed: A new Announcements page in the admin panel opens on this week and next, with every sent and scheduled announcement on its day and the ones not sent yet underneath. Write a title and a message and watch them appear on a lock screen beside you; it saves as you type. Choose who it's for, now or later, and what it opens. Sending asks you to check first and then says how many phones it went to. A sent announcement shows how many people have read it. Scheduled ones also show on Home's week, and one waiting to be sent shows under Needs you.
Where: Admin panel → Announcements (needs the Announcer role).
Try: Write an announcement and press Send to my phone; check it on your phone. Choose Later, pick a day and time a few minutes ahead, press Schedule it, check what the sheet says, and watch it land on its day and then arrive.
