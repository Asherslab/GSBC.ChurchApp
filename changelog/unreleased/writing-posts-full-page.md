Writing a post now looks like the post: bold looks bold and lists look like lists as you type, and in the admin panel a post has a whole page to itself.

## For staff
Changed: The text in a post formats as you type, with no ** marks and no "How it looks" box: use the bar above the text, or ⌘B, ⌘I and ⌘K at a desk; the marks show faintly only where your cursor is. Pasting from Word or Google Docs keeps bold, italic, lists and links. In the admin panel, writing or changing a post is a full page: the post on the left with Write and Preview on a phone tabs, who it's for, the notification, when and the link in a column on the right that you can fold away. A ministry's posts open the same page. On the phone the format bar sits just above the keyboard.
Where: Admin panel → Church news → New post, or open a post. A ministry's News → New post. In the app, News → New post.
Try: Start a church news post, type a line, press B and type some bold words, start a list from the bar, paste a paragraph from a Word or Google document, fold the settings column with its arrow, then look at Preview on a phone before posting.
