A ministry like the church office or accounts can now be hidden, so only its own people and staff see it.

## For staff
Changed: A ministry's Settings has *Hidden from everyone else* in The details. A hidden ministry, and every ministry under it, leaves the list of ministries, search, Home tiles and joining for everyone except its team, its attendees and their parents, the leaders above it and staff who look after ministries. What it posts, files or puts in a calendar for everyone goes to those people only. Its people see a *Hidden* pill on its page, and the admin panel's ministry tree marks it with a crossed-out eye. The switch explains what it means in its own line, so nothing else on the page moves. Life Groups are always shown, so a Life Group can't be hidden or sit under a hidden ministry.
Where: A ministry's Settings → The details, in the app (More → Ministries) and the admin panel (Ministries & groups → Ministries).
Try: Hide a test ministry, save, and check it's gone from the ministries list for someone who isn't on it, still there (with the Hidden pill) for someone on its team, and marked in the admin tree. Then show it again.
