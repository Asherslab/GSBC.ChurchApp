*Read the post* in the What's Next email now shows the post itself on a computer, or on a phone without the app, instead of a page asking you to open the app.

## For staff
Changed: A church news post opened from a link in a browser shows the whole post (pictures, video, files, event and sermon cards) under a navy Good Shepherd band, with a line at the end that it's also in the app. A post for everyone opens without signing in; a post for signed-in people or a role shows only to someone signed in to the admin panel who may see it, and otherwise says to open it in the app. *See all events* in the email still opens the calendar on a phone with the app, and in a browser goes to the website's Events page.
Where: The *Read the post* and *See all events* buttons in the What's Next email (Weekly digest → Copy for email), or any church news post's shared link.
Try: Copy the email for a digest with a church news post in it, paste it into a test email, and open *Read the post* on a computer while signed out of the admin panel. Then try *See all events*.
