The weekly digest starts on any day, today by default, and covers one week, two weeks, a month or dates you choose.

## For staff
Changed: The digest used to start on the Monday after today, so a digest made on Monday missed that week's events (Anniversary Sunday, the AGM, Kids camp). It now starts today unless you choose another day under From, and How long is One week, Two weeks, Month or Custom (any end date, up to three months). The desk reopens the digest you worked on this week until it ends; next week starts a new one.
Where: The admin panel, Weekly digest.
Try: Open Weekly digest, check From is today, choose Two weeks and see this Sunday's events in the list. Choose Custom and pick an end date.
