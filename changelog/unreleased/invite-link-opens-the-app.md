Tapping the link in your invitation email on your phone now opens the app ready to set up your sign-in, instead of a "not found" page.

## For staff
Changed: The invitation email's link (…/welcome?code=…) opened the app on a "Not found" page. It now opens Welcome with the code filled in, so the person can create their passkey straight away.
Where: The invitation email, opened on a phone with the app installed.
Try: Send yourself an invitation from People, open the email on your iPhone and tap the link. The app should open on Welcome with "Create a passkey".
