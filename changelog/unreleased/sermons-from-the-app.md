A sermon now shows its Bible passage.

## For staff
Changed: Sermons no longer come from the old website. Every sermon is uploaded in the admin panel, and the website shows them through its Sermons pages from the app. The switch for where sermons come from, Check now and copying the website's audio are gone, along with "from the website" labels and Use the website's. A sermon has a Bible passage and an About this sermon field you can fill in or correct, and a series or speaker has one picture you can choose or remove. In dev and UAT the sermons read from the website were cleared; they come back, every one of them, with the new Import from the website page.
Where: Admin panel → Sermons; Sermons → Series and speakers; a sermon's page on the phone and on the website.
Try: Open a sermon in the admin panel, type its passage (for example John 3:16-21) and save; open it on your phone and see the passage under the title.
