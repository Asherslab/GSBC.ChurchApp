Two map areas can now include the same region, such as a city that overlaps the area around the church.

## For staff
Changed: A map area you add can take in regions another area already has. Those regions aren't built again and phones don't download them twice; the estimate says how many are already in another area. A region stays on phones while any area has it on, so switching it off in one area, or deleting that area, keeps it for the others; its row says which other area has it ("also in Around the church"). The message "A region you add is already in another area" is gone. South Brisbane has moved onto the same grid as Around the church by itself, and its own regions build again once.
Where: Admin panel → Outreach → Map → Map areas.
Try: Add a map area called "Brisbane city", 10 km around the city. The line under the fields says how many of its regions are already in Around the church. Add it, open it, and switch off a region that is also in Around the church: the foot says it stays on phones for Around the church.
