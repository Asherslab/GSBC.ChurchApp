Changing an event in the admin panel now opens a full page, as Church news does, and its About has the same formatting bar and Insert as news.

## For staff
Changed: In the admin panel, choosing an event in the calendar shows it on the right as people see it, with Change the event (or Change this date for one date of a regular event). Change, New event and a regular event each open a page of their own: the event's head at the top, its fields below, where it shows (or how a regular event runs) in a column on the right that folds away, a Preview on a phone tab, and Cancel and Save at the foot. Back returns you to where you were in the calendar. An event's About, on the phone and in the admin panel, now uses the same editor as news: bold, lists and links in its bar, and pictures, video, files and app cards under Insert. A head no longer says a word twice: when the title already has the label ("Anniversary" over "Anniversary Sunday Service"), the calendar's name shows above it instead.
Where: Calendar in the admin panel; editing an event on the phone.
Try: Open Calendar, choose a Sunday service and press Change this date. Add a sentence and a picture to About this date, look at Preview on a phone, then save and check you're back on the calendar with it chosen.
