What's next shows the next two weeks at church in the app: the one-off events, the specials and the breaks, each with its picture.

## For staff
Changed: A new Weekly digest page makes What's next from the calendar. It fills itself from the next two weeks' one-off events, specials, breaks and dates still needing details, in the calendars you choose. Untick what you'd leave out, drag or use the arrows to reorder, reword anything for the digest (the calendar keeps its own words) and add something that isn't on the calendar. Copy for email gives the finished email to paste into the email tool; Send to the app puts it on What's next in the app, where each item from a private calendar shows only to the people that calendar is for. Sending doesn't notify anyone; Send to my phone sends you alone a test notification that opens it.
Where: The admin panel, Weekly digest (for the new Digest maker role, given in Roles). In the app, What's next is a place you can link to from Home or an announcement.
Try: Open Weekly digest, reword one item and watch the preview change, switch the preview to Email, then Copy for email and paste it into a new email. Send it to the app, then Send to my phone and tap the notification.
