The tabs along the foot of the app can now change without an update, and places that aren't tabs are under More.

## For staff
Changed: The tab bar is chosen in a draft, like Home. Up to four tabs, then More, which is always last. Each tab opens any place in the app, with its own word and icon, and can be for everyone, people signed in or people in roles, between dates. Taking a tab off the bar keeps its page: it moves into More and is still reached from Home's tiles, links and notifications. Until you publish a change, the tabs stay Home, Sermons and More.
Where: Admin panel → Drafts → a draft → Tab bar. Click a tab on the phone to change it, or Add a tab.
Try: Start a draft, open Tab bar, add a tab that opens This week, move it next to Home, and preview the draft on your phone under More → Previews.
