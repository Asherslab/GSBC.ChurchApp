The first build for staff to try: signing in, drafts and previews, and themes.

## For staff
Changed: This is the app's foundation rather than its features. Home, Account, Previews, Appearance and Storage are in; sermons, events and prayer come next.
Where: Home, and the More tab for everything else.
Try: Sign in under More → Account. If you can arrange the app, make a draft in the admin panel and look at it on your phone under More → Previews. Switch between light and dark under More → Appearance. Anything wrong or confusing: take a screenshot and send it with TestFlight's feedback.
