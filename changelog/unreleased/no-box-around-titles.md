title: No stray outline around page titles
audience: everyone
added: 2026-11-09

Page titles, such as a sermon's, no longer sometimes show a coloured outline when you open them.

## For staff

Changed: the app moves focus to each page's title so screen readers announce it; iOS sometimes drew a focus outline around it. Only Home's title hid it before; now every title does.
Where: any page with a title, e.g. a sermon.
Try: open a few sermons, series and speakers; no outline around the title.
