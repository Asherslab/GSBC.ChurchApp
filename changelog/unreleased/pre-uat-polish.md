Send several chat messages in a row: the keyboard stays up. Switches that are off are easier to see, and initials keep their circle in dark mode.

## For staff
Changed: After you send a chat message, the cursor stays in the message box (the keyboard stays up on the phone). A person's page in the admin panel opens on a new Profile tab: their name, email, birth date and age (change it there), and their account, passkey and when they joined, each in its own card; the heading keeps just their name, with a pill when the account needs you (turned off, not invited yet, or an invitation that expired). On a ministry's Settings, turning on *Hidden from everyone else* explains what that means in the switch's own line, so nothing else on the page moves. Also: page tabs scroll sideways on narrow windows instead of wrapping, a record beside a list (Prayer, Weekly digest) no longer runs below the window, so its Save is in reach, Sermons' search says "Search sermons", the ⋯ beside someone's chat message sits next to their bubble, your Account page lists your name, email and birth date one under the other, and *Import from the website* is now *Import sermons*, so the sidebar fits on one line.
Where: Chats (app and admin panel); People → a person; Ministries → a ministry → Settings; Account.
Try: Send three messages in a chat without tapping the box again. Open someone in People and look at Profile. Turn *Hidden from everyone else* on and off on a ministry's Settings and watch nothing jump.
