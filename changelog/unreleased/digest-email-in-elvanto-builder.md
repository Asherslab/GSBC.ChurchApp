The weekly digest's email now comes out centred on a full-width background and stays light in dark mode when sent from Elvanto.

## For staff
Changed: Copy for email keeps the email light in Apple Mail's dark mode, and the line under the Email preview says how to paste it into Elvanto. Paste it into an HTML block in Elvanto's drag-and-drop builder, not the classic HTML source, which pinned the email to the left and let Mail recolour it.
Where: Weekly digest → Email → Copy for email.
Try: In Elvanto, start an email in the drag-and-drop builder, set Body → Content Width to 600px, add an HTML block, set its padding to 0, paste, and send yourself a test.
