Listen to or watch every sermon, by series or speaker, and pick up exactly where you left off, even with the screen locked.

## For staff
Changed: A Sermons tab, filled from the website's sermons. It opens on the sermon you were part-way through (if you listened in the past week), otherwise the newest. Audio plays in the background with the lock screen, Control Center, AirPods and car controls; the player at the bottom has speed, back 15 and forward 30 seconds and a sleep timer. Videos play right in the app. Where you got to is kept on the phone, and once you sign in it follows you to your other phones. Home's Latest sermon tile is real now.
Where: The Sermons tab, and the Latest sermon tile on Home.
Try: Play a sermon, lock the phone and use the lock-screen controls. Switch tabs while it plays. Completely close the app and reopen it: it should remember where you were. Open a series and a speaker, and search for a word from a title. On a sermon with a video, press Watch. Sign in on a second phone and check Keep listening.
