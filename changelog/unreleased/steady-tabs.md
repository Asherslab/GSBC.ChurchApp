The row of tabs at the top of a ministry now only slides sideways, and keeps the tab you're on in view.

## For staff
Changed: The tab row under a ministry's header (About, News, Calendar, Files, People, Settings) no longer drops or cuts off its labels while you swipe it. It moves sideways only, has no scrollbar, and scrolls itself to show the tab you're on.
Where: Any ministry in the app, and every page with a row of tabs.
Try: Open a ministry you lead on the phone, swipe the tab row left and right, then tap Settings: the row stays level and Settings stays in view.
