While you type in a form on the phone, Cancel and Save stay at the bottom of the page instead of riding on the keyboard, and menus no longer get cut off.

## For staff
Changed: On the phone only a message box (a chat's, Send feedback's) sits on the keyboard; a form's Cancel and Save stay at the foot of the page. Send feedback keeps its picture box above the text when the keyboard is up. Menus such as a person's role always show whole. A chat's Delete is at the foot of its settings (the cog) instead of a separate ⋯, and its attach button is a paperclip. In the admin panel every place that takes a file (a ministry's files, the media library, a sermon's audio, a Home icon, Send feedback) has the same dashed box you can drop files on or click. The date picker jumps to any month or year from its heading, and a birth date no longer offers Today or the Sundays. Settings pages run the full width with short fields side by side. In Calendar the order of calendars changes the moment you drop one, the drag handle sits inside the chosen calendar's highlight, and an editor's Cancel and Save sit right at the bottom.
Where: The phone's forms and menus; a chat's settings; Ministries → a ministry → Files and Settings; Account; Calendar; any date field.
Try: On the phone, edit a date of a regular event and type in its title: the keyboard covers Save until you scroll to it. Open a ministry's People and a person's role menu. In the admin panel, drag a document onto a ministry's Add a file, open a birth date and click the month to pick 1985, and drag a calendar into a new place.
