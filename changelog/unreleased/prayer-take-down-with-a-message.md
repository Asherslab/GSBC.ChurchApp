Taking down your prayer request no longer says it was answered. You can leave a message for those who prayed, such as a thank you.

## For staff
Changed: On your own request, *Take down your request* (on the prayer wall) and *Close your request* (otherwise) replace *Remove from prayer wall* and *Mark as answered*. A message is optional; with one, the wall shows it under the request for 3 days as "Message from Sam S.", not as "Answered". The pastoral team sees *Closed by them* and the person's message, kept apart from their own *Answered*. Requests people had already taken down with words of thanks now show as *Closed by them* with those words as their message.
Where: Prayer → Yours → a request, in the app. In the admin panel, Prayer has a *Closed by them* filter, and a closed request shows its message under the care steps.
Try: Send a request for the wall, approve it, then on the phone tap *Take down your request*, write "Thanks for praying" and choose *Take down in 3 days*. Check the wall shows "Your message: Thanks for praying" without a tick, and that the admin panel shows *Closed by them*.
