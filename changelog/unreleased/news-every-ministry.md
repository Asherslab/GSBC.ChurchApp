Church staff who look after news can see every ministry's posts in one place.

## For staff
Changed: Church news in the admin panel has All ministries, under its own heading in the list on the left: every ministry's posts that are for everyone, newest first, each naming its ministry and saying when it's in church news or asking to be. Open one to read it; publishers can put it in church news from its ⋯ menu. The app's News has the same as a third tab, All ministries. Both are for people with the new permission "See every ministry's posts for everyone", which Church news Publishers have; give it to other roles under Roles.
Where: Admin panel: This week → Church news → All ministries. App: News → All ministries.
Try: As a Church news Publisher, open All ministries, open a ministry's post and choose Put it in church news… from ⋯. On your phone, open News and the All ministries tab.
