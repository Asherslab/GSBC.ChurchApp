Wherever a ministry offers something for its team, it now names the team, such as "The Discipleship Team".

## For staff
Changed: When you choose who a ministry's post, file, calendar or chat is for, the team option is named for the ministry ("The Discipleship Team") instead of "The team". A post for the team shows the same name, and its button says "Post for the Discipleship Team".
Where: A ministry's News, Files, Calendar and Chats, in the app and the admin panel, and Chats → Start a chat in the admin panel.
Try: Open a ministry, start writing a post or adding a file, and look at "Who it's for". Then start a chat and check the same.
