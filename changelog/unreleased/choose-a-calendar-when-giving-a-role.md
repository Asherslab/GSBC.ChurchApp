Giving someone a role at a calendar now means choosing the calendar from a list.

## For staff
Changed: In Who can do what, "Which calendar" was a text box, and whatever was typed was saved, so a role given at "Sunday services" applied to no calendar at all. It's now a list of the calendars, and a role can only be given at one that exists. Drafts are chosen from a list the same way.
Where: The admin panel, Who can do what, Give a role.
Try: Choose someone, give them Calendar editor, and pick a calendar from Which calendar.
