Speakers are now just a name and a photo, managed in the admin panel.

## For staff
Changed: A speaker no longer has Other spellings or Or join them with. Rename a speaker freely: sermons from the website stay with them. When a sermon is credited to the wrong person, correct the speaker on that sermon; if the website used a new spelling for someone, correcting its first sermon makes later ones under that spelling go to the right person too. Two speakers can't have the same name.
Where: The admin panel, Sermons, Series and speakers; and the Speaker field on a sermon.
Try: Open Series and speakers, choose a speaker and rename them, then check that their sermons still show under them.
