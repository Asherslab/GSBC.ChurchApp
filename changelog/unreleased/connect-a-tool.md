Developers can connect a tool, such as Claude on their computer, to read the app's errors without opening the admin panel.

## For staff
Changed: People with the Developer role can connect a tool on their Account page. It gets its own session, listed with your devices, that you can sign out like any other. A tool can't use anything sensitive (roles, people's details, accounts, the pastoral inbox) and stops working after 30 days unused.
Where: The admin panel → Account → Connect a tool.
