Coverage, places to skip, saved areas, reports, map areas and planning a day now share one big map in the admin panel.

## For staff
Changed: Outreach has a Map tab in place of Coverage, Places, Areas and Reports, and Map areas has left Settings. Down the map's left edge are its jobs (Coverage, Places, Saved areas, Reports, Map areas, Plan a day); choosing one pulls out its pane, and the same button folds it away for the whole map. Whatever you click on the map (a street, a skipped place, a report, a saved area) opens in a card at the top right. Layers (top left) switches what's drawn, and the map remembers your layers, the job you had open and where you were looking, on this computer. You can now type an address to skip it (Places → Type an address to skip) instead of finding it on the map. Drawing an area shows a navy bar at the top with what to do, Undo last point, Cancel and Finish. Planning a day's routes happens on the same map: Plan a day → choose the day.
Where: Admin panel → Ministries & groups → Outreach → Map.
Try: Open Places, type an address you know and add it as a place to skip, then delete it from its ⋯. Draw a skip area with Finish, and use Undo last point while drawing. Fold the pane away, switch a layer off, leave the page and come back: it should open as you left it. Open Plan a day and choose a day.
