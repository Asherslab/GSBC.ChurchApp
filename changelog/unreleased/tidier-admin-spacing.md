Lists, tables and bars across the admin panel sit properly in their space again.

## For staff
Changed: The chat list on Chats has the same gap on its left as the search above it. Weekly digest's send buttons sit on a bar that runs the width of its pane, with room under them. Tables keep their columns inside the list beside an open record: People, Safeguarding, App review, App health and Chat safety hide a column the record already shows rather than cutting the last one off, App health's call names break at their dots, and number columns line up under their headings. A person's Safeguarding and Family tabs, Sermons' player, Home's "Needs you" dates, Paused people, the filter lists down the left of a page, and the Announcements week strip ("Today" fits its day) no longer squeeze or crowd. On a phone-width window, tables, chips, chosen rows and the first line under a page's tabs have their gutter.
Where: Chats, Weekly digest, People (and a person's Safeguarding and Family tabs), Safeguarding, App review, App health, Chat safety, Sermons, Home, Announcements, Places, Ministries.
Try: Open Chats and check the chat icons sit 16px in from the sidebar. Open App health, choose an error, and check the tables beside it fit without scrolling sideways.
