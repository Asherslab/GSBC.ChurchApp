Remove your answered request from the prayer wall, with words of thanks shown for 3 days first if you like.

## For staff
Changed: A person's own request on the wall has a Remove from prayer wall button in place of It was answered. It still marks the request answered, with optional words of thanks; with thanks it stays on the wall for 3 days showing them, otherwise it comes off at once. Requests not on the wall keep It was answered.
Where: Prayer → your request (from Your requests) in the app.
Try: Put a test request on the wall, open it as its sender, tap Remove from prayer wall, add thanks and check the wall shows them and says it leaves in 3 days. Try another with no thanks and check it comes off at once.
