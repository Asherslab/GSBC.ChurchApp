Starting a new chat in a ministry no longer fails without saying why.

## For staff
Changed: Only people with a role in a ministry (its leaders, or its team where the ministry lets the team start chats) can start its chats, because they're the people in them. Someone who looks after every ministry from the admin panel (an Owner or Ministries Manager) without a role there no longer sees New chat; the Chats tab says why and how to start one. Before, New chat showed for them, saved an empty chat and then failed. If starting a chat is ever refused, the sheet now shows the reason.
Where: A ministry's Chats tab, in the app and the admin panel; Chat safety → Chats → New chat.
Try: As an Owner with no role in a ministry, open its Chats tab: you should see the line about having a role there. Give yourself Leader under People, come back and start a chat.
