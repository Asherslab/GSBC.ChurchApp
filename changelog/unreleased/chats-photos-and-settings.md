Tap a photo in a chat to see it full screen, swipe between a message's photos, and swipe down to close. A chat's settings are behind the cog.

## For staff
Changed: A message's photos open in the picture viewer. The chat's top-right icon is now the settings cog (mute and who's in it); leaders delete a chat from the ⋯ beside it. In a ministry's Chats tab, someone who can't start a chat is told who can (the leaders, or the leaders and team, as the ministry's settings say).
Where: The app, in any chat and a ministry's Chats tab.
Try: Open a chat with a photo, tap it, swipe down. Tap the cog. As a leader, use ⋯ → Delete the chat… in a test chat.
