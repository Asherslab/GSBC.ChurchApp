Home's title no longer stays cut off after you scroll down and back up, and the What's new pages fit on the screen.

## For staff
Changed: On the phone, scrolling Home down and back up left the top of "Good Shepherd" cut off until you changed tabs; it now comes back whole. What's new no longer runs off the right edge when there are many pages to step through. In the admin panel's chats, a message's menu now ends neatly on Delete (no line or gap under it), with Delete last after a rule, and the round cog that showed and hid *Chat settings* is now a panel arrow that points the way the pane will go. On a narrower window (1200px and under), showing Chat settings now moves the chat list aside so the conversation isn't squeezed to a word a line.
Where: Home on the phone; What's new on the phone; Chats in the admin panel.
Try: On Home, scroll down a little and back to the top. In the admin panel, open a chat, hover a message and open its ⋯, then use the arrow at the right of the conversation's heading to hide and show Chat settings.
