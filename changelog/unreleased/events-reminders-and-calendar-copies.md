Organisers are reminded when a date of a regular event is still missing what it needs, and a calendar can copy another calendar by its iCal address.

## For staff
Changed: When a regular event says what each date needs (a place, for example), its organisers get a reminder in the app 7 days and 2 days before a date that's still missing it, once each. The reminder clears itself when the details are added. Organisers are the regular event's *Event organiser* holders, or the calendar's editors when nobody holds that. A calendar given an iCal address (a school's term dates, a Google Calendar a ministry already keeps) copies its events every half hour and stays read-only here. Each calendar also has its own link for calendar apps on phones and computers.
Where: Reminders arrive in your inbox and as a notification. The iCal address is in a calendar's settings in the admin panel's Calendar page.
Try: Set a regular event to need a place, leave a date in the coming week without one, and look for the reminder in your inbox; then add the place and see it clear.
