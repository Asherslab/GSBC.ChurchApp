Life Groups show in the church calendar on the Sundays they meet, and an all-year group that's off for a while says where to go instead.

## For staff
Changed: Life Groups can show as one weekly entry in a calendar of your choice. It's on every Sunday a set of groups meets, at that set's time; the Sundays between sets, and any Sunday taken off a set, show as not on, like a break. It follows every change to the Sundays by itself, so it can't be edited in Calendar, which says where it's changed. In the app, tapping it opens Life Groups. An all-year group can also be marked not on for one set of Sundays, with a note for its people and a group to point them to. Its people keep their place, they're told once, and it's back on the next set by itself.
Where: admin panel → People → Life Groups: *Shown in a calendar* at the top, and *An all-year group isn't on these Sundays* under a set of Sundays.
Try: On Life Groups, choose a calendar under *Not shown in a calendar* and save. Then open Calendar, pick that calendar and look for Life Groups on the Sundays they meet and the not-on Sundays between. Mark an all-year group not on for a set, and check Life Groups in the app shows it with your note.
