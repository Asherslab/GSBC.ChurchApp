Days away show on a month and can repeat weekly, for part of a day, with weeks off. Someone training on a roster sees who they're on with.

## For staff
Changed: Days away are set on a month: a whole day, a run of days, or every week on chosen days, all day, mornings, evenings or between times, with an end date or until further notice; choose a day on the month to skip it just that once. Leaders can now change the days away of anyone on their team. On a roster date, someone not yet trained for a position sits on their own line under it ("Training, with Jo"); alone, the line is amber and the place stays open until someone trained is on. How the roster works is in groups, with the swap rule as three choices and a sentence saying what happens. Deleting a position, taking a date off the roster and taking someone off a date are in the ⋯ menus.
Where: A ministry's Roster tab (Days away at the bottom, How it works in the ⋯ menu next to Add a date), Account on the phone, and a person's Days away tab in the admin panel.
Try: Add "Every Sunday, mornings, until further notice" for yourself, then choose a Sunday on the month and say you're not away that day. Put someone who isn't ticked as trained on a position that needs training, check it's amber, then put someone trained on with them.
