A calendar that's no longer needed can be removed, with everything in it.

## For staff
Changed: A calendar's settings in the admin panel have Remove this calendar. Before anything happens it says what goes: its events and regular events, how many people and phones have added it, and that calendar apps subscribed to its link stop getting it. Removing it takes its events off every phone and calendar app, including other calendars they were shown on, and can't be undone. Events from other calendars that were shown on it stay where they belong.
Where: The admin panel, Calendar: the settings button beside a calendar (for whoever manages calendars).
Try: Make a calendar called "Test", add an event to it, then open its settings and choose Remove this calendar. Read what it says will go, and remove it.
