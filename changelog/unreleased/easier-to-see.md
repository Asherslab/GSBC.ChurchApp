Buttons you can't press yet, quiet text, past events and greyed-out rows are easier to read, in light and dark and in every theme.

## For staff
Changed: A button that can't be used yet ("Add the note" before you've written one, Save with nothing to save) is now an outline with a readable label, instead of a faint grey shape that almost disappeared. Greyed-out rows, past events and past days, hints and placeholders, and the quieter text on the masthead change colour instead of fading, so they stay readable. Themes the church makes keep quiet text readable on panels and fields too, and App styling now warns before a theme would make it hard to read.
Where: Everywhere in the app and the admin panel; the theme warnings are in App styling → a custom theme's colours.
Try: Open a Feedback ticket or a prayer request and look at "Add the note" before you type, in light and in dark. Then open the Calendar and look at an event that has already happened.
