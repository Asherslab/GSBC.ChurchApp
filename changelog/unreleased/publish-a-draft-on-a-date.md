A draft can now be published on a date you choose, such as next year's theme on 1 January, even if nobody has the admin panel open.

## For staff
Changed: Turning a draft on for everyone now offers three plainly different choices: turn it on now (and off later, or by itself at a time), turn it on between two dates, or publish it on a date. Publishing on a date does exactly what Publish now does, at that moment, for good, with nothing to turn off. The draft and the Drafts list show "Publishes on Friday 1 January 2027, 12am" until then. If something it changes was changed for everyone in the meantime, it doesn't publish: the draft says why, and whoever scheduled it gets a notification.
Where: Admin panel → Drafts → open a draft → More → Turn on for everyone, or publish on a date. Scheduled drafts are listed under Publishing on a date.
Try: Open a draft, choose Publish it on a date, and pick a time a few minutes away. Watch it move to Published recently by itself. To cancel one, open it and choose Don't publish it then.
