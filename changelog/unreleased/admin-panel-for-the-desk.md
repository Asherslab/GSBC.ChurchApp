## For staff
Changed: The admin panel has a sidebar with every area, and opens on This week: the next seven days of dated changes and anything waiting on you, such as a draft to publish. People and Media are tables you can sort, filter and search, with the one you choose shown beside the list. Up and down arrows move through a list.
Where: The admin panel, on a computer.
Try: Open People and press the down arrow to step through accounts. Open someone in People and see their roles.
