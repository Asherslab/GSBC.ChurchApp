The app and the admin panel now do each kind of job one way, with buttons in the same places, so once you've learnt one screen you know the rest.

## For staff
Changed: Anywhere you save, Cancel is at the bottom left and Save at the bottom right, with "Saved" or "You have changes that aren't saved" on its own line above them; a form for something new no longer says it has unsaved changes. In the admin panel, a list keeps the record you chose open beside it at every window size (the Calendar's editor no longer drops below the week at narrower widths), dialogs show their main button at the right with Cancel or Keep it at the far left, and the sidebar's Account opens a menu where you can choose Light, Dark or Automatic for the admin panel and sign out. Sidebar names no longer break in the middle of a word. On the phone, a ministry's roster shows Add a date with a ⋯ menu beside it for Positions and How it works, and its grid no longer shows a sliver of the scrolled dates beside the positions.
Where: Every admin page with a list, the sidebar's Account, the Calendar in the admin panel, and a ministry's roster on the phone.
Try: In the admin panel, open the Calendar in a narrow window and click an event; then click Account at the bottom of the sidebar and choose Dark. On the phone, open a ministry you lead, go to its roster and tap ⋯ beside Add a date.
