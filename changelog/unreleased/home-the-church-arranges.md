Home is now a grid of tiles the church arranges: this Sunday's bulletin, the latest sermon, this week and more, in the app's own colours.

## For staff
Changed: Home is laid out as tiles of different sizes, each one set up by staff. Some tiles are filled by a part of the app (Notifications shows what's unread); parts still being built show an example and open a "coming soon" page. The "Connection" list under the tiles is gone; if the phone is offline, the top of Home says so.
Where: Home, the first tab.
Try: Open the app and look at Home. Tap a tile and check it opens the right place. Open Notifications from its tile and come back.
