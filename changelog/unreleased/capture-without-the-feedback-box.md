Capture this page in Send feedback now takes a picture of the page itself, not the feedback box on top of it.

## For staff
Changed: The feedback box hides as soon as you press Capture this page, before the browser asks what to share, and the picture is taken only once the page is showing without it. Before, Safari often captured the box itself.
Where: Admin panel → Send feedback (bottom left) → Capture this page.
Try: Open Send feedback on a page, press Capture this page, share the window, and check the picture shows the page with nothing over it.
