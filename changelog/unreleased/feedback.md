Staff and testers can report a problem or an idea from the app, with screenshots and videos from the photo library.

## For staff
Changed: A new Feedback role shows Send feedback in More. Pick screenshots or screen recordings, say what's wrong or what you'd like, and send; it works with no signal and goes when the phone is back online. The app adds its version, the phone, the page you came from and the phone's state by itself. Anyone else reaches it by tapping the church logo at the foot of More three times, on a phone the app has verified, up to 5 a day, so you can report something from a member's phone when they hand it to you. In the admin panel, Feedback (under Technical) is a board of numbered tickets: New, In progress, Fixed to check, Resolved, and Not doing. Open one beside the board, or ⌘-click (Ctrl-click) to open it in its own tab. Keep notes, move it along by dragging or with its status, and block a phone that misuses it. Pictures and videos are deleted once a ticket is Resolved or Not doing.
Where: More → Send feedback in the app (with the Feedback role), or three taps on the logo at the foot of More. Technical → Feedback in the admin panel, with the Feedback Triage role.
Try: Give yourself the Feedback role, send feedback from your phone with a screenshot, then open Feedback in the admin panel, move it to In progress and add a note.
