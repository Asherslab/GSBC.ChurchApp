Staff and testers can report a problem or an idea from the phone or the admin panel, with screenshots and videos, without leaving the page they're on.

## For staff
Changed: On the phone, Send feedback is written like a message: the paperclip adds screenshots or videos, and it works with no signal. It records the page you came from, the last few before it and, on iPhone, the pages you took screenshots on. Anyone else reaches it by tapping the church logo at the foot of More three times, on a phone the app has verified, up to 5 a day. In the admin panel, Send feedback (bottom left) opens over the page: Capture this page takes a screenshot without the feedback box, or paste or drop your own. Feedback is a board of numbered tickets (New, In progress, Fixed to check, Resolved, Not doing): keep notes, move a ticket along, open its screenshots full size, and block a phone that misuses it. Pictures and videos are deleted 7 days after a ticket closes, so one closed by mistake can be reopened with them.
Where: More → Send feedback (or three taps on the logo at the foot of More); bottom left of the admin panel; Technical → Feedback.
Try: Send feedback with a screenshot from the phone and with Capture this page from the admin panel, then move a ticket along on the board.
