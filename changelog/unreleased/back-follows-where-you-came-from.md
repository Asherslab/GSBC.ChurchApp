Pages you open from the tab bar no longer show a Back button, and pages you open from More say "More".

## For staff
Changed: Prayer, Ministries and Life Groups always said "‹ Home" at the top, even when you opened them from the tab bar or More. Now a place on the tab bar has no Back (and shows the bell), one opened from More says "‹ More", and one opened from a Home tile says "‹ Home". News, Sermons, the calendar, Chats and the pages in More follow the same rule, so the calendar opened from More now has a way back.
Where: Any page you reach from the tab bar, More, or a Home tile.
Try: Open Prayer from More (it says "‹ More"), then from a Home tile if there is one (it says "‹ Home"). If Prayer is one of your tabs, open it from the tab bar: no Back, just the bell.
