Prayer's To approve row no longer says requests are waiting once they're on the prayer wall.

## For staff
Changed: To approve counted every request not yet marked answered, so requests already on the wall still showed as waiting. It now counts only requests asking for the wall; requests on the wall are listed under On the prayer wall now.
Where: Prayer → To approve, in the app.
Try: Approve a request for the wall and check To approve's count drops and the request moves to On the prayer wall now.
