title: A special date no longer lists the usual dates
audience: everyone
added: 2026-11-09
icon: calendar
area: Calendar
---
A date with its own name, such as Anniversary Sunday Service, no longer lists the ordinary Sundays as its other dates.

## For staff
Changed: When one date of a regular event is given its own title, its page leaves out Other dates, in the app and on the website's events page. A date changed only in its time or place still lists them.
Where: An event's page in the app's Calendar, and the website's events page.
Try: Open Anniversary Sunday Service: no Other dates. Open an ordinary Morning service: its other Sundays are listed.
