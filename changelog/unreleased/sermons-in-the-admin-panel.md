Sermons are uploaded, checked and corrected in the admin panel.

## For staff
Changed: Admin panel → Sermons lists new sermons that need a look beside the one you're checking: fix its title, speaker, series, service, Bible passage or video, then Looks right · next. Series and speakers can be renamed and given a picture. The AV team adds Sunday's audio straight from a computer into the church's own storage, and a podcast feed is made for Apple Podcasts and Spotify. New roles: Sermons Editor and Sermons Uploader.
Where: Admin panel → Sermons, and Sermons → Series and speakers.
Try: Correct a sermon's series. Upload an mp3 with a title and speaker, and see it on your phone a moment later.
