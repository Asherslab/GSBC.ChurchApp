New sermons can be checked, corrected and uploaded in the admin panel.

## For staff
Changed: Admin panel → Sermons lists new sermons that need a look beside the one you're checking: fix its title, speaker, series, service or video, then Looks right · next. What you correct stays put when the website changes, and "Use the website's" puts it back. Series and speakers can be renamed, given a picture, and two spellings of one speaker joined. Where new sermons come from switches between the website and uploads here; with uploads, the AV team adds Sunday's audio straight from a computer, the website's audio is copied into the church's own storage, and a podcast feed is made for Apple Podcasts and Spotify. New roles: Sermons editor and Sermon uploader.
Where: Admin panel → Sermons, and Sermons → Series and speakers.
Try: Correct a sermon's series, then Use the website's. Join two spellings of a speaker. Switch to Uploads here, upload an mp3 with a title and speaker, and see it on your phone a moment later; then switch back to the website.
