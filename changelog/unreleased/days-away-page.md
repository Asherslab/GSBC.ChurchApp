Saying when you're away is now its own page, with the month to tap or a list of everything you've said. Open it from a team's Roster tab or from Account.

## For staff
Changed: The month of days away no longer sits at the bottom of the Roster tab. The roster shows a short "Your days away" row with when you're next away, and leaders get a second row, "The team's days away", with how many people are away in the next four weeks. Each opens a page with Month and List: Month is the calendar you tap to add or change days; List shows every repeating rule and one-off day. Add days away is on both.
Where: A ministry's Roster tab, or Account → Rosters → Your days away, on the phone.
Try: Open a team's Roster tab, tap Your days away, add a weekend away from the month, then switch to List to see it. As a leader, open The team's days away and choose someone else.
