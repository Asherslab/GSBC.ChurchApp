Drafts have undo and redo, and undoing a publish now asks first and can be published again.

## For staff
Changed: While editing a draft, Undo and Redo sit beside Share (or press ⌘Z and ⇧⌘Z); typing in one field undoes as one step, and "Undone. Redo" shows by the save status. Undo changes to a tile, or Put back a tile you took out, and it says what happened with an Undo. Under Published recently, click a publish to see what undoing it would change and who it affects, then Undo this publish; it stays in the list as Undone, with Publish it again to bring it back.
Where: Admin panel → Drafts → your draft; and Drafts → Published recently.
Try: In a draft, change a tile's title, press ⌘Z and then ⇧⌘Z. Open a publish under Published recently to read what undoing it would do (then Cancel, unless you mean it).
