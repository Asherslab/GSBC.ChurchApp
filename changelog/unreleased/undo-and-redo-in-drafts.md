Drafts have undo and redo, and undoing a publish now asks first and can be published again.

## For staff
Changed: While editing a draft, Undo and Redo sit beside Share (or press ⌘Z and ⇧⌘Z); typing in one field undoes as one step, and "Undone. Redo" shows by the save status. Undo changes to a tile, or Put back a tile you took out, and it says what happened with an Undo. When the window is narrow, Share and More show as icons and Undo and Redo move into More, so Publish now always stays on one line. Under Published recently, click a publish to see what undoing it would change and who it affects, then Undo this publish; it stays in the list as Undone, with Publish it again to bring it back (and if a later publish already brought its changes back, it shows as published again, and the app tells you when there's nothing to change).
Where: Admin panel → Drafts → your draft; and Drafts → Published recently.
Try: In a draft, change a tile's title, press ⌘Z and then ⇧⌘Z. Open a publish under Published recently to read what undoing it would do (then Cancel, unless you mean it).
