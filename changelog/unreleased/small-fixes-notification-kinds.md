Writing a post no longer blanks the page, post links open the right post, and notification settings list only what reaches you.

## For staff
Changed: On the phone, the format bar sits on the keyboard while you write a church news or ministry post, with the post still showing above it (it used to cover the whole page in white). Opening one church news post from another now shows the new post instead of the first, and the same is fixed for a team's days away, a person's page in the admin panel and the writing pages. Notification settings show leaders' notifications (asks to join, roster swaps, chat reports, details a regular event needs) only to people who lead or run those things, and a parent's notifications only to parents.
Where: The phone's New post page; church news posts that link to each other; Account → Notification settings.
Try: Start a new church news post on the phone and tap into "Write the post": the title and the text stay in view with the format bar on the keyboard. Sign in as someone who leads nothing and open Notification settings: there's nothing about asks to join or roster swaps.
