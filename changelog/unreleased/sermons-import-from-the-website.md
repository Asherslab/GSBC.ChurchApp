The admin panel can bring every sermon on the old website into the app.

## For staff
Changed: A new admin page, Import from the website, brings all of the website's sermons (887 on 9 November, not just the last year the podcast feed had) into the app with their audio, pictures, series, speakers, Bible passages and videos, copied into the church's own storage. It runs on the server, shows what it has found, brought across, skipped and couldn't bring across, and lists any failures with Try again. Running it again only picks up what's new or failed. Imported sermons don't notify anyone and don't land in Needs a look. Older sermons that said "Guest Speaker" get their speaker from the title ("Bro Kit Ward - Hope In A Storm" becomes Kit Ward's "Hope In A Storm"). The page goes away once the app is in production.
Where: Admin panel → Import from the website (the Owner only).
Try: Start the import, watch the numbers climb, close the page and come back; then open Sermons on your phone and scroll back through the years.
