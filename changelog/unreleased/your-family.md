Add your children to your Account, give a child with a phone a sign-in code, and choose how ministries reach them. Young people can ask a parent to link.

## For staff
Changed: The Account page has Your family. A parent adds a child (name and birth date; no email or phone needed), opens them to make a sign-in code for the child's own phone, chooses how ministries reach them (directly, through the parent, or both) and can ask another parent to link too. A young person gives their birth date and asks a parent by email; the parent says yes on their Account page and gets a notification. Signing in has "Got a code from your parent?". Everyone is asked their age once (or can say they're 18 or older).
Where: Account, in the app and the admin panel.
Try: Add a child, open them and make a sign-in code. On another phone, sign in with "Got a code from your parent?". Then, signed in as a young person, ask a parent with their email and say yes as the parent.
