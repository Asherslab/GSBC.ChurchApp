Choosing Other for a weekly digest's length shows the whole To date.

## For staff
Changed: With Other chosen, the To date ran off the edge of the editor and was cut off. It now moves onto its own line when there isn't room.
Where: Admin panel → Weekly digest, How long → Other.
Try: Choose Other and check you can see and pick the To date.
