Outreach days are planned on a map: draw the area, check the routes, publish them, and print one map a route with a QR code that opens it in the app.

## For staff
Changed: Outreach has Days and Areas tabs. A day has its date, times, where people meet and its campaign; its routes page splits an area you draw (or a saved area) into even routes, avoiding streets the campaign did recently, and gives you tools to adjust them: scissors, a brush, a lasso, an eraser, a start pin and the meeting point, with undo and redo, lock, split, merge and rename, Rebalance and Regenerate. Nothing reaches walkers until you publish. Print the maps makes an A4 page for each route with its streets in order, the bundles to take and its QR code. On the day the same page, and a page on the phone for outreach leaders, shows who has each route and how much is walked, with the routes no one has first.
Where: Admin panel → Ministries & groups → Outreach → Days (and Areas). On the phone: Outreach → the day → How the day is going.
Try: Add an outreach day for next Saturday, open its routes, draw an area around a few streets near church, brush a street into another route, then publish and print the maps. Scan a printed QR code with your phone.
