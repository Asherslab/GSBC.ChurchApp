Asking for prayer no longer offers "I'd like someone to get in touch" or asks for a phone number or email.

## For staff
Changed: A prayer request no longer carries a phone or email, and the pastoral team's steps are New and Answered (Followed up is gone; requests that were followed up are New again). The Getting in touch section and the Get in touch pill are gone, and any phone or email already sent was deleted.
Where: Prayer → Ask for prayer in the app; Prayer → To approve → a request on the phone; People → Prayer in the admin panel.
Try: Ask for prayer and check there's no get-in-touch switch. Open a request as the pastoral team and check its steps read New and Answered with no Getting in touch section.
