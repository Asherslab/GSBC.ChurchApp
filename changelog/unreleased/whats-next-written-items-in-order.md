Things the church office writes into What's next now sit in the order they chose, in the right week.

## For staff
Changed: something added to the weekly digest with Something else (no calendar date) showed in the app under the current week, above everything else, while the desk's preview showed it in the digest's own weeks. The app now places it as the preview does, and it leaves What's next when the digest's dates end.
Where: Weekly digest in the admin panel; What's next in the app.
Try: Add something with Something else below a calendar event, send the digest, and check What's next in the app shows it below that event.
