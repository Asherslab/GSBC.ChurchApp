Your blue card and Safe Ministry training on your Account page now come straight from Safe Ministry Check, kept up to date each night.

## For staff
Changed: Once the church's Safe Ministry Check key is in, each night the app reads (never changes) Safe Ministry Check and links each record to someone in the app by email, or by name and birth date. A linked person's blue card, training and Safe Ministry Check status come across and can't be edited here; change them in Safe Ministry Check. When Safe Ministry Check hasn't cleared someone, or something there needs looking at (a blue card it couldn't verify, a referee), Safeguarding flags them "Check Safe Ministry Check". Nothing else is copied across.
Where: People → Safe Ministry Check (when it last synced, Sync now, and the records still to match); each person's Safeguarding tab (where their details came from, link or unlink); People → Safeguarding (the new "Check Safe Ministry Check" filter).
Try: Open People → Safe Ministry Check and press Sync now. Choose a record under Still to match and link it to the right person. Open someone who's linked, check their blue card matches Safe Ministry Check, and try Unlink on a wrong match.
