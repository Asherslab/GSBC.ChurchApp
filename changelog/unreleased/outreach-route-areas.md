An outreach day can now have several areas: draw each route yourself, let the app split a bigger area into even routes, or both.

## For staff
Changed: Planning a day takes more than one area. Draw a route (click round its streets) and it's one route just as you drew it; draw the next straight away. Draw an area to split and the app splits it into routes as before. Each area has a chip on the map that says what it is, where you can switch it between one route and split, redraw it, save it for next time, or take it off the day. Where a route you drew overlaps an area the app splits, your route keeps those streets: the overlap is hatched and labelled, and every letterbox is counted once. Rebalance and Regenerate only rework the app's routes. Days you'd already planned are one area split by the app, as before.
Where: Admin panel → Outreach → Map → Plan a day → choose a day. The pencil (Draw a route) and the pentagon (Draw an area to split) are in the tools down the map's left edge.
Try: Open a day, draw two or three routes with the pencil, then split a saved area beside them. Draw one of your routes over the edge of the split area, check the hatching and that no letterbox is in two routes, then press Regenerate and see your routes stay as they are. Open an area's chip and take it off the day, then Undo.
