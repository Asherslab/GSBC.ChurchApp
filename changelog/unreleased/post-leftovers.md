Videos in a post show a picture of the video before you play it, and tables in a post fit a phone's width, scrolling sideways when they're wide.

## For staff
Changed: A YouTube or Vimeo video in a post (and in the editor) shows the video's own thumbnail, and an uploaded video shows its first frame, instead of a black tile. Tables keep short headings and cells on one line; a wide table scrolls sideways with a shadow at its edge. In the admin panel the formatting bar stays one row at every width, folding Code, Highlight, Strikethrough and the rest into its ⋯ when there isn't room, and it no longer sits on a square band in dark mode. Long menus, like Insert, show a shadow where there's more to scroll. *Preview on a phone* draws the church's own tabs. The ⋯ on a picture that's still uploading is easier to see in dark mode, and the table panel's cells no longer touch its edge.
Where: Church news and a ministry's News: write or change a post, and the post's page in the app.
Try: Put a YouTube link and a table with four columns in a post, then open *Preview on a phone*. Make the window narrower and watch tools move into the bar's ⋯.
