Outreach has a home in the admin panel: its campaigns, the settings walks run on, and the follow-ups and reports walkers send in.

## For staff
Changed: A ministry can run outreach (turn on Outreach under What it uses in its Settings); its leaders then hold the outreach permissions. Outreach has campaigns, each keeping its own record of streets done (General outreach is always there), settings for the rest period, walking pace, letterbox and door times, daylight hours, walking between streets and what's kept, and Follow-ups and Reports tabs for what walkers send in. The walking screens on the phone come next.
Where: Admin panel → Ministries & groups → Outreach.
Try: Turn on Outreach for a ministry, open Outreach, add a campaign with dates and a leaderboard, then change the rest period on Settings and save. Try deleting a campaign that has an outreach day still to come and read why it can't go yet.
