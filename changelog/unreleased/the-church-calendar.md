See what's on at church this week or swipe through the months, choose the calendars you want, and spot what's changed or not on at a glance.

## For staff
Changed: The calendar is in the app and the admin panel. On the phone, This week is one running list from today, with Week and Month (swipe the month sideways to move between months), chips to hide a calendar for a moment, and Browse to add calendars or subscribe in a calendar app. An event opens on its date with where, who to contact and other dates; something changed is marked until you've opened it. Home's This week tile now shows Coming up: one-off events, specials and dates that aren't on. In the admin panel, Calendar is a workbench: pick one of your calendars on the left to see and add to just that calendar, or All your calendars to see them together (with a switch per calendar to leave one out); the agenda or month is in the middle, and the chosen event, a calendar's settings (the sliders beside its name) or a break on the right. Needs details lists dates still missing a place or a contact. To put another calendar's event on yours, pick your calendar and choose From another calendar (in the app, Show an event from another calendar on your calendar's page): if you don't edit its calendar, its editors are asked, under Asked to publish. A regular event (Morning service, every Sunday) has its own page: it opens on its run of dates (changed ones marked, dates that aren't on as open rings), then what every date has beside how it runs (how it repeats, breaks, what each date needs, other calendars it's shown on). A date of a regular event is only that date: change its time, place or title for that day, or set it as not on, and it says what's different from usual with Put it back. New event asks Once or Regularly first. Each calendar lists its regular events (the Regular events view in the admin panel, and on a calendar's page in the app for its editors), and their dates carry a small repeat mark.
Where: In the app, This week (the Home tile, or Calendar). In the admin panel, Calendar in the sidebar. Calendar editors can also add and change events on their phone: + at the top of This week or Month adds one to a calendar they edit.
Try: On your phone, open a Sunday service in a calendar you edit, tap Edit, change the place for that date, save, and choose Tell people; then tap Edit the regular event › and look through its dates. Tap + on This week and choose Once or Regularly. In the admin panel, pick Sunday services, choose Regular events, open one and click a date in its strip to change only that date. Open Needs details and fill one in, then open Breaks and move the Christmas break's dates. Pick Kids on the left and add an event to it; then choose All your calendars and turn a calendar off to check for clashes.
