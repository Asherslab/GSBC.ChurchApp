See what's on at church this week or swipe through the months, choose the calendars you want, and spot what's changed or not on at a glance.

## For staff
Changed: The calendar is in the app and the admin panel. On the phone, This week is one running list from today, with Week and Month (swipe the month sideways to move between months), chips to hide a calendar for a moment, and Browse to add calendars or subscribe in a calendar app. An event opens on its date with where, who to contact and other dates; something changed is marked until you've opened it. Home's This week tile now shows Coming up: one-off events, specials and dates that aren't on. In the admin panel, Calendar is a workbench: pick one of your calendars on the left to see and add to just that calendar, or All your calendars to see them together (with a switch per calendar to leave one out); the agenda or month is in the middle, and the chosen event, a calendar's settings (the sliders beside its name) or a break on the right. Needs details lists dates still missing a place or a contact.
Where: In the app, This week (the Home tile, or Calendar). In the admin panel, Calendar in the sidebar. Calendar editors can also add and change events on their phone: + at the top of This week or Month adds one to a calendar they edit.
Try: On your phone, open an event in a calendar you edit, tap Edit, change the place for This date only, save, and choose Tell people. In the admin panel, open Needs details and fill one in, then open Breaks and move the Christmas break's dates. Pick Kids on the left and add an event to it; then choose All your calendars and turn a calendar off to check for clashes.
