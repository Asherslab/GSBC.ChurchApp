See what's on at church this week, choose the calendars you want, and spot what's changed or not on at a glance.

## For staff
Changed: The calendar is in the app and the admin panel. On the phone, This week is one running list from today, with Week and Month, chips to hide a calendar for a moment, and Browse to add calendars or subscribe in a calendar app. An event opens on its date with where, who to contact and other dates; something changed is marked until you've opened it. Home's This week tile now shows Coming up: one-off events, specials and dates that aren't on. In the admin panel, Calendar is a workbench: your calendars on the left, the agenda or month in the middle, and the chosen event, a calendar's settings or a break on the right. Needs details lists dates still missing a place or a contact.
Where: In the app, This week (the Home tile, or Calendar). In the admin panel, Calendar in the sidebar. Calendar editors can also change events on their phone.
Try: On your phone, open an event in a calendar you edit, tap Edit, change the place for This date only, save, and choose Tell people. In the admin panel, open Needs details and fill one in, then open Breaks and move the Christmas break's dates. Turn a calendar off on the left to check for clashes.
