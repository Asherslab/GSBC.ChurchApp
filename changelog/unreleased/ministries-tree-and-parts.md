Ministries under another now show right beneath it, a ministry shows only the parts it uses, and its calendar can include the ones under it.

## For staff
Changed: The app's Ministries list is a tree, like the admin panel's. In a ministry's Settings, *What it uses* turns News, Calendar and Files on or off: off hides the tab and everything in it from everyone, leaders too, and nothing is deleted. A hidden calendar's events leave people's week and calendar links until it's turned back on. *Show the calendars of the ministries under it* puts their events on the ministry's Calendar under *Coming up*, each saying which ministry it's from, and people only see what they'd see in that ministry. *New ministry* can now make a Life Group under any ministry, such as Kids Life under Kids Ministries.
Where: More → Ministries in the app, and People → Ministries in the admin panel. A ministry's Settings → *What it uses*.
Try: Open Ministries and tap a ministry under another. In Kids Ministries' Settings, turn on *Show the calendars of the ministries under it*, then open its Calendar. Turn Files off on a ministry you lead, check its Files tab has gone, and turn it back on.
