A sent announcement can be recalled: it leaves the app on every phone and stays in the admin panel as a record.

## For staff
Changed: A sent announcement has Recall…. It leaves the app's notifications on every phone, signed in or not, and anyone who hasn't had it yet won't get it, even if quiet hours were holding it. A push already on a lock screen can't be taken back. It stays in Announcements, marked "Recalled by" you with the time, and Copy into a new announcement starts a fresh one from it.
Where: The admin panel, Announcements: open a sent announcement. Recalling needs the same permission as sending.
Try: On the test server, send an announcement, see it in the app's notifications on your phone, then recall it and watch it leave the list and the bell's count. Open it again in Announcements to see how it's marked.
