The app now asks once if you'd like to hear from the church, then lets you choose what buzzes your phone.

## For staff
Changed: The first time the app opens, a sheet over Home offers notifications before anything else. Turn on notifications brings up the iPhone's own question; say Allow and the same sheet shows what can buzz your phone, with Done. Not now closes it and it won't ask again, but More → Notification settings can still turn them on. It shows only while the phone hasn't decided yet, so it doesn't appear in the web version. In the admin panel, Insights now counts how many phones have notifications on, turned off or not chosen yet, under How phones are set up.
Where: The first open of the app; Insights in the admin panel.
Try: Delete the app and install it again from TestFlight, then open it: the sheet should come up over Home. Tap Turn on notifications and Allow, then check the choices and Done. Delete and reinstall once more and tap Not now: completely close the app and open it again, and it shouldn't ask.
