Your passkeys and the devices you're signed in on moved to Account → Security, so the Account page stays short.

## For staff
Changed: Account now shows one Security row (how many passkeys you have and how many devices you're signed in on) and a Sign out button. Security has your passkeys (add, remove), every device and tool you're signed in on, this device first, with browsers named plainly ("Safari on a Mac") (sign one out, or Sign out everywhere), and in the admin panel Connect a tool.
Where: Account → Security, in the app and the admin panel.
Try: Open Account, tap Security, add or remove a passkey and sign out of an old device. Go back and check the row's counts changed.
