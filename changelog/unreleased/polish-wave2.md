Putting a ministry's tabs in order no longer blinks, and the admin panel fits a phone-sized window again.

## For staff
Changed: Dragging a tab in a ministry's Settings → Its tabs moves smoothly and stays where you drop it, and the tabs along the top change once, when you let go. Send feedback on the phone shows your screenshots and videos at the top again, with the message box at the foot. Deleting a news post or a pastoral note is now in its ⋯ menu and asks once; pastoral notes can be deleted on the phone too. Settings headings are smaller so the page title leads, and the last row of a group has no line under it. List pages in the admin panel keep a margin at the screen's edge on a phone.
Where: A ministry's Settings → Its tabs (phone and admin panel); More → Send feedback (phone); a ministry's News → a post → ⋯; Prayer → a request → Notes for the pastoral team → ⋯ (phone).
Try: Drag News below Calendar and watch the list and the tabs at the top. Open Send feedback and choose two screenshots. Open a post you wrote, choose ⋯ → Delete the post…, then Keep it.
