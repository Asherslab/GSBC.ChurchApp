Staff can add a map area for outreach by finding the town or suburb, and choose which parts of an area phones get.

## For staff
Changed: Adding a map area no longer needs latitude and longitude: find a town or suburb by name (or click the map), say how far around it, and see its squares (regions) with how many letterboxes and about how big the download is before it builds. Each area now opens on its own map page, where you click a region to switch it off (phones drop it and its maps are deleted; coverage and history there stay) or a + on the edge to add one. Only what you add is built, not the whole area again. Before anything goes, it says what uses it, such as an outreach day planned there.
Where: Admin panel, Outreach → Settings → Map areas. Open an area, or Add a map area.
Try: Open Around the church and click a square on the south side: the pane shows what it frees and what uses it; Cancel to leave it. Then Add a map area, type "Gympie", and look at the squares and the estimate without adding it.
