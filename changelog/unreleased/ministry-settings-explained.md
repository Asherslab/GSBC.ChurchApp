When you lead a ministry, its settings now say what each joining choice means, and ask before you leave with changes you haven't saved.

## For staff
Changed: The two joining choices are labelled *Joining the team* and *Coming along*, and each option says what happens (for example, Ask: "People ask to join the team, and a leader says yes"). A Life Group shows just *Joining the group*. The same labelled choices are on *A new ministry*. *Who it's for* on posts, links and calendars, and *Add them as* on adding a person, now have labels too. The Details fields are spaced apart, and *Save the details* shows "You have changes that aren't saved" or "Saved" beside it instead of a box. Leaving a ministry's settings, or a post, link, calendar, Sunday or Life Groups form, with changes you haven't saved asks first: save, discard them, or stay. Closing or reloading the browser tab warns too.
Where: Ministries → a ministry → Settings, and Make a ministry, in the admin panel and the app; Life Groups in the admin panel.
Try: Open a ministry's Settings, change the About text, then click another tab or ministry. Choose Stay, then Save and go on, and check the change is kept.
