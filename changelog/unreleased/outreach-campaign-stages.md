The app tells you when an outreach campaign is about to end, so you can help get the last tracts out.

## For staff
Changed: Outreach campaigns are now a board like Life Groups: Preparing, Ongoing, Wrapping up and Finished, with General outreach always on in the strip above. A campaign moves by itself on its dates (Ongoing on its start date, Wrapping up a set number of days before it ends, 7 to start, Finished the day after its end date), or by hand from its card's ⋯, by dragging it, or from the stage line in its record. Moving into Wrapping up tells everyone once that it ends soon. Walkers only see a campaign while it's Ongoing or Wrapping up. Each campaign now has its own bundle size (different paper, different bundles) and a count of bundles made, so you can see about how many went out against how many were printed.
Where: Admin panel → Ministries & groups → Outreach → Campaigns. Choose a card to open it where it sits.
Try: Open a campaign, set its bundle size and bundles made, and save. Drag a Preparing campaign to Ongoing. Check a day's planner and printed maps count bundles of the campaign's own size.
