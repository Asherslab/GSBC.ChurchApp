Speakers' photos are sharper, and a few speakers who had none now have one.

## For staff
Changed: The app now takes each speaker's photo from the website's Our team page, which has them at more than five times the size of the series pages' small circles. Speakers who aren't on that page keep the photo from the series pages, and a photo you chose for a speaker in the admin panel still wins. Some speakers get a photo for the first time. Team entries for couples are left out, so a speaker who's only shown with their partner still has none: choose one for them in the admin panel if you'd like.
Where: A speaker's page in the app, sermon rows, and Sermons → Series and speakers in the admin panel.
Try: After the next website read, open a sermon by a speaker on the Our team page and tap their name: the photo is crisp, not blurred.
