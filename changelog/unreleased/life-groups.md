Life Groups lists every group, with yours and your children's first, and shows in the church calendar on the Sundays they meet.

## For staff
Changed: Any ministry can be a Life Group with *Show in Life Groups* in its Settings, and keeps every other setting, including its own team. The Life Groups page is a board of each set of Sundays by stage (Being planned, Shown in the app, Now on, Finished) with all-year groups along the top; *Plan the next groups* takes a From and To date range. Life Groups can show as one weekly entry in a calendar of your choice, and a Life Group's linked calendar shows only its own Sundays. An all-year group can be marked not on for a set of Sundays, with a note and a group to point its people to. Leaders tell the group about one Sunday (like no class) with *Tell the group about a Sunday* on its Calendar tab. Finished groups can be archived and restored.
Where: Ministries & groups → Life Groups in the admin panel; More → Life Groups in the app; a Life Group's Calendar tab.
Try: Plan the next groups with a From and To date. Show Life Groups in a calendar and find them on the Sundays they meet. On a Life Group's Calendar, Tell the group about a Sunday and post No class this Sunday. Archive a test group and restore it from Archived groups.
