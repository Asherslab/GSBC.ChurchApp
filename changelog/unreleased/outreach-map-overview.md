The outreach map in the admin panel now shows the whole of Australia around our maps, so it's easier to see where to add a new map area.

## For staff
Changed: Outreach → Map pans over all of Australia. Places with walker maps are drawn clear and everywhere else sits under a grey veil, with a key saying what each shade means. Over the Queensland border it's hatched: areas can't be added there, because the address data is Queensland's. Outside our maps you can zoom in as far as suburbs, and the other jobs say they work where walkers have maps. Phones don't change: they still download only the regions they walk in.
Where: In the admin panel, Outreach → Map. Adding an area is under Map areas → Add a map area.
Try: Open Map areas → Add a map area, zoom out past the edge of our maps and click a town to the north. Its squares and letterboxes show before anything is built. Then click just over the New South Wales border and see the squares marked Outside Queensland.
