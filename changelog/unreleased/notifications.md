Hear from the church on your phone, and find every notice under the bell, even offline. Choose what buzzes your phone in More → Notification settings.

## For staff
Changed: The app can now send notifications. There's a bell at the top of each main screen with a count of what you haven't read; tapping a notification opens the thing it's about. Anything that needs you (a draft shared with you, or one waiting to publish) sits under Waiting at the top of the notifications until it's done, wherever you do it; everything else is under Everything. More → Notification settings chooses which kinds buzz your phone, and only lists the kinds you could get. If notifications are turned off for the app, Open Settings takes you there.
Where: The bell at the top of Home; More → Notification settings.
Try: Open More → Notification settings and tap Turn on notifications. In the admin panel, share a draft with someone who has the app: it arrives under Waiting with Preview it, and opens the draft's preview. Publish or delete that draft and check it leaves Waiting on their phone.
