Hear from the church on your phone, and find every notice under the bell, even offline. Choose what buzzes your phone in More.

## For staff
Changed: The app can now send notifications. There's a bell at the top of each main screen with a count of what you haven't read; tapping a notification opens the thing it's about. More → Notifications chooses which kinds buzz your phone. When a draft is waiting to publish, or someone shares a draft with you, you're told.
Where: The bell at the top of Home; More → Notifications.
Try: Open More → Notifications and tap Turn on notifications. In the admin panel, share a draft with someone who has the app, and check it arrives on their phone and opens the draft's preview. Ask to publish a draft and check a publisher is told.
