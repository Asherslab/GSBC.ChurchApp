title: Typing no longer pushes the app around
audience: everyone
added: 2026-11-14
icon: smartphone
area: The app
---
With the keyboard up, what you're typing stays in view, a button puts the keyboard away, and taking a photo to attach no longer closes the app.

## For staff
Changed: The tab bar hides while you type and the top of the screen stays put. A message box (a chat's, Send feedback's) sits right on the keyboard, and in a chat the keyboard stays up between messages; tap outside the box to lower it. A form's Cancel and Save stay at the foot of the page. Sheets like Add to the team sit just above the keyboard. Writing a post, the format bar sits on the keyboard with the post above it and ends with a Hide the keyboard button. The app now asks for the camera and microphone when you take a photo or video to attach; before, the app closed.
Where: Any screen with a text box: chats, Send feedback, a new post, Add to the team, Ask for prayer, Account.
Try: Send three chat messages in a row without tapping the box again. Start a new post and tap the keyboard button at the right end of the format bar. In Send feedback, attach a picture and choose Take Photo or Video.
