audience: everyone

With the keyboard up, the screen now ends just above it: the tabs step aside, nothing slides under the clock, and what you're typing in stays in view.

## For staff
Changed: The app now follows the phone's keyboard everywhere. The tab bar hides while you type, the top of the screen stays put, sheets (like Add to the team) slide up first and then sit just above the keyboard, and Send or Save stays right above the keyboard without its small print. Replying to a long message in a chat no longer pushes the Send button off the screen.
Where: Any screen with a text box: chats, Add to the team, Send feedback, Ask for prayer, a new post, Account and searching sermons.
Try: Open a chat and tap the message box, then reply to a long message. Open a ministry's People, tap Add to the team and search for someone. Write in Send feedback and check Send sits just above the keyboard.
