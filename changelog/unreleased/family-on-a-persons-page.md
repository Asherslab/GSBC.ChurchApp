A person's page has a Family tab: their birth date, children and parents.

## For staff
Changed: People → a person → Family shows their birth date, what's waiting (a young person's ask to a parent), the children they look after and their own parents. Staff with Safeguarding Manager can link a child or a parent, add a child who isn't in the app for a parent, unlink, take back an ask, and set how ministries reach a young person.
Where: People → a person → Family.
Try: Open a parent, link a child from the search, then open the child and see the parent listed.
