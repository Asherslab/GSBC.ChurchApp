You can delete your account from your Account page.

## For staff
Changed: Anyone signed in can delete their own account, and staff who manage people can delete someone's account. It signs them out everywhere and removes their passkeys, roles, listening history, notifications and private calendar link, and takes them off events they're a contact for. Their name stays on what they did, and their email can be used to sign up again. The church's last Owner can't be deleted until someone else is an Owner.
Where: In the app, More → Account → Delete my account…. In the admin panel, People → choose someone → Delete the account….
Try: Invite a test account, sign in with it, give it a role, then delete it from People and check it's gone from the list.
