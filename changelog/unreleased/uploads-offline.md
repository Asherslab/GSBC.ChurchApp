A picture or document you add on your phone is kept on the phone and sent with your change as soon as there's a signal, even if you've closed the app.

## For staff
Changed: On the phone, choosing a picture for an event, or adding a file to a ministry, no longer needs a connection. The file stays on the phone, the change shows as waiting to send, and both go together once you're online. If the library already has the same file, it's used rather than sent again. Leaders can now add documents and pictures to their ministry's Files from the phone (until now only links), and documents open from the Files list.
Where: In the app: an event's picture (Change → Upload → Choose a picture from this phone), and a ministry's Files → Add a file. Anything still waiting is listed under More → Storage → Waiting to send.
Try: Turn on aeroplane mode, add a file to a ministry's Files and save it. Completely close the app, turn aeroplane mode off and open the app again: the file goes, and it appears in the ministry's Files. Then tap it to open it.
