The short line about a ministry is now labelled "Short description", with a line saying where it shows.

## For staff
Changed: The field that used to say "In a line" is now "Short description", and the line under its label says it's one sentence shown under the ministry's name in the Ministries list and at the top of its page.
Where: A ministry's Settings → Details, and the New ministry sheet, in the app and the admin panel.
Try: Open a ministry's Settings, write a short description and save, then look at the ministry's page and the Ministries list.
