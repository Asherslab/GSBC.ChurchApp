The short line about a ministry is now labelled "The ministry's purpose in one line", with a hint saying where it shows.

## For staff
Changed: The field that used to say "In a line" is now "The ministry's purpose in one line", and its hint says it shows under the ministry's name on its page and in the lists of ministries.
Where: A ministry's Settings → The details, and the New ministry sheet, in the app and the admin panel.
Try: Open a ministry's Settings, fill in its purpose in one line and save, then look at the ministry's page and the Ministries list.
