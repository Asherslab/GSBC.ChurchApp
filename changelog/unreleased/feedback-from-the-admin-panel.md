Staff can report a problem or an idea about the admin panel without leaving the page they're on.

## For staff
Changed: Send feedback sits at the bottom left of the admin panel, just above Account, for everyone who uses the admin panel, whatever their role. It opens over the page you're on: Capture this page takes a screenshot of it (your browser asks to share the tab first), and you can paste (⌘V), drop or choose screenshots and videos too. Say what's wrong or what you'd like, and Send. The admin panel adds the page you were on and the last few before it, your browser and window, light or dark mode, its version and who you are. It lands on the same Feedback board marked Admin panel, and the board can show only what came from the app or from the admin panel. A ticket's status is now a row of stages you move it along. Closing a ticket keeps its pictures and videos for 7 days before deleting them, so one closed by mistake can be reopened with its files; the ticket says the day they go.
Where: Admin panel → Send feedback (bottom left). The board is Technical → Feedback.
Try: Sign in as someone with an admin page but no Feedback role (a ministry leader, say) and check Send feedback is there. On any admin page, click Send feedback, Capture this page, write a sentence and Send. Open Technical → Feedback, choose The admin panel, open your ticket, move it to In progress, then Resolved, and check the date its files go.
