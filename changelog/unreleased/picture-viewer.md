A screenshot on a feedback ticket opens to fill the window, so you can read it.

## For staff
Changed: Clicking a screenshot on a feedback ticket opens it over the whole window on a dark backdrop, with arrows and a strip of the others when there are several, and Download. The arrow keys move between them and Esc closes it; on a phone, swipe sideways for the next one and down to close.
Where: Feedback → any ticket with screenshots, docked beside the board or opened as a page.
Try: Open a ticket with two or more screenshots, click one, press → and ←, download one, then press Esc.
