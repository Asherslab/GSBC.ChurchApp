The bar above the tabs is gone: the Sermons tab shows play or pause, and the Sermons page keeps what you're listening to at the top with all its controls.

## For staff
Changed: The small player that sat above the tab bar on every screen has been removed. While a sermon plays, the Sermons tab's icon is a pause sign; when it's paused, or nothing is playing, it's a play sign. Tapping the tab still opens Sermons. The top of the Sermons page now shows the sermon that's playing, or was last playing, with a position bar you can drag, back 15 and forward 30 seconds, play and pause, and the speed (tap it to change); tap the sermon's picture or title to open it. Until someone has played a sermon, the top shows the latest sermon as before. The lock screen and Control Centre controls work as they did.
Where: The Sermons tab in the app.
Try: Play a sermon, then go to Home: the Sermons tab shows a pause sign. Pause it from the lock screen and watch the tab change to play. Open Sermons, drag the position bar, tap 1× to change the speed, and tap the title to open the sermon.
