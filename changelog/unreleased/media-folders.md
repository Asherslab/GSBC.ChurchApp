Media has folders, so pictures and files stay easy to find as the library grows.

## For staff
Changed: Make folders (and folders inside folders) in Media, and move pictures and PDFs into them by dragging them onto a folder, or by choosing several and pressing Move to…. Moving a file never changes where it's used: tiles, drafts and themes keep showing it. Deleting a folder asks where its contents go and never deletes a file. Search finds a file in any folder and says which folder it's in. Files people share through the app will be kept apart under Made by the app, marked with a lock. The picture sheet you use when choosing a picture for a tile can browse folders too, and opens where you last were.
Where: Media in the admin panel, and Choose a picture anywhere in the admin panel.
Try: Open Media, press New folder, then drag a few pictures onto it. Open Home in a draft, choose a picture for a tile, and open your folder from the sheet.
