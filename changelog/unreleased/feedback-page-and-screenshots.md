Feedback from the app now records the page you were on before you went to send it, and where you took screenshots.

## For staff
Changed: Feedback sent from the phone records the last page you opened outside the More tab, not a More page you passed on the way to Send feedback. It also sends the last few pages you opened, like feedback from the admin panel does, and on iPhone the pages you took screenshots on in the last hour, with the time. The ticket shows them as Before and Screenshots.
Where: More → triple tap the church logo → Send feedback. The ticket is in the admin panel under Technical → Feedback.
Try: Open Calendar, take a screenshot, go to More, open Help improve the app, go back to More and send feedback. Open the ticket in the admin panel and check its page is /calendar, Before lists the pages you went through, and Screenshots says /calendar with the time.
