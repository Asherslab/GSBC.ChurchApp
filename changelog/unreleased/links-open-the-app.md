A link from the church opens the right place in the app, and tells you kindly if it isn't there for you.

## For staff
Changed: Links copied from Places in the app open the app on a phone that has it, straight at that page or file. A link to something that's been taken down, or that you can't see, shows a short explanation instead of a blank page. On a computer, the link shows which place it is and how to get the app.
Where: Any link starting with the app's address and /open, in an email, a message or a web page.
Try: Copy a link from Places in the app, message it to yourself and tap it on your phone. Then completely close the app and tap it again.
