One-off events and changed dates are tinted in the calendar, and tapping an event in Home's This week opens it.

## For staff
Changed: In the app's week and month lists, one-off events and changed dates (such as Anniversary Sunday Service) sit on a soft tint in the theme's highlight colour; ordinary repeats stay plain. On Home, each event in the This week tile opens that date's page, and the tile's heading still opens the calendar. The event editor shows its picture as the app frames it, with Change and Remove beside it, in the admin panel and on the phone; Home's tile settings use the same picture field.
Where: The app's Calendar (week and month) and Home's This week tile; the event editor in the admin panel's Calendar and on the phone.
Try: Open Calendar in the app and find Anniversary Sunday Service. On Home, tap it in This week. In the admin panel, open an event with a tall picture and see it shown whole.
