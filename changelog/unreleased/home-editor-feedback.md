In a draft you can now change the words at the top of Home and the colour that fades over a tile's picture, and the colour swatches show the draft's own theme.

## For staff
Changed: The tile colour swatches now show the colours of the theme in your draft, in light or dark as the phone beside them, and change as you change the draft's colours. A tile with its words over a picture has a new choice of fade colour, from the same theme colours. The title at the top of Home ("Good Shepherd") can be changed, and the line under it can be today's date, your own words, or nothing. Preview controls now say they preview: *Preview the draft as*, *Preview in* and *Preview the draft on*; they never change what anyone sees.
Where: Admin panel → Drafts → a draft → Home screen. Click the top of Home on the phone for its words; choose a tile with a picture behind its words for the fade under Picture.
Try: Open a draft with your own theme and choose a tile: the swatches match the phone. Click "Good Shepherd" on the phone, change it, and choose *Your own words* for the line under it. Give a picture tile the *Pale highlight* fade.
