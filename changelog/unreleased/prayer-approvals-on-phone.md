The prayer wall team can now read and approve requests for the prayer wall on their phone, even with no signal.

## For staff
Changed: With the Prayer wall or Pastoral team role, Prayer in the app has To approve: the requests asking for the prayer wall, each with the same choices as the admin panel (the words, starting from theirs; no name unless you choose theirs; how long it stays), then Put it on the wall or Not for the wall. You can also change or take down what's on the wall. The pastoral team also sees new requests there, and can mark one followed up or answered, add a note, and call or email someone who left a number or email. Anything you do with no signal waits on the phone and goes by itself. The notification for a new wall request now opens To approve. The full inbox, with filters and history, stays in the admin panel. In the admin panel, the wall's name now starts at No name, as on the phone.
Where: Prayer → To approve in the app (with the Prayer wall or Pastoral team role).
Try: Give yourself the Prayer wall role, ask for prayer on another phone with "The pastoral team and the prayer wall", then open the notification on your phone, change a word and put it on the wall. Do one in aeroplane mode and watch it send when the signal comes back.
