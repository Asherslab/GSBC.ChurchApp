Sermon, series and speaker pictures load in the size each screen needs, and a sermon keeps the same identity wherever the app reads it from.

## For staff
Changed: Sermons now keeps each website picture once, as the website has it, and Media makes the sizes the app needs, so new sermon artwork no longer shows up twice in Media as a “large” and a “small” file. Speaker photos are kept whole instead of being cut to small squares. On Series and speakers and in Upload a sermon, a line under the picture says what the app shows, such as “The app shows a 1280×720 copy · 210 KB”, and you can remove a sermon picture's original in Media to save space without breaking anything. Behind the scenes, every sermon, series and speaker from the website now gets the same id in every copy of the app's data, so a series can't turn up twice after a server change. Follows, listening progress, downloads and links made before this all carry across.
Where: Admin panel → Sermons → Series and speakers, Upload a sermon, and Media → Made by the app → Sermon artwork.
Try: Open a series on Series and speakers and read the line under its picture. In Media, open a sermon picture and remove its original, then check the sermon still shows its picture in the app.
