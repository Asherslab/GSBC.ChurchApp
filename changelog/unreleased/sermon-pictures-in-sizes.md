Sermon, series and speaker pictures load in the size each screen needs, and links, downloads and listening progress from before all carry across.

## For staff
Changed: Sermons now keeps each website picture once, as the website has it, and Media makes the sizes the app needs, so new sermon artwork no longer shows up twice in Media as a “large” and a “small” file, and it's named after the website's own file. Speaker photos are kept whole instead of being cut to small squares. On Series and speakers and in Upload a sermon, a line under the picture says what the app shows, such as “The app shows a 1280×720 copy · 210 KB”. A large sermon picture (one with a For phones size) can lose its original in Media to save space; smaller ones keep theirs, because the app shows them full width. Sermons, series and speakers from the website now stay the same records however often they're read, so a series can't turn up twice after a server change; links, downloads, follows and listening progress from before all carry across.
Where: Admin panel → Sermons → Series and speakers, Upload a sermon, and Media → Made by the app → Sermon artwork.
Try: Open a series on Series and speakers and read the line under its picture. In Media, open a large sermon picture (one with a For phones size), remove its original, and check the sermon still shows its picture in the app.
