News posts read better, with real spacing, headings, tables and parts, and chat messages show bold, italic, lists and quotes.

## For staff
Changed: A post is now a title and one editor. The bar has a text style menu (Text, Heading 1, 2, 3), bold, italic, strikethrough, highlight, lists, quote, link and code, with superscript and subscript under ⋯. Insert holds pictures, videos and files (from Media's library or uploaded there), YouTube and Vimeo links, splitting the post into cards, a line across, a table, a code block, a footnote and emoji. Pictures, videos and files show in place in the editor with their caption typed under them; drag one by its handle, cut and paste it, add a picture beside it to make a gallery, replace it or take it out. Numbered lists now format as you type. A post without a picture at the top opens with a navy band holding its title. Chat messages render Markdown; the composer looks the same.
Where: Church news → New post (or open a post), a ministry's News → New post, and the same on the phone. Chats everywhere.
Try: Write a post with a heading, a numbered list, a highlighted phrase and a picture from Insert → Picture, type a caption under it, add === on its own line to start a second card, and look at Preview on a phone. In a chat, send **bold** and a list.
