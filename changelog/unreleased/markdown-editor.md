Posts are written in one editor that formats as you type, with headings, lists, tables, pictures and video, and chats show bold, italic, lists and quotes.

## For staff
Changed: A post is a title and one editor. Its bar has a text style menu, bold, italic, highlight, lists, quote, link and code (the rest fold into ⋯ when there isn't room); ⌘B, ⌘I and ⌘K work at a desk, and pasting from Word or Google Docs keeps bold, lists and links. Insert adds pictures, video, files, YouTube and Vimeo links, a table, a line, a footnote, emoji and === to start a new card. Pictures, videos and files show in place with their caption under them; drag any block by its ⠿ handle (or Alt ↑ and Alt ↓), and a gallery picture's ⋯ moves, replaces or takes it out. Videos show a thumbnail, and wide tables scroll sideways on a phone. In the admin panel writing a post is a full page with Write and Preview on a phone; on the phone the format bar sits on the keyboard with the post above it.
Where: New post in Church news or a ministry's News, on the phone and in the admin panel; any chat.
Try: Write a post with a heading, a list, a table and a gallery; drag the gallery by its handle and check Preview on a phone. Send **bold** in a chat.
