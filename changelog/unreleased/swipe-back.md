On any page with a Back button, swipe in from the left edge of the screen to go back, as in other iPhone apps.

## For staff
Changed: The left-edge swipe now starts reliably even when your thumb moves a little up or down, and it does exactly what the page's Back does: back to where you opened the page from, or the page's own Cancel (which still asks before losing unsaved changes).
Where: Every page in the app with Back (or Cancel) at the top left.
Try: Open a news post or an event, put your thumb at the very left edge and swipe right. Then open an editor, type something, and swipe: it should ask before discarding.
