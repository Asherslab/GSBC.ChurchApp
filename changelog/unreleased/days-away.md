If you serve on a roster, mark the days you're away on a month, for you and your children, and every ministry you serve in sees them. They can repeat weekly.

## For staff
Changed: Days away belong to the person, not a ministry. Their page has Month (tap days to add or change them) and List (every repeating rule and one-off day). A day away can be a whole day, a run of days, or every week on chosen days, all day or part of it, with an end date or until further notice; skip one day of a repeat from the month. The Roster tab shows "Your days away" with when you're next away, and leaders get "The team's days away" and can change anyone's on their team. Staff with Ministries Manager see and change them on a person's page in the admin panel.
Where: Account → Days away, or a ministry's Roster tab, on the phone. In the admin panel, People → a person → Days away.
Try: Add "Every Sunday, mornings, until further notice", then tap a Sunday on the month and say you're not away that day. Open List to see it, and check it on a ministry's Roster tab.
