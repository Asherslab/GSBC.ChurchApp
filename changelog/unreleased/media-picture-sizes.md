Every picture in Media now has smaller sizes made for phones, so a big upload never slows the app down.

## For staff
Changed: When a picture goes into Media, the library makes a size for phones (1280 wide) and a thumbnail (480 wide) by itself, and each screen in the app uses the one it needs. The library shows one tile per picture with a mark such as “3 sizes”, and the picture's details list every size with its dimensions and weight. Sermon artwork that used to appear twice, as a “large” and a “small” file, is now one picture. Wherever you place a picture (Home tiles, settings, the picture chooser), a line under it says which size is shown, such as “Shown at For phones · 1280×720 · 210 KB”. A picture with a big original is flagged “Large original”, and its details offer Remove the original to save space: its sizes stay, so everything using it keeps working. If you later upload that same original again, the library offers to put it back. Big pictures no longer get a size warning at upload; PDFs and GIFs still do.
Where: Admin panel → Media (the Large originals filter, and a picture's details), and the picture field and chooser on Home tiles and settings.
Try: Upload a large photo and open its details to see its sizes. Place it on a Home tile and read the “Shown at” line. Then remove its original, check the tile still shows it, and upload the same photo again to put the original back.
