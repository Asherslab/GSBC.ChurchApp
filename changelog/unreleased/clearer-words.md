Fields, buttons and messages are written in plain, whole sentences, and fields that weren't obvious now say what they're for.

## For staff
Changed: Labels, descriptions, buttons, empty pages and problem messages were rewritten so each one makes sense on its own. Example text in empty fields is gone ("Like 15 Nov", "Christmas 2026"); where a field needs explaining, a line under it says what it does and where people see it. Buttons name what they act on ("Delete the chat", not "Delete it"), and a Save that can't go yet says which field needs filling in. On the phone, the line at the top while it updates reads "Updating…", and "Offline" when it can't reach the church. This round covers App styling, Home and tabs, sermons, rosters and days away, people and safeguarding, feedback and roles; the calendar, ministries, news, prayer, media and chats follow.
Where: Everywhere in the app and the admin panel.
Try: Add days away on the phone, start a new draft in App styling, and upload a sermon: read each field and button and tell us anything still unclear.
