Roles are now named by feature and level, and you give them from a person's page or from the thing itself.

## For staff
Changed: Every role belongs to a part of the admin panel and says its level: Sermons Uploader and Editor, Calendar Editor, Publisher and Manager, Digest Maker, Announcements Writer and Sender, App Editor and Publisher, Media Viewer, Editor and Manager, People Viewer and Manager, Roles Manager, App Review Manager, Insights Viewer, Developer, and Owner. Each brings what its job needs from elsewhere, so an editor who adds pictures can see and upload them in Media. Choose someone in People to open their page: their roles are ticks grouped by feature, and the (i) beside each says what it lets them do. Roles for one calendar, one regular event's organisers, or a draft's reviewers are given on that thing, with its People button. The Roles and Who can do what pages are gone, and roles are no longer made here. Everyone keeps what they could do before: Account managers are now People Managers and App Review Managers, Announcers are Announcements Senders, App editors are App Publishers, and Access managers are Roles Managers.
Where: The admin panel: People → choose someone → Roles. On a calendar: its settings → People. On a regular event: Organisers, under How it runs. On a draft: Share.
Try: Open a test account in People, tick Announcements Writer, press its (i), then untick it. Open a calendar's settings, press People, add someone as an Editor and tick Publisher too.
