The outreach maps in the admin panel are now one map of every area we have, and show plainly where our maps end.

## For staff
Changed: Coverage, the places and reports maps, saved areas, the day planner and Map areas now draw every built region as one map you can pan across, so there's no Region to choose and no empty patches. Coverage opens on the church (or on the campaign's own area) and its figures count the streets on the map in view. Beyond our maps the ground is hatched with its edge drawn; zoom out past it and a note says the maps cover the map areas in Settings, with a link to Map areas if you look after them. Squares still building or switched off are labelled.
Where: Admin panel → Outreach → Coverage (and the maps on Places, Reports, Areas, a day's plan and Settings → Map areas).
Try: open Coverage, drag and zoom across the region boundaries, then zoom out past the hatched edge.
