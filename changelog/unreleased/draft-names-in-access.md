Roles given for one draft show the draft's name.

## For staff
Changed: When someone is given a role for one draft, such as previewing it on their phone, Roles and Who can do what now say which draft, for example For the draft “Christmas Home”, instead of For one draft. A renamed draft shows its new name straight away.
Where: The admin panel, Roles and Who can do what.
Try: Share a draft with someone, then open Who can do what, choose them, and read their roles.
