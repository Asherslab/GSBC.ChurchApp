Fields side by side in a form line up again, whether or not one of them has a description under its label.

## For staff
Changed: Wherever two or three fields share a line, their boxes now start level, however long a description above one of them runs. Dialogs also keep their rounded corners when you open a date's calendar, and buttons no longer change shape when you tab to them.
Where: Any two-column form: a ministry's Settings → Details, an event's place and dates, How it repeats, breaks, away times, a sermon's details, and a person's blue card and training.
Try: Open a ministry's Settings and check Name and Short description start level. Add a regular event, open How it repeats, and check First date and Last date line up, then open the Last date's calendar and watch the dialog's corners stay the same.
