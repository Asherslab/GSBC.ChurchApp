Ministries are easier to look after: one role menu per person, settings in groups, tabs in your order, and Sunday news for Life Groups.

## For staff
Changed: A person's role in a ministry is one menu (Attendee, Team, Admin, Leader, or take them off), and they stay chosen while you change it. Add to the team stays open so you can add several people, and says who you've added. Settings are in groups; the tabs a ministry uses are turned on and put in order by dragging (or with each tab's ⋯ menu). Archive and Delete are in the ministry's ⋯ menu. A Life Group no longer has Its Sundays: a linked calendar shows its events while the group's set runs, and a change to one of its Sundays (like no class) is a News post for that Sunday, shown under it in Coming up. Calendars, linked calendars and documents have a ⋯ menu for Change, Remove, Unlink and Delete, each asking once; pressing ⋯ no longer opens the calendar. Leaders can link a calendar from the phone. A parent's yes for their child shows first on the ministry's About, and leaders see when a young person asking has no parent linked yet. The Life Groups page uses the same board as Feedback, with each set's actions in its ⋯ menu.
Where: A ministry's People, Calendar, Files and Settings tabs, in the app and the admin panel; Life Groups in the admin panel.
Try: Open a Life Group's Calendar, press Tell the group about a Sunday and post No class this Sunday; see it struck through in Coming up. Drag a tab in Settings → Its tabs. In People, change someone's role and watch them stay chosen.
