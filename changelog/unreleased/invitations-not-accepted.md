People shows who you've invited and not heard from yet, and you can send their invitation again or withdraw it.

## For staff
Changed: People has a filter, Invited, not accepted, listing everyone invited who hasn't set up their sign-in, with who invited them and when the link stops working. Choose someone to send the invitation again (a new link for 7 days; the old one stops) or withdraw it. An invitation still waiting after two days, or one that expired, shows under Needs you on the admin panel's home page.
Where: The admin panel, People, and the home page's Needs you.
Try: Invite yourself at a second email address, open People and choose Invited, not accepted, then send it again and check that only the newest link works.
