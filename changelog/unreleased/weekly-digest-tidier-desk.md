The weekly digest's list keeps every date in one tidy column, and its calendar choices are quieter.

## For staff
Changed: Each item's date and time sit in their own column that wraps instead of running past the list, with the move arrows always beside it. From, How long and the dates it covers line up together, and the calendars are small ticked chips under a Calendars label. Moving an item with its arrows from the keyboard keeps you on that item, and the arrow keys change How long.
Where: The admin panel, Weekly digest.
Try: Choose Month and look at an item with two dates, such as Outreach Saturday. Untick a calendar, then tick it again. Press Tab to an item's down arrow and press Enter twice.
