See how many phones opened the app this week, whether that's growing, and what's popular, in plain totals.

## For staff
Changed: Insights shows how many phones opened the app this week and this month, how many are new, the last 12 weeks as a line, and the most tapped Home screen tiles. Counts are of phones, never people, and anything below 5 shows as "fewer than 5". Only people given the Insights viewer role see it.
Where: The admin panel → Insights.
Try: Open the app, tap a Home screen tile, then put the app in the background. After about an hour (or the next day), open Insights in the admin panel and look for your phone in this week's number.
