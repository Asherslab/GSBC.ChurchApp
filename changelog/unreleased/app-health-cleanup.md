Admin pages that stopped working when the server said no now say why, and App health lists only real failed calls.

## For staff
Changed: Putting a sermon in its suggested series shows the server's reason (such as a series already having that name) instead of "This page stopped working". A person's page, their roles and what they can do, and the roles tool no longer report a crash when your sign-in has run out. App health's failed calls now list only calls that actually failed (the server erroring or not answering), not answers like "that draft was deleted", "that code isn't right" or "sign in first".
Where: Sermons (checking a sermon), People → a person and their roles, and Technical → App health, in the admin panel.
Try: Open App health and check the failed calls are only ones worth a look.
