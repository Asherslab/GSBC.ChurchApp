Ministries now have group chats and leaders-only channels, under Chats and on each ministry's Chats tab.

## For staff
Changed: Leaders start a chat for the team, for attendees and their parents, or for parents only, as a group chat or a channel only leaders post in. Members send photos and files, reply, react, mention someone with @, and see who has read their last message; edits and deletes show in the chat, and the church keeps every version. A young person only gets a chat once a parent says yes to the leader's first message, and parents can read their child's chats and write for them. Chats with young people are flagged until two adults in them hold a current blue card. Anyone can report a message to the ministry's leaders or to the church's chat auditors (who are told urgently and can pause someone from chats). Asking to join a ministry now starts a chat with its leaders, who add the person from that chat. A ministry can turn Chats off in Settings → What it uses.
Where: Chats on the Hub and in More; a ministry's Chats tab; Settings → What it uses → Chats. In the admin panel: Chats (reports, every chat you may check, paused people and the checks log). Give the *Chat auditor* role to the staff and pastors who need it.
Try: As a leader, open a ministry's Chats tab and start a chat for the team. Send a message, reply to it and react. In the admin panel, open Chats → Chats, choose the chat, say why you're reading it and read it, then look at the Checks log.
