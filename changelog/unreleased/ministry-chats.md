Ministries have group chats and leaders-only channels, on your phone and, for leaders and staff, on a computer too.

## For staff
Changed: Leaders start a chat for the team, for attendees and their parents, or for parents only, as a group chat or a channel only leaders post in (a ministry can let its team start chats too). Members send photos and files, reply (tap a reply's quote to jump to the message), react, mention someone with @ and see who has read their last message; photos open full screen. A young person only gets a chat once a parent says yes, and parents can read their child's chats. Chats with young people are flagged until two adults in them hold a current blue card. Anyone can report a message to the leaders or to the church's chat auditors, who can pause someone. Asking to join a ministry starts a chat with its leaders. The admin panel has Chats for your own chats (Enter sends, pictures paste or drop in), and Chat safety for reports, checking a chat on a page of its own with search and filters, paused people and the checks log. Give the *Chat auditor* role to those who need it.
Where: Chats in the app and a ministry's Chats tab; Chats and Safety → Chat safety in the admin panel.
Try: As a leader, start a team chat, send a photo, reply and react. Reply from Chats in the admin panel, then check the chat in Chat safety.
