Life Groups now lists every group, whichever ministry it belongs to, and shows the ones you and your children are in first.

## For staff
Changed: being a Life Group is now a switch on an ordinary ministry, *Show in Life Groups*. Any ministry can have it, top-level or under another ministry, and it keeps every other ministry setting. Life Groups in the admin panel lists every ministry with the switch on, says where each one sits ("Under Kids Ministries"), and offers *Add a Life Group*. A group's room is now its *When and where it meets*, and *Join as a parent* is gone because parents join for their children in every ministry. Planning the Sundays (*Plan the next groups*) takes a From and To date range, adds every Sunday in it, and lets you take any off; it is for staff and the new Life Groups planner role, not for whoever leads a Life Groups ministry.
Where: admin panel → People → Life Groups; any ministry's Settings → Life Groups; *A new ministry*.
Try: Open Life Groups in the admin panel and check Kids Life is under *All year*, marked "Under Kids Ministries". Open a ministry's Settings, switch on *Show in Life Groups*, save, and check it appears on Life Groups in the app.
