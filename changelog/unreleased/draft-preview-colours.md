Previewing a draft on your phone now shows its colours, and Previews lists only drafts that still exist.

## For staff
Changed: A theme tried on your phone under More → Appearance used to stay on top of everything, so a draft's colours didn't show while you previewed it. Previewing a draft now always shows the draft's colours, and Appearance says so while you're previewing. Drafts removed from the database no longer linger in More → Previews.
Where: The app, More → Previews and More → Appearance.
Try: Choose a draft with different colours under More → Previews and look at Home.
