The church website can show the app's events and sermons, so they're kept in one place.

## For staff
Changed: Pages for the website's Events and Sermons, in the website's own look, read straight from the app: what's on week by week, each event with its details, Register and add to a calendar; the latest sermon, search, series, speakers and each sermon with its player. Anything changed in the app shows on the website within a minute. Day-to-day events (Sunday services) appear once under Regular gatherings; a regular event's new switch, List every date on the website, lists all its dates (Outreach). On a computer they use the full width of the website's page, with sermons as a grid beside search, series and speakers.
Where: On the church website, once its Events and Sermons pages show them (paste the embed from docs/features/website.md into a page's source code). Until then, in development at dev-embed.baptist.com.au.
Try: Turn on List every date on the website for a regular event (its page, How it runs) and see each date listed. Open the Events page, choose a calendar such as Kids, and open an event. Open a sermon and play it, then share it: the link opens the same sermon on the website.
