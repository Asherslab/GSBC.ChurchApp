A round of small fixes: a changed event stands out cleanly in dark and keeps off the screen's edge, rows that scroll sideways show there's more, and text no longer leaves one word alone on a line.

## For staff
Changed: On the phone, Outreach's *How long* choices show their track, the end-of-walk buttons are the same height, and a changed event's band is a clean warm tint in dark. In the admin panel, date and time fields line up with the fields beside them, the save bar no longer jumps when "You have unsaved changes." or "Saved" appears, the dark backdrop behind a dialog is deeper, page tabs stay on one line and scroll, and Plan a day's route list fits at 1024px wide with each route's letterboxes and time under its name.
Where: Outreach on the phone; the Calendar's week; any form with Cancel and Save; Outreach → Days and Outreach → Map → Plan a day in the admin panel.
Try: In the admin panel, open an outreach day under Outreach → Days, change its end time and watch the foot stay put. Make the window narrow and check the page tabs scroll with a sign at the edge. In dark, open a Delete dialog and check it stands off the page.
