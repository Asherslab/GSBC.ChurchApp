Small look fixes: changed events read cleanly in dark, rows that scroll sideways fade at the edge, and text no longer leaves one word alone on a line.

## For staff
Changed: On the phone, Outreach's *How long* choices show their track, the end-of-walk buttons are the same height, and a changed event's band is a clean warm tint in dark. In the admin panel, date and time fields line up with the fields beside them, the save bar no longer jumps when "You have unsaved changes." or "Saved" appears, the dark backdrop behind a dialog is deeper, page tabs stay on one line and scroll, fading at an edge where there's more as the Calendar's chips do on the phone, and Plan a day's route list fits at 1024px wide with each route's letterboxes and time under its name.
Where: Outreach on the phone; the Calendar's week; any form with Cancel and Save; Outreach → Days and Outreach → Map → Plan a day in the admin panel.
Try: In the admin panel, open an outreach day under Outreach → Days, change its end time and watch the foot stay put. Make the window narrow and check the page tabs fade at the edge where there's more. In dark, open a Delete dialog and check it stands off the page.
