Leaders and staff can now read and reply to their chats from a computer, in the admin panel, with everything their phone has.

## For staff
Changed: The admin panel has Chats for your own chats: every chat you're in down the left (search, unread counts, the ministry each belongs to), the conversation in the middle, and the chat's settings and people on the right. Enter sends, Shift and Enter starts a new line, and pictures paste or drop in. Replies, reactions, editing and deleting your own messages work as on the phone, and the same rules apply. The old Chats desk is now called Chat safety and sits in the Safety area; reports, checking a chat and paused people are all still there.
Where: Admin panel → Chats (near Home), and Safety → Chat safety. A ministry's Chats tab in the admin panel lists its chats too.
Try: Open Chats in the admin panel, pick a chat, type a reply and press Enter; it shows on your phone a moment later. Then open Chat safety and check the Reports tab is where it was.
