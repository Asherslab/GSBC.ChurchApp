The admin panel's Account menu shows in full, and the Calendar's buttons no longer squeeze together.

## For staff
Changed: The Account menu at the foot of the sidebar opens over the page instead of being cut off at the sidebar's edge (Safari showed only part of Light or dark). On the Calendar, the Agenda, Month and Regular events switch and the buttons beside it keep their size and move under the title when there isn't room, instead of clipping.
Where: Account at the foot of the admin sidebar; the Calendar page with a calendar chosen and an event open.
Try: Open Account in Safari and choose Dark; on Calendar choose Services, open an event, and check the toolbar above the list.
