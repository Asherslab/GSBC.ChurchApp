Tiles with a picture now show the › or ↗ in their corner, like the other tiles, so they read as buttons.

## For staff
Changed: A tile with a picture behind it or above it shows a small round mark in its top-right corner (› opens something in the app, ↗ opens a web page), shaded so it reads on any picture; a picture beside the words shows the mark beside them. Tiles that already say where they go ("Read the bulletin ›") or have a play button don't get a second one.
Where: The app's Home, and the phone preview in Admin panel → Drafts → Home screen.
Try: Give a tile a picture and choose Picture behind, then look at its corner in light and dark.
