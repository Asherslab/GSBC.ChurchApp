Life Groups in the admin panel shows each set of Sundays by where it is, and groups that are done can be archived instead of deleted.

## For staff
Changed: the Life Groups page is now four columns: Being planned, Shown in the app, Now on and Finished. Each set of Sundays sits in one, with its groups, any group that still needs where it meets, and any all-year group that's off for it. Finished sets fold to one line each, by year, and can archive their groups in one go. All-year groups sit in a strip at the top. Any ministry can now be archived from its Settings: it leaves every list and the app and nothing more can be posted, but its people, news, files and calendars are kept, and Restore brings it back as it was.
Where: admin panel → People → Life Groups; any ministry's Settings → Archive; Life Groups → Archived groups.
Try: Open Life Groups and find the set being planned. Archive a test ministry from its Settings, check it's gone from Life Groups, then restore it from Archived groups.
