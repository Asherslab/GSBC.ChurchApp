If you serve on a roster, the days you're away are now on your Account page too, for you and your children, and every ministry you serve in sees them.

## For staff
Changed: Days away belong to the person, not a ministry. They show on Account (for anyone on a team with a roster, and their children), still on each Roster tab, and on a person's page in the admin panel (*Days away* tab) for staff with Ministries Manager, who can add or remove them for that person.
Where: Account in the app; the Roster tab of a ministry; admin panel → a person → Days away.
Try: Add days away from Account, open a ministry's Roster tab and see them there. In the admin panel open the same person and remove them from their Days away tab.
