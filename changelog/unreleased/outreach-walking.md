Got an hour? Open Outreach and the app plans a loop of streets the church hasn't letterboxed lately, then marks them as you walk with your phone in your pocket.

## For staff
Changed: The phone has Outreach. Anyone can plan a walk from their door (or from a street they find or a pin they drop) for the campaigns they carry; the plan says how far, how many letterboxes and bundles, when they'll be back, and why a walk stops short, with the two ways to get more. While walking, the street and side lead in big type with the next turn, the map follows them, and they can mark No junk mail, a conversation, a place that isn't a home (with a note and a photo, for review), or a bit they skipped. At the end they correct what the app saw and save. Doorknocking runs in pairs and in daylight, with no answer, a conversation, a follow-up (only with consent) or "wants nothing to do with it" at each door. Outreach days list their routes with who's taking each; a route's QR link opens it ready to start. My outreach shows each person's walks and the "count me by name" switch, and Offline maps keeps a region's map on the phone. The Outreach tile on Home goes live while an outreach day is on.
Where: The app → Outreach (add the Outreach tile to Home, or Outreach to the tab bar, in App styling).
Try: Outside, open Outreach, pick 30 min and Plan my walk, then Start the walk and walk a street with the phone in your pocket. Check it marked the street, tap Finish, untick a street you didn't do and save. Then open My outreach.
