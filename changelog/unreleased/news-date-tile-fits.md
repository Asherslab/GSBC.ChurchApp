## For staff
Changed: the date block beside a news post's title showed "Yesterday", which is too wide for it and ran into the title. It now shows the weekday for yesterday's posts; the line at the foot still says "Posted yesterday".
Where: any news post, in the app and the admin panel.
Try: Open a post from yesterday and check the date sits inside its box.
