Dates and times in the admin panel can be typed in plain words or picked from a calendar.

## For staff
Changed: Every date and time field in the admin panel is now the app's own, in place of the browser's picker. Type "15 Nov", "next sun" or "7:30pm" and the line under the field says it back in full; or click the calendar or clock at the field's right end to pick from a month or a list of times. An event's end can no longer be set before its start on the same day. The phone keeps its own date and time wheel.
Where: Anywhere in the admin panel that asks for a date or a time: an event, a break, the weekly digest, a person's birth date, a sermon upload.
Try: Open the Calendar, add an event, type "next sun" in its date and "7pm" in Starts, then open the clock and choose an end time.
