When something goes wrong, the app lets the church know by itself, with nothing about you. You can turn this off in More.

## For staff
Changed: If the app hits an error or crashes, it now sends a report to the church on its own, so there's no need to send screenshots. Reports say what failed and on which version of the app, never who you are or what you typed. They wait if you have no signal and are never shown in Storage.
Where: More → Help improve the app.
Try: Open Help improve the app and read what's sent. Turn the switch off, completely close the app, open it again, and check the switch is still off. Turn it back on.
