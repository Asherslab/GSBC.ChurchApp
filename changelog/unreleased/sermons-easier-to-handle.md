## For staff
Changed: On the phone, swipe the small player at the bottom sideways or down to tuck it away; the sermon keeps playing and the lock screen controls stay. It comes back when you press play again, start another sermon, or open that sermon's page; looking at other sermons leaves it tucked away. Pull the full player (and any sheet in the app) down by its top to close it, the way iPhone sheets work. Sermon pictures now show the whole graphic instead of a square crop, and on a sermon's page the back link and the title no longer run together.
In the admin panel: Series and Speaker on a sermon are now searchable pickers; type a few letters to find one. Add series and speakers from the picker, the upload form, or Series and speakers. Series and speakers each have a search box, you can add a speaker, and you can give a speaker a photo. The "No series" filter is gone (most sermons have none, and that's fine), and rows are no longer tinted for no reason. Uploaded sermon audio is kept once in Media, in a Sermon audio folder, so you can see how much space it takes.
Where: The Sermons tab on the phone; Sermons, Series and speakers, and Media in the admin panel.
Try: Play a sermon, swipe the player away, lock the phone and use the lock screen controls, then open a different sermon (the player stays away) and then the one playing (it comes back). Open the player and pull it down. In the admin panel, open a sermon and type in Series. Open Media → Made by the app → Sermon audio and click a file.
