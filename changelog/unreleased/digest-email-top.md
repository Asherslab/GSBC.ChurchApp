The weekly digest's email can leave out its title, and show just the dates, under a top photo that already says What's Next.

## For staff
Changed: With a photo across the top of the email, you choose what goes under it: the title and the dates, just the dates as a dateline, or nothing.
Where: Admin panel → Weekly digest → Email → Top of the email.
Try: Choose your What's Next banner as the photo, pick Just the dates, then Copy for email.
