You can make a folder while choosing a picture, and upload straight into it.

## For staff
Changed: Choose a picture has a New folder button beside Upload, the same as on the Media page. The new folder goes in the folder you have open and opens, so an upload lands in it. A folder that holds only folders now says so, instead of "No pictures yet". The digest's top photo says what the email shows, not the app.
Where: Anywhere you choose a picture in the admin panel, such as Sermons → Series and speakers, or the Weekly digest's Email tab.
Try: Edit a series, choose a picture, tap New folder, name it and upload a picture into it. Then open Media and check the folder and picture are there.
