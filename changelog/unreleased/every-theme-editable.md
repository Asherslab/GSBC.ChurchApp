The church's brand and the 2026 theme are now themes like any other, and any theme can be copied.

## For staff
Changed: The church's brand and the 2026 theme are ordinary themes: change their name, fonts and colours, or delete them. Every theme card has Change it, Copy and Delete. Copy makes "<name> (copy)" in your draft to change. The theme everyone sees can't be deleted until another is chosen for everyone.
Where: Admin panel → App styling → Colours, in a draft.
Try: Open a draft, copy the 2026 theme, change its masthead colour, and choose it.
