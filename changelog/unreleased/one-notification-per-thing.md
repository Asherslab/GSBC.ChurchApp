A new sermon reaches you once, not twice, and what you turn off in Notification settings stays out of the bell too.

## For staff
Changed: When more than one reason applies to you, you get one notification, in the inbox and on your lock screen, and it's the most specific one. Following a series and all sermons gives you "New in Summer Fit: …"; following only all sermons gives you "New sermon: …". If you turn off a series but still follow all sermons, you get "New sermon: …" instead. If you turn off every reason, you get nothing: a kind that's turned off no longer lands in the bell either. What's already in the bell stays. Account safety notices can't be turned off and always arrive, and a test announcement you send yourself always reaches you. The same goes for Tell people on an event shown to several roles: someone in two of them hears once.
Where: The bell on the phone, the lock screen, and More → Notification settings.
Try: Follow a series and turn on Tell me when a new sermon is out on the Sermons tab. When the next sermon in that series is out, the bell shows one row and your phone buzzes once. Then turn off A new sermon is out in Notification settings: the next one doesn't appear in the bell at all.
