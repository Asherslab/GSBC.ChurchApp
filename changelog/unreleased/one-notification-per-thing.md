If you follow a series and every new sermon, a new sermon in that series now reaches you once, not twice.

## For staff
Changed: When more than one reason applies to you, you get one notification, in the inbox and on your lock screen, and it's the most specific one. Following a series and all sermons gives you "New in Summer Fit: …"; following only all sermons gives you "New sermon: …". If you turn off pushes for a series but still follow all sermons, you get "New sermon: …" instead. The same goes for Tell people on an event shown to several roles: someone in two of them hears once.
Where: The bell on the phone, and the lock screen.
Try: Follow a series and turn on Tell me when a new sermon is out on the Sermons tab. When the next sermon in that series is out, the bell shows one row and your phone buzzes once.
