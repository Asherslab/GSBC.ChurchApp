Each ministry now has at most two calendars, one for everyone and one for its team, so the calendar list stays short.

## For staff
Changed: Instead of adding calendars one by one, a ministry has a Public calendar (everyone sees its events) and a Team calendar (only its team does), each turned on or off in its Settings. They're named for the ministry ("Impact Kids", "The Impact Kids Team") and follow it if it's renamed. Turning one off deletes it and its events, after saying how many go. A ministry's extra calendars were merged: events for everyone, attendees or parents are now in its Public calendar, and team events in its Team calendar. If you subscribed to one of the merged calendars in another calendar app, subscribe to the Public or Team calendar instead.
Where: A ministry's Settings → Calendars, in the app and the admin panel. Link a calendar is still on its Calendar tab.
Try: Open a ministry's Settings, turn on its Team calendar and save, then add an event to it from Calendar. Turn it off again and check Save says which events go first.
