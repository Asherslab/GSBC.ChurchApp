Someone under 18 who asks to join a ministry's team now waits for a parent to say yes, and the team's leaders hear about it. Coming along needs no parent's yes.

## For staff
Changed: A young person tapping Join for a team asks a parent first. Their parents get "Sam asks to join Production's team" and answer on the ministry's About (*Waiting for your yes*: Yes or Not yet). The leaders are told straight away, and again when a parent says yes (on an open team they're then on it; on an Ask team the leaders say yes next). Leaders can't say yes before a parent does; the ask says "Under 18: waiting for a parent's yes". A parent joining their child up counts as the parent's yes.
Where: A ministry's Join sheet; its About for parents; People for leaders.
Try: Sign in as a young person (a test account under 18), join a team, then as their parent open the ministry and say yes. Check the leader's notifications.
