Arrange the app's Home on a phone in the admin panel: add tiles, drag them into place, resize them and recolour them from the theme.

## For staff
Changed: In a draft, Home screen shows a phone you arrange directly. Drag a tile to move it, or drag its corner to make it wider or taller; with the keyboard, Tab to a tile, then the arrows move it and Shift with the arrows resizes it. Click a tile to set its colour (always one of the theme's, with its text colour paired for you), icon, words, picture, where it opens, who sees it (everyone, people signed in, or people in a role) and the days it shows. Upload your own SVG icon: it's cleaned, drawn in the theme's colours, and shown in light and dark before you use it. Columns sets how many columns Home has, and Show it as previews it for a visitor, someone signed in, someone in a role, or on a chosen day.
Where: Admin panel → Drafts → your draft → Home screen.
Try: Start a draft, add a Plain tile, make it two columns wide and navy, give it an uploaded icon and a link, then preview the draft on your phone under More → Previews and publish it.
