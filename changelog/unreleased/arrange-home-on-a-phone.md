Arrange the app's Home on a phone in the admin panel: add tiles, drag them into place, resize them and recolour them from the theme.

## For staff
Changed: In a draft, Home screen shows a phone you arrange directly. Drag a tile and the others slide aside to make room, as on an iPhone's home screen: the gap is where it will land, a small tile held over a big one makes the big one give way, holding near the top or bottom scrolls the phone, and the picture depends only on where you're holding the tile, so wandering off and back puts everything where it was. Let go to settle it there, or press Escape to put it back; what you see when you let go is exactly what's saved, and a drag that's interrupted (the window losing focus, a right click) leaves the phone tidy. Drag its corner to make it wider or taller; with the keyboard, Tab to a tile, then the arrows move it and Shift with the arrows resizes it. Click a tile to set its colour (always one of the theme's, with its text colour paired for you), icon, words, picture, where it opens, who sees it (everyone, people signed in, or people in one or more roles, found by typing) and the days it shows. Upload your own SVG icon: it's cleaned, drawn in the theme's colours, and shown in light and dark before you use it. Every tile has a layout designed for each size (Layout at this size: Automatic, or another that suits it). Save a tile to the library to add it again later as a new copy, or Make a copy of it. Columns sets how many columns Home has, Show it as previews it for a visitor, someone signed in, someone in a role, or on a chosen day, and Preview switches the phone between light and dark. Choosing a picture, here and anywhere else in the admin panel, opens a picture sheet: search, upload, click a picture and Choose.
Where: Admin panel → Drafts → your draft → Home screen.
Try: Start a draft, add a Link tile, make it two columns wide and navy, give it an uploaded icon, a picture and a link, show it only to two roles, try its layouts, save it to the library and add it again, then preview the draft on your phone under More → Previews and publish it.
