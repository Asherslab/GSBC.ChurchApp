On settings pages the sand background now runs all the way down to the tab bar, with no white strip under the last group.

## For staff
Changed: Every page made of settings groups (a ministry's Settings, Account, Security, Notifications, a chat's settings, a prayer request) keeps its sand background behind the last group, down to the tab bar.
Where: The app, scrolled to the bottom of any of those pages.
Try: Open Kids Ministries → Settings and scroll to the bottom: the sand reaches the tab bar.
