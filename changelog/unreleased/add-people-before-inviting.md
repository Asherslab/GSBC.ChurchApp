Staff can add someone to the app without emailing them, set them up, and send everyone's invitations together when the app is ready to announce.

## For staff
Changed: Add someone (it was Invite someone) has a second button, Add without inviting. The person gets an account straight away, so you can give them roles in Who can do what, make them a ministry leader or team member and put them in a family, but they get no email or notification until their invitation is sent. If they sign up themselves with the same email, the app emails them their invitation link instead of making a second account.
Where: People & access → People → Add someone. People added this way are under the Not invited yet filter, which has a button to send all their invitations. Each person's Profile tab has Send the invitation.
Try: Add a test person without inviting them, make them a ministry leader, then open Not invited yet and send the invitations. Check they're under Invited afterwards.
