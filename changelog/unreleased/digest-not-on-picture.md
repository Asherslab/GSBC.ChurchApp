A date that isn't on now shows its picture in the What's Next email, unless you take it off.

## For staff
Changed: The digest email always left out the picture of a date that isn't on (a break, a cancelled week), whatever you chose. It now keeps the picture the item has, and you can change or remove it like any other item's, including choosing beside the words or across the top.
Where: Admin panel → Weekly digest, open a date that isn't on.
Try: Keep or choose a picture on a date that isn't on and check the Email preview; remove it and check it goes.
