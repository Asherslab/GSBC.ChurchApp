Each calendar in the admin panel opens on its own page with tabs, like a ministry, and the church's calendars can be put in the order everyone sees.

## For staff
Changed: Choosing a calendar on the Calendar page shows its tabs: Events, Regular events, People and Settings. Its settings (name, who it's for, publishing, copying from iCal, the link for calendar apps) moved from the panel beside the list to the Settings tab; who's on it is listed on People, where you add someone or change their role; Remove this calendar is in the ⋯ menu by its name. Calendar Managers can drag a calendar up or down the list by the handle that appears beside it (or press Alt and an arrow key on the handle); the order is the one phones show in Browse calendars and the Calendar tab. New calendar opens its settings in the middle of the page.
Where: The admin panel, Calendar.
Try: Choose Services and open each tab. Drag Life Groups to the top, then check Browse calendars on a phone.
