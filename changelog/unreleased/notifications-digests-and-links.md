No more notifications about what you did yourself, prayers for your request come as a summary, and every notification opens its page.

## For staff
Changed: Nobody is notified about something they caused: a post, a message, a report, an ask, an announcement or feedback they sent. "Someone prayed for your request" comes 15, 30 and 45 minutes and 1, 3 and 6 hours after a request goes on the prayer wall, then once a day at midday, each saying how many prayed since the last, and nothing when nobody did; the pastoral team can change those times. New feedback comes once a day at midday when there is any, saying how many and opening the board, instead of one waiting notification per report. Notifications for a new prayer request, prayer requests to approve, a chat, a ministry's news, people or roster, and your own prayer request now open their page instead of "There's nothing here". A push about work done in the admin panel opens the notifications list on the phone.
Where: Notifications on the phone; the Prayer page in the admin panel for the prayer times; Notification settings to turn any of them off.
Try: Post an important ministry notice and check you aren't told about it but another leader is. Pray for someone's request on the wall and check they hear about it 15 minutes after it went up. Tap "A new prayer request" and check it opens the request.
