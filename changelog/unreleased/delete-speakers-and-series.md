Staff can delete a speaker or a series, and an uploaded sermon once it's hidden.

## For staff
Changed: A speaker or a series can be deleted. If it has sermons, you choose whether they move to another speaker or series or are left without one, and the dialog says what happens, including to later sermons from the website and to anyone following the series. An uploaded sermon can be deleted for good once it's hidden from the app; its audio and everyone's listening progress go with it. Sermons from the website can still only be hidden.
Where: Admin panel → Sermons → Series and speakers: choose one, then Delete this speaker… or Delete this series… at the bottom. For an uploaded sermon: Sermons → Hidden, choose it, then Delete for good….
Try: Delete a speaker with a few sermons and move them to someone else, then check those sermons show the new speaker on your phone. Delete a series and leave its sermons out, then check it's gone from Listen.
