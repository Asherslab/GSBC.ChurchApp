Your request shows its steps. Asking starts with the prayer wall, and the wall shows your name as "Sam S." unless you choose No name.

## For staff
Changed: A request's page on the phone opens with a band saying who sent it and when, and the pastoral care steps (New, Followed up, Answered) as the same step track as feedback tickets: tap a step to move it there, back too. The prayer wall, Getting in touch and Notes are each their own group, with Save bottom right and Not for the wall beside Cancel. Names on the wall are first name and last initial. On the admin panel, the Prayer desk is a workbench like People: filters and search on the left, the requests as a table, the request opening beside it with the same steps; Delete is in its ⋯ menu, and so is deleting a note.
Where: Prayer → To approve → a request, on the phone. People → Prayer, in the admin panel.
Try: Ask for prayer on the phone and see the wall is chosen and your name shows as "First L.". Open the request as staff, move it to Followed up and back to New, and put it on the wall. In the admin panel, open a request and delete a note from its ⋯ menu.
