Chat messages are snug again: each bubble fits its words, without an empty line above them.

## For staff
Changed: Every chat message had a blank line above its words, so bubbles were nearly three times as tall as they needed to be and a conversation took far more scrolling. Now each bubble is just tall enough for the name, the message and the time.
Where: Any chat, on the phone (Ministries → a ministry → Chats, or More → Chats) and in the admin panel's Chats.
Try: Open a chat with a few short messages; each one-line message sits in a one-line bubble.
