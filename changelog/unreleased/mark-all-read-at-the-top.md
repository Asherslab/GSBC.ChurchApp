Mark all as read now sits at the top of your notifications, so you no longer scroll to the end for it.

## For staff
Changed: Mark all as read was under the whole list, so with many notifications you had to scroll to the end to find it. It now sits at the top of the list, beside its heading, and shows only while something is unread. The orange dots also go the moment you tap it, where before they stayed until you left the page.
Where: The bell at the top of the phone's tabs (Everything, when there is also a Waiting list).
Try: With a few unread notifications, open the bell (choose Everything if it asks) and tap Mark all as read at the top; the orange dots and the button go.
