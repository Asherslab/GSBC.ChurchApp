The admin panel's sidebar keeps the pages you pin at the top and folds the rest into areas, and Jump to finds any page by name.

## For staff
Changed: The sidebar is grouped into areas by job: Sundays & sermons, This week (Church news, Calendar, Prayer, Weekly digest), Ministries & groups (Ministries, Life Groups), Safety (Safeguarding, Safe Ministry Check, Chat safety), People & access (People, App review), The app and Technical; Home and Chats sit above them. Each area folds and stays as you left it on that computer, and shows an orange count only when something inside it is waiting for you. Pin the pages you use most and they sit at the top on any computer. Jump to (⌘K on a Mac, Ctrl K elsewhere) opens any page by typing part of its name. On a narrow window the sidebar folds into a Menu button.
Where: The admin panel's sidebar, on the left.
Try: Pin a page with the pin beside it. Fold an area you don't use. Press ⌘K, type "digest" and press Return.
