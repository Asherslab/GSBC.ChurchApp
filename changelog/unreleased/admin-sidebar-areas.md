The admin panel's sidebar keeps the pages you pin at the top and folds the rest into areas, and Jump to finds any page by name.

## For staff
Changed: The sidebar is grouped into areas by job: Sundays & sermons, Calendar & news, The app and People. Each area folds and unfolds and stays as you left it on that computer; a folded area shows how many things are waiting inside it, and the area you're in opens by itself. Pin the pages you use most and they sit at the top on any computer you sign in on. Jump to (⌘K on a Mac, Ctrl K elsewhere) opens any page you can use by typing part of its name. On a narrow window the sidebar folds into a Menu button.
Where: The admin panel's sidebar, on the left.
Try: Point at a page in the sidebar and choose the pin beside it to pin it. Fold an area you don't use. Press ⌘K, type "digest" and press Return.
