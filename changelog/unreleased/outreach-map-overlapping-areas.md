The Outreach map no longer hatches over streets that have a map where two map areas overlap.

## For staff
Changed: Where two map areas overlap (Around the church and South Brisbane), every built region's map shows. Squares that are switched off, waiting or have no homes are marked only where no area has a map. Zoomed out, the edge of the mapped area is one clean line, with no diagonal cut out west. The streets count on Coverage counts each street once. A new map area now lines up with the existing areas' regions and leaves out regions another area already has, so no street is built or downloaded twice.
Where: Admin panel → Outreach → Map → Coverage, and Map → Map areas → Add a map area.
Try: Zoom right out, then pan from Redcliffe down past Bulimba and Coorparoo to Annerley and Algester. Every suburb in an area should show its streets, and hatching should appear only outside every area. Then start adding a map area at Carindale: its regions line up with Around the church's and skip the ones it already has.
