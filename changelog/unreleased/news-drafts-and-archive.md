A church news post can be saved as a draft before it's published, and a post you take down is archived, so it can be put back.

## For staff
Changed: Next to the main button when you write or change a post that isn't published there's now Save as a draft. It keeps the post under Being written on the Church news desk, where nobody but the people who write church news can see it. A post's ⋯ menu has Archive instead of Delete: it takes the post out of everyone's News and takes back its notification (a published post asks first), but keeps it under a new Archived group. From there, Put it back brings it back as a draft (it doesn't notify anyone again, even when it's published again), and Delete it for good removes it.
Where: Church news in the admin panel (New post, the ⋯ on a post and the Archived group), and the News desk on the phone.
Try: Write a post and press Save as a draft, then find it under Being written. Archive a published post and check it's gone from News, then put it back from Archived and publish it again.
