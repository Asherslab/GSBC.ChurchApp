Home can show the church's news: the newest post with its picture, how many posts are new since you last looked, or the latest headlines. Tap one to read it.

## For staff
Changed: a News tile you can add to Home. It has three layouts: Front page (the newest post with its picture; at 2 × 2 the one before it under a line), What's new (how many posts are new since someone last opened News, and the newest) and Headlines (the newest headlines with when each was posted). A post marked urgent today takes the tile over in every layout.
Where: Admin panel → App styling → Home screen → Add a tile → News; its Layout setting picks Front page, What's new or Headlines.
Try: Add the News tile in a draft, resize it and switch its layout to see each one on the phone, then publish. Post an urgent church news item and watch the tile turn to it for the day.
