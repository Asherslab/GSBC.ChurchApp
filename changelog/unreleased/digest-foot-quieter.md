The Weekly digest no longer repeats how many items it puts on What's next beside the send button.

## For staff
Changed: The line beside Send said "Puts 3 items on What's next. Nobody is notified again." It now says nothing when sending again without notifying, and only who is notified when someone will be.
Where: Admin panel → Weekly digest, at the bottom.
Try: Send a digest, then change it and look at the line beside Send again without notifying.
