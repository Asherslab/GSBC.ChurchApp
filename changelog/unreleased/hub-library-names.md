Saved tiles get their own names, one tile per name, and are easy to rename or take out.

## For staff
Changed: Save to the library now asks for the tile's name in the library, filled in from its title; only staff see it, and the tile keeps its own title on the phone. If the name is already taken, choose Replace to update the saved tile, or Save as a new name. In Add a tile (and in the panel when no tile is chosen), Manage beside Your library lets you rename saved tiles or take them out, with Undo.
Where: Admin panel → Drafts → your draft → Home screen → a tile → Save to the library; and Add a tile → Your library → Manage.
Try: Save a tile as "Ministries (wide, picture)", save another tile under the same name and choose Replace, then rename one under Manage and take one out and Undo it.
