Sermon pictures are now kept once, however many sermons share them.

## For staff
Changed: When many sermons, or a whole series, use the same picture, the app makes and stores it once. The pictures the app makes from the website's artwork now show in Media in a Sermon artwork folder the app keeps for itself (for staff who can edit sermons); they can't be deleted or moved there.
Where: Media in the admin panel, Sermon artwork folder.
Try: Open Media and look in Sermon artwork: there's one picture for each distinct sermon graphic, not one per sermon.
