The admin panel's sidebar no longer shows how many pages each area has.

## For staff
Changed: An area's name in the sidebar has a number beside it only when something inside it is waiting for you (the orange count, for example Safety's checks). The grey page counts are gone.
Where: Admin panel → the sidebar on the left.
Try: Fold an area with something waiting (such as Safety) and check its orange count lines up at the end of the row; the other areas show just their names.
