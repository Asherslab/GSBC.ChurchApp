Double-tapping or pinching no longer zooms the app in. To make text bigger, change your phone's text size: the app follows it.

## For staff
Changed: The app can't be zoomed with a double tap or a pinch any more, as a native app can't; double-tapping a calendar chip used to zoom the page in. Text size follows the phone's own setting (Settings → Display & Brightness → Text Size on an iPhone), as it already did. The admin panel still zooms as any website does.
Where: Anywhere in the app.
Try: Double-tap a calendar chip on the Month view: nothing zooms. Then make your phone's text larger and reopen the app.
