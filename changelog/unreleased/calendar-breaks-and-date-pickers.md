Every date in a break now shows as not on, not just its first two weeks, and an event's page is tidier.

## For staff
Changed: A break marks every date it covers as not on, with its name and when it's back. An event's page no longer lists the regular event's other dates, and a date that's not on shows its not-on note once instead of a second "Changed · Not on" box. An event whose end time isn't after its start shows only the start time ("10:30am · every Sunday"), and the phone won't let you choose an end time before the start. In the admin panel, date and time pickers open beside their field and stay on screen, in side panels and pop-up sheets too, and a To date opens on the From date's month.
Where: The app's Calendar (Month) and event pages; the admin panel's Calendar → Breaks → New break, and a regular event's How it runs → Repeats.
Try: Add a break of a month or more and check every date in it has the open ring on the phone's Month view. In the admin panel, open New break, pick a From date, then open To: it should open on that month, fully on screen.
