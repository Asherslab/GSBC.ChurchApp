A walk now has the whole screen, and an outreach day's routes show each route's number, length, letterboxes and who's on it.

## For staff
Changed: The planner's route labels never sit on top of each other: each finds a spot on its own route, and the map opens a little closer at 1024 wide. Printed route maps name the streets around the route as well as the ones on it, so a route can be walked from paper. On Coverage, a street you pick is outlined in ink instead of navy, so it stands out from streets done recently in light and dark. A route's time is the same everywhere (planner, printed sheet, the phone's day list and route page): walking it and delivering to its letterboxes, without the walk from the meeting place, which some places added before. Walk-throughs print dashed, as the key says. Tables in the admin panel (Prayer's From, for one) only drop a column when it really doesn't fit.
Where: Outreach → Days → a day → Plan the routes, and Print the maps; Outreach → Coverage; Prayer. On the phone, Outreach → an outreach day, and a walk.
Try: Open a planned day at a narrow window and zoom out: no number covers another. Print the maps and check the side streets are named. Pick a street on Coverage in light and dark. Start a walk on the phone and check the tab bar is gone and ‹ Outreach takes you back with the walk still on.
