The Outreach map no longer stalls while it counts the streets in view.

## For staff
Changed: With Coverage open, panning and zooming the Outreach map no longer freezes for a moment each time the map stops. The streets done and letterboxes figures update as soon as the map settles, and a new part of the map fills in its coverage without holding up the map. The figures are the same as before.
Where: Admin panel → Outreach → Map → Coverage.
Try: Zoom out until tens of thousands of streets are in view, then drag the map across to another area. It should keep moving smoothly, show "Counting…" briefly while a new area loads, then show the figures.
