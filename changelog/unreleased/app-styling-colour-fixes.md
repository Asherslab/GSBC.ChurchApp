The phone preview in App styling shows every theme properly in light and dark.

## For staff
Changed: With the 2026 theme, the phone preview's light mode showed dark green with hard-to-read titles when your computer was in dark mode; it now shows the theme's real light colours. Dragging or resizing a Home tile on the phone while the Colours panel is open no longer shows "can't be placed in Colours": it opens the Home screen part and moves the tile.
Where: Admin panel → App styling.
Try: Open a draft, choose the 2026 theme in Colours, and preview in light mode. Then drag a tile on the phone while Colours is open.
