Rosters learn three things leaders asked for: people still training, a clearer swap setting, and which calendars a roster draws from.

## For staff
Changed: On a position that needs people trained for it, someone not trained yet goes on as training: they don't fill the place, and they're flagged "Training: needs someone trained on with them" until someone trained is on the same date and position (in any order). Leaders hear if a trainee is left with nobody trained. Swaps are one choice, *When someone asks the team*: nobody, anyone can take it, or anyone can offer and a leader says yes; *Ask someone on the team yourself* is its own switch, and the person asking never chooses whether a leader confirms (a roster that had both now has a leader confirming). *Calendars it rosters from* lets a ministry choose calendars; Add a date starts with their events and Every calendar is still there. Adding someone to a team reaches the roster at once, not after up to 10 minutes. Position names keep their capitals in flags and notifications.
Where: A ministry's Roster tab → How the roster works, Positions, Add a date.
Try: Tick a position "needs people trained for it", put on someone untrained and see the flag, then put on someone trained. Change the swap setting and ask for a swap. Choose a calendar in How the roster works and open Add a date. Add someone to the team and check they can be put on straight away.
