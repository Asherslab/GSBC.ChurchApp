Where an event, a group or an outreach day meets shows its address, a note and a small map. Tap it for directions in Apple Maps or Google Maps.

## For staff
Changed: Anywhere you say where something happens, you now choose a place: At church, or Somewhere else. For somewhere else, search for the address (any address in Australia, a street, a suburb or a named building), or tap the map to put the pin on the exact spot. Then add a name and a note, such as where to park. A Life Group that meets in a home can be marked as a home: only the group's own people see the street address and the map, and everyone else sees "A home in" and the suburb. When and where a ministry meets are now two fields. An outreach day's meeting place is now the pin on its map, so the planner no longer has its own meeting-point pin. Places typed before this stay as they were until you change them.
Where: Admin panel → Outreach → Days → a day → Where people meet. An event's Place, in the calendar on the phone or in the admin panel. A ministry's Settings → Where it meets, on the phone or in the admin panel.
Try: Open next Saturday's outreach day, press Change under Where people meet, choose Somewhere else, search for a shopping centre car park, tap the map to move the pin to the right corner, add "Park at the far end", and press Use this place, then Save. On the phone, open the day and tap the place: check the map, then Directions. Mark a Life Group's place as a home, then look at it on a phone signed in as someone who isn't in the group.
