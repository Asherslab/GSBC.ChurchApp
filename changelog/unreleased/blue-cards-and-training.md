If you serve with children or young people, your Account shows your blue card and Safe Ministry training as the church office has them, and when they're due.

## For staff
Changed: Blue cards (number, status, expiry, when it was checked) and Safe Ministry training (done, due again; three years by default) are recorded by hand on a person's new Safeguarding tab, and shown to them on their Account page. Nothing is ever blocked: a card or training that has run out or is due within 60 days is flagged. A new Safeguarding page lists everyone flagged, soonest first, with young people no parent has linked; anything run out also shows in Home's Needs you. New roles Safeguarding Viewer and Safeguarding Manager, under People.
Where: People → a person → Safeguarding and Family; People → Safeguarding; Account.
Try: Give yourself Safeguarding Manager, record a blue card that expires next month on someone, then open Safeguarding and their Account page.
