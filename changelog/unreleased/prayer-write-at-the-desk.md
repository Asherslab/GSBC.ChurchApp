Prayer wall staff can write a prayer request in the admin panel, under their name, someone else's, no name or the church's.

## For staff
Changed: Prayer has a Write a request button. A request written there goes straight onto the prayer wall (or only to the pastoral team), from your name, someone else's (for someone who rang or emailed), no name or the church's ("GSBC" to start). Any prayer wall staff can change, take down or delete one from the church or for someone else. On the phone, prayer wall staff can also choose the church's name when they ask for prayer. The church's name is under Prayer → Settings.
Where: Admin panel → Prayer → Write a request (top right); Prayer → Settings for the church's name; Prayer → Ask for prayer on the phone.
Try: Write a request from the church, check the card beside it, put it on the wall and find it on the phone's prayer wall. Then change the church's name under Settings and see the wall follow.
