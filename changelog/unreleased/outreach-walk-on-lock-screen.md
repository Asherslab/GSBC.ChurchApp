On an iPhone, an outreach walk now shows on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island: the street, which side, how many houses to go and the next turn. You can mark No junk mail, count a conversation, pause or finish without opening the app, and the walk keeps marking streets with the phone in your pocket.

## For staff
Changed: While a walk is on, a Live Activity shows it on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. Its buttons (No junk mail, or At a door when doorknocking; Spoke; Pause; Finish) do the same as the walking screen's, and each mark is sent like any other. The phone buzzes when you reach a turn. Finish opens the app on the end of the walk so you can check what was covered. The walk now keeps going when you leave the walking screen, and a walk left on for more than an hour is paused when you next open the app.
Where: In the app, Outreach → Plan my walk → Start the walk, then lock the phone. iPhone only, iOS 17 or later; the first time, iOS asks whether to allow Live Activities.
Try: Start a short walk, lock the phone and walk. Tap Spoke on the Lock Screen and check the line says it was counted. Tap No junk mail beside a letterbox, then Undo. Pause and carry on, then Finish and check the app opens on the end of the walk.
