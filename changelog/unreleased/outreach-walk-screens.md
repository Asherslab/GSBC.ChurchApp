Your dot on the walk's map now shows which way you're facing, the streets show as soon as you open Your walk, and you choose what you're carrying right there.

## For staff
Changed: A soft orange fan from your dot shows which way you're facing (from the compass, or the way you're walking); it widens when the compass isn't sure. The map shows the streets in view at once, even while the phone is still downloading the area's map. What you're carrying is now chosen on Your walk and the walk redraws straight away; the choice stays when you close it. The small Around you map has gone from the Outreach page, and the walking screen's buttons keep the same margin as the rest of the page.
Where: In the app, Outreach → Plan my walk (Your walk), then Start the walk.
Try: Open Outreach and tap Plan my walk. Tap the campaign row under Change the walk, turn a campaign on, close the sheet and open it again: it stays. Start the walk, turn on the spot and watch the fan follow you.
