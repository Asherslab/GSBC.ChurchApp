Every page now has an icon of its own, so Ministries, People, Chats and Feedback no longer look alike, and What's new has a gift.

## For staff
Changed: Ministries, People, Chats, Feedback, App health, Insights, Safe Ministry Check, Announcements, the weekly digest, App styling, Places in the app, What's new, Help improve the app and Notification settings each have an icon no other page uses. Notification settings shows a cog, as every "settings of this" does.
Where: the admin panel's sidebar, and More and Places in the app on the phone.
Try: Open the sidebar's People area and check Ministries, People, Chats and Safeguarding each look different; open More on the phone.
