Recordings and pictures the app keeps for sermons can now be deleted from Media once nothing uses them, so deleting sermons frees their storage.

## For staff
Changed: after you delete a sermon, its recording stays in Media's Sermon audio folder marked Not used, and you can now delete it there. Pictures in Sermon artwork that no sermon, series or speaker uses can be deleted too. Files still in use can't be.
Where: Admin panel → Media → Made by the app → Sermon audio (or Sermon artwork), filter Not used.
Try: Hide and delete a test sermon, open Sermon audio, filter Not used, and delete its recording.
