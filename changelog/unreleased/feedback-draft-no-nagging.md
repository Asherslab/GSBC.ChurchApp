You can close Send feedback, move between pages to gather screenshots, and send it later without being asked to leave without saving each time.

## For staff
Changed: A feedback draft waiting to be sent no longer brings up "Leave without saving?" every time you open another page. It still asks if you close the browser tab.
Where: Send feedback, at the bottom of the admin panel's sidebar.
Try: Write a few words in Send feedback, close it, open two other pages, then open Send feedback again and send it.
