Ministries, Life Groups and your account are kept on your phone, so they open at once, even with no signal.

## For staff
Changed: Every ministry and Life Group you can see is synced to your phone in the background, so a page opens at once and never shows "Can't load this ministry" while you're offline. Joining, posting, adding files, changing settings and people, Sunday changes, sermon follows and calendar choices go through the phone's outbox: the ministry page shows "1 change waiting to send", or "Couldn't send" with Try again. Team-only posts only reach the phones of people who may see them. Your account's name, email, passkeys and devices show offline but can only be changed online.
Where: The Ministries and Life Groups pages in the app, a ministry's page, and Account → Security.
Try: Open a ministry, turn on flight mode, open another ministry and a Life Group, post something, then turn flight mode off and watch it send.
