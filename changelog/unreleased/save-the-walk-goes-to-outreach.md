When you save a walk, the app goes straight back to the Outreach page and says it's saved, with how far you walked and how many letterboxes.

## For staff
Changed: Save the walk now returns to the Outreach page you planned from, with a "Your walk is saved" box (distance, letterboxes and a link to My outreach) until you leave the page. Back from there goes where you were before the walk, never to the walk's pages or "No walk on". At the end of a walk, two stretches of one side of the same street now say which end they are ("north side, east end"), and a stretch you tick because the app missed it now counts its letterboxes.
Where: App → Outreach → Plan my walk → Start the walk → Finish → Save the walk.
Try: Walk a short loop, tap Finish, tick one street the app missed, and save: Outreach should say the walk is saved with that street's letterboxes, and Back should take you to where you opened Outreach from.
