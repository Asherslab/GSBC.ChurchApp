Scrolling a page with a Back button no longer runs its big title underneath Back.

## For staff
Changed: As a page scrolls, its big title used to slide up under a see-through top bar, so for a moment Back sat on top of it (Notifications showed it most). The bar now turns solid as soon as the title reaches it, the title slides under it, and the small title appears in the bar once the big one has gone.
Where: Any page with a big title and Back: Notifications, Browse calendars, a sermon series, Account.
Try: Open Notifications and scroll down slowly.
