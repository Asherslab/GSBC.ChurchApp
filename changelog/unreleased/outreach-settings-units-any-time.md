Outreach walks can be planned and started at any time of day.

## For staff
Changed: Number fields show their unit inside the box: 10%, 8 seconds, 4 km/h, 500 metres, 30 days. Outreach no longer has walking hours: the *Daylight and pairs* settings are now *Doorknocking* (in pairs or not), and the phone no longer warns about or blocks walks after dark. *What counts as a walk* now says why it's there: so a walk left running in a car or on a bus doesn't mark streets done. *Bundle size* is now the size new campaigns start with.
Where: Admin panel → Outreach → Settings; also a route's size on Days and Areas, a new map area's distance, a Home tile's width and height, and App review's sign-in code.
Try: Open Outreach → Settings and change *Spare tracts*; the % stays beside the number. On the phone, plan a walk in the evening: it can start.
