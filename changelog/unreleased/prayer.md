Ask the pastoral team to pray for you, even with no signal. It stays private unless you share it on the prayer wall for the church to pray too.

## For staff
Changed: Anyone can send a request from the app, signed in or not; it goes only to the pastoral team unless they ask for the prayer wall too, and then nothing shows until someone chooses the words, whether a name shows (first name and last initial, like "Sam S.") and for how long. The person sees their request's steps (New, Followed up, Answered), can say it was answered, take it off the wall or delete it, and hears when people pray for it. The prayer wall team approve requests on their phone under Prayer → To approve, even offline; the pastoral team also mark requests followed up or answered, keep notes, and call or email. The full inbox, with filters and history, is in the admin panel. Requests are deleted a year after their last change. Two roles: Pastoral team (everything) and Prayer wall (only requests that ask for the wall).
Where: More → Prayer in the app (To approve, for the prayer team); This week → Prayer in the admin panel.
Try: With the Pastoral team role, ask for prayer with the wall on another phone, then put it on the wall from yours, tap Pray, and add a note.
