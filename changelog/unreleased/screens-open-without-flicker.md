Moving between screens no longer flickers: a screen you've opened before appears straight away, and nothing fades through grey on the way.

## For staff
Changed: Every page used to fade in from 40%, which let the navy behind the page show through as a grey veil, and a page often drew a grey loading placeholder first and then faded in a second time. Pages now appear at once, a screen you've already opened draws what it showed last time while it checks for anything newer, and a loading placeholder only shows when loading takes long enough to notice. It's the whole app: the calendar, Sermons, Home, Notifications.
Where: Anywhere in the app.
Try: On This week, open an event and go back, tap + Browse and go back, and switch to Month and back. Then tap the sermon on Home and go back.
