A person's page in the admin panel opens on Profile: their name, email, birth date and age, and their account, each in its own card.

## For staff
Changed: The Profile tab shows their name, email, birth date and age (change it there), their account, passkey and when they joined. The heading keeps just their name, with a pill when the account needs you (turned off, not invited yet, or an invitation that expired). The Family tab is only about their family. On Account, in the app and the admin panel, your birth date sits with your name, and Your family is one group with Add a child inside it.
Where: People & access → People → a person. Account in the app.
Try: Open someone in People, look at Profile and change their birth date; check the Family and Safeguarding tabs still read it.
