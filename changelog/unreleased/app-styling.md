Drafts is now App styling: it opens on the app as everyone sees it today, and you change something by clicking it.

## For staff
Changed: The Drafts page is now App styling. It opens on a phone showing the app as everyone sees it, a sentence saying what that is, and what's coming up in the months ahead (this replaces Church year). Click a tile, a tab, or Colours, Home screen or Tab bar, and you're changing it in your draft: your open one if you have one, a new one named after what you're changing if you don't, or the one you pick if you have several. A draft started this way that you leave without changing anything goes away by itself. Your drafts, the ones on for an event or publishing on a date, and what was published recently are under Drafts at the top right; Published recently stays folded until you open it. Colours now reads as the theme the app uses all year (the church's brand, the 2026 theme, or one made here), and the page keeps the same layout whichever part you're changing.
Where: The admin panel, App styling (it was Drafts).
Try: Open App styling and click a tile on the phone. Change its words, watch the phone follow, then choose Back to what everyone sees. Click something under Coming up to see the app on that day. Open Drafts at the top right and unfold Published recently.
