# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

Proposed 2026-10-26, not yet confirmed by staff: one GSBC design on iOS and Android, packaged with
Capacitor, with native behaviours (haptics, sheets, swipe-back, system share and browser) rather than
a separate native look per OS.

## Users

Members and visitors of Good Shepherd Baptist Church, Albany Creek (Brisbane). Which moments matter
most (in the pew on Sunday, midweek catch-up, newcomers, older members) is **undecided**; the owner
wants to choose from design options and put them to church staff and the pastor.

## Product Purpose

Replace the church's Tithe.ly app, whose design and features were shaped by Tithe.ly's limits, with an
app that feels like this church: this week's service and bulletin, sermons to watch or listen to,
events, prayer requests, giving and contact, with content staff can change without a store release.
The core ideas of the Tithe.ly app stay; their form is open.

## Positioning

The app is the church's own: its bulletin, its sermons, its people, its rhythm of Sundays and
Wednesdays, rather than a generic church template. Balance across features is wanted; no single area
is the centre of gravity (owner, 2026-10-26).

## Operating Context

- Weekly rhythm: Sunday 9:00 LIFE Groups, 10:30 and 6:00 worship (both streamed on YouTube),
  Wednesday 7:00 Bible study and prayer, Friday prayer meetings, Impact Kids and Impact Youth.
- Each Sunday has a printed and digital bulletin ("What's Next"): welcome letter, sermon outlines with
  Bible passages for AM and PM, this week, what's next, how to give, a notes page.
- Sermons are published to baptist.com.au and its podcast RSS feed (audio, artwork, series, YouTube).
- A yearly theme (2026: "Almighty God", Job 33:4) runs through the bulletin and the website.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Giving stays on Tithe.ly, opened in the browser (SPEC.md §7).
- No accounts needed to read anything. Prayer requests are private to the pastoral team by default.
- Events are being redone; an event is a calendar entry with an image, title and description.
- Undecided: tab structure and home layout, whether the prayer wall ships in v1, light or dark default,
  brand theme versus yearly theme.

## Brand Commitments

- GSBC Logo Pack 2022: the mark is a "G" that opens into a hill. Use only the supplied files; never
  redraw it. Minimum on-screen size 85px wide.
- 2022 brand guide palette: navy #2A3D5E, beige #D5C9B7, aqua #009C8F, orange #E7AE74, plus black,
  white and grey. Typeface Montserrat (Bold and Regular).
- Yearly themes are separate from the brand: 2026 uses the website's greens (#4A7355, sage #84A87D,
  cream) with DM Serif Text headings. Which one the app uses is for staff to decide.

- The 25 Oct 2026 bulletin as screens in the owner's recording of the Tithe.ly app.

## Product Principles

- This week first, everything one tap away.
- Readable by everyone in the congregation, in a dim auditorium and in daylight.
- Content is the church's own; never fake people, events or claims.
- Staff change content without a release; design changes ship through the stores.
